# Features shelf: Rimeholt

Map features for the Rimeholt palette, made alone (swarm-lab features job, platform 0005) and kept for maps to be assembled from (docs/feature-library.md). Each folder: the candidate's patch (its feature module, any new sculpts and set pieces), its renders and both critics' scores. Not in arpg-game until a map uses one.

Across the run (second critic): Judged from the renders alone. The rework helped most of the set: A, C, D, E, F, G, H and J improved, while B and I held level. Three problems run across the board. First, the 'in along the road' camera comes back empty or blocked by a wall for most features (A, D, G, I, and seed 3 of nearly all), so that column carries no evidence. Second, the seeds vary too little: D, G, H and I are one layout repeated. Third, most features sit in a square frame that shows from the game camera. J, H and D bring silhouettes or light this game hasn't got and are the safest to keep. A and C are good-looking set dressing once their shapes loosen. F is usable as a backdrop wall. B, E and G are weaker, and I should be left off until its heaps and doors read.

## Second run (rimeholt-features-2, 2026-10-30)

Twelve builders on the same brief, now with the varied-seeds platform (`TestRng.mixed`, the variety spec). Three critic passes, with two reworks between them; each critic was fresh and saw only renders. 8 of 12 survived. The final critic shelved four:

| Feature | Idea | Pass 1 → 2 → 3 | Why kept (final critic) |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RimeholtIceboundLongship](RimeholtIceboundLongship/) | A hill-folk burial longship frozen at a tilt into a lake that never thaws, bow up and whole, broken in two, or sunk to its ribs with the hull left under the ice, the ship standing in a wide pale ice collar on a clean painted lake with a tight cold line of light along its keel beneath. | 6.93 → 7.08 → 6.78 | A complete, instantly readable landmark with a working keel glow and varied wreck states; any frozen lake map can take it. |
| [RimeholtAuroraWell](RimeholtAuroraWell-2/) | A deep, quiet ice shaft with a near-black broken collar, its lit floor the only light in the dark and a pale forked spire standing to one side so the game camera looks past it down the well; the road runs around, across, or down into the shaft and out the far side, and a column of aurora climbs from the floor. | 6.83 → 6.73 → 6.45 | The aurora shaft is the best vertical effect and the well shapes vary enough to anchor a field's centre. |
| [RimeholtCrevasseBridge](RimeholtCrevasseBridge-2/) | A deep crevasse torn across Rimeholt's night snow, one long drop from the snow to a single dark floor where a pale seam of lit ice runs in a line of cold light, crossed by the hill-folk's lashed-timber plank bridge, short or long over the cut it finds, with snowdrifts banked at the rim beside it. | 6.9 → 6.23 → 5.58 | A crossing feature with real cut-shape variety and a readable bridge that solves 'how the road gets over' in many maps. |
| [RimeholtYardangField](RimeholtYardangField/) | A wind-carved yardang field of only one to three enormous near-black granite fins, each a high blunt prow falling down a long low tail, every crest carrying one bright unbroken line of packed snow and streaming long bright spindrift in one direction, with a wind-cut arch on the highest crest and the road weaving between their tails. | 5.8 → 4.33 → 5.63 | The seeds vary most of all and the dark prow with its arch and streaming spindrift is a distinct silhouette worth reusing. |

Two names repeat from the first run (`RimeholtAuroraWell`, `RimeholtCrevasseBridge`): their folders end in `-2`. They are different modules under the same name, so a map can use only one of each pair until one is renamed.

Scores from different runs come from different critics and aren't comparable. Within this run the critics didn't agree well between passes: the geyser basin went 7.1 → 6.4 → 4.3, and the yardang field went 5.8 → 4.3 → 5.6. Read the notes in `about.json`, not only the totals.

The main gap, raised on their own by five builders: the offline shots don't draw set pieces. A feature's effect (aurora, steam, wisps, spindrift) shows only in its separate set-piece panel, never in its photographs.
