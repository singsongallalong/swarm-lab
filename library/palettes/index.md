# Palettes shelf

Area palettes made ahead of the areas (swarm-lab palettes job). Each folder: the palette module (`data/palettes/<Name>.luau`, applies on platform 0004), its swatches, the moor repainted in it, a ruin, and its critic scores. Not in arpg-game until an area uses it (docs/feature-library.md).
