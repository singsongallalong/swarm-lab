# The Classic brief's directions: six takes on one black-and-crimson lolita outfit for the
# blocky classic body. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Faithful: stay close to the described outfit (crimson-laced black bodice, waist bow, tiered crimson-cream-black skirt, bell sleeves, a lace headband with a side bow), as the cleanest, most iconic version you can build.
Catalog minimal: the fewest, boldest parts that still say gothic lolita, like a 2008 catalog item: one big A-line skirt block with a single lace tier, one great bow, wide bell sleeves, a bonnet; spend the saved parts nowhere.
Clockwork: the tiny clock grows into a motif: a clock-face bow centre at the waist, a key or a pocket watch hanging at the hip, gear-toothed lace on the tiers; black, crimson and dull brass.
Inverted: a crimson dress with black lace tiers, black bows and black gloves; the same silhouette with the colours swapped, so crimson leads from the high camera.
Long and solemn: the skirt falls in tiers to the ankles, the bell sleeves hang to the fingertips, and the headdress carries a short black veil behind; mourning more than cute.
Stitched doll: a toy dress patched together, its tiers in slightly mismatched panels with big cross-stitch seams and a heart-shaped patch, the bow lopsided; cute and a little eerie.
