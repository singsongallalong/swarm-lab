# Ashveil: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Ember widow: a charcoal gown whose hem is burnt into ragged tongues with violet glowing edges, a veil of ash grey.
Aurora priestess: a pale ash gown with a tall halo of violet light-shapes rising behind the head, bone trims.
Giant's bone: a bodice and bell skirt ribbed with bleached giant-bone strips, violet seams between them.
Cinder lolita: a bell skirt of layered charcoal ruffles edged violet, a bow, a small burnt bonnet.
Gatekeeper: a high-collared armoured gown, a violet gem at the throat and violet cracks down the skirt.
Smoke dancer: drifting tiered panels like smoke, darker at the top and paler at the hem, violet ember spots.
