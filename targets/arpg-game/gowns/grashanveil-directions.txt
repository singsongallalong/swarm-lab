# Grashanveil: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Sun priestess: a pleated pale gown, a gold disc headdress like a sun behind the head, a terracotta sash.
Statue dress: a gown with folds like carved stone drapery, a stone diadem, gold arm bands.
Plains dancer: a wrap skirt flaring in terracotta and gold tiers, a short bodice, beaded hair falls.
Oracle: a hooded pale robe-gown with a gold eye sigil, long hanging gold chains at the hem.
Grashan queen: a high stepped crown like a ziggurat, a stiff bell skirt with gold bands.
Overgrown: an ancient ceremonial gown with ivy and plains flowers grown through it, moss at the hem.
