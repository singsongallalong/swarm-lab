# Feastqueen: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Feast queen: a towering gold crown, a wine-red bell gown with gold panels, a long train, rings of candle-amber gems.
Golden hostess: an ivory gown with gold bands and a gold collar, a cup-bearer's sash, amber lights at the waist.
Dream fading: a gold gown whose hem darkens into shade-black smoke tiers, a halo crown.
Wine dancer: tiers of wine-red ruffles flaring wide, a gold bodice, gold veils from the crown.
Shade bride: a pale ghostly gown with gold jewellery only, candle-amber eyes in a veil.
Sun court: a sun-disc collar framing the head, a gold-and-ivory pleated bell, gold sleeves.
