# Mossveil: six takes for the Drowned Marches wardrobe pilot. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Gothic lolita of the moss: a bell skirt of layered moss-green ruffles over a pale bone petticoat, a black bodice with a great bow, a bonnet with fungus caps.
Fungal ballgown: a full skirt whose tiers are shaped like the gills of a mushroom cap, a cap-shaped wide hat; bone, mushroom tan and black.
Fern lady: long fern-frond panels falling from the waist, a high leafy collar, a fern circlet; deep green and black.
Ruin priestess: a pale-stone-grey robe-gown with carved-ring trims and moss creeping up the hem; a tall stone-like headdress.
Lichen courtesan: corseted, off-shoulder sleeves, a draped lichen-yellow overskirt drawn to one hip; risky and elegant.
Bog orchid: petal-shaped skirt panels like a dark orchid, black and moss with one sickly pale-yellow accent.
