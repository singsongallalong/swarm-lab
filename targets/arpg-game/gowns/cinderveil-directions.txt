# Cinderveil: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Basalt gown: a black columnar skirt like basalt columns, red seams between the columns, an obsidian crown.
Vent priestess: a grey hooded gown with sulphur-yellow trims, veils like vent smoke.
Obsidian court: a glossy black fitted bodice, a wide black bell skirt with a single red hem line.
Magma lolita: a black bell skirt of ruffles with red-lit edges, a bow, a tiny horned bonnet.
Statue devotee: a pale grey statue-drapery gown cracked with red, a stone circlet.
Cave dancer: short layered skirts over leggings, red ribbons, a crown of black crystal.
