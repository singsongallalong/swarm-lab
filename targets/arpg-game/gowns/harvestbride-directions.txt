# Harvestbride: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Harvest festival: a full gathered skirt in wheat gold with a white apron, a laced dark bodice, a crown of wheat and red poppies.
Milkmaid: a short-sleeved blouse under a green corset bodice, a tiered skirt, a wide straw hat with a ribbon.
Village bride: a simple cream gown with a red sash, a flower wreath and a short veil, embroidered hem.
Shepherdess: a quilted skirt hitched up over a petticoat, a hooded shawl, a crook-shaped hair ornament.
Orchard keeper: an apple-red dress with a dark apron full of pockets, a kerchief, a basket shape at the hip.
Fort lady: the tower lord's daughter, a fitted walnut-and-gold gown with a heraldic tabard panel and a small circlet.
