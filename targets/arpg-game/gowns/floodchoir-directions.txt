# Floodchoir: six takes for the Drowned Marches wardrobe pilot. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Chorister: a long black robe-gown with a wide white ruff collar and a red under-cassock showing at the hem; a small cap.
Drowned nun: a habit cut into an elegant gown, a long wimple and veil, the hem dark and wet, a lantern at the belt.
Stained-glass lady: a black gown with panels of colour-blocked crimson, violet and amber like a church window, leaded black lines between.
Candle widow: deep mourning black with a veiled hat ringed by short candles (no flames: amber wax colour only), lace gloves.
Gothic lolita of the parish: bell skirt, bow, ruffles and a bonnet in black and church crimson, with a small cross-shaped clasp.
Bell-ringer: a cloak-gown shaped like a bell, a hood, rope trims, tarnished bronze bell charms at the hem.
