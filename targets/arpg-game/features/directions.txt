# Twelve feature ideas for Rimeholt, each pushing an effect, a geometry or a detail.
A frozen geyser field: vents in the ice that breathe columns of frost-steam (your own set piece: rising, spreading motes), ringed by ice cones built up in rings around each vent.
An ice-bound longship: a hill-folk's burial ship frozen into the lake at a tilt, its mast and ribs standing out of the ice (your own sculpts), the under-ice glow showing its hull beneath the surface.
The hanging glacier: a tongue of glacier ice pouring over a granite ledge, frozen in mid-flow as a wall of stepped seracs (per-cell lifts), meltwater icicles and a cave mouth under it.
A ring of frost-giants: four colossal kneeling figures of snow-crusted stone round a frozen pool (your own sculpts, many times a man's height), facing inward, a slow snowfall falling only inside their ring.
The aurora well: a deep round shaft in the ice with stepped sides, a column of pale aurora light rising out of it into the sky (your own set piece), runestones round its lip.
A crevasse bridge: a deep crack across the ice, its walls stepping down into blue dark, crossed by a bridge of ice and lashed timber (your own sculpts), drifting snow pouring over its edge.
The barrow-lights: a cluster of snow barrows whose doors leak a cold blue light, wisps rising from the doorways (your own set piece), cairns and grave-markers between them.
Pressure ridges with trapped things: long heaved slabs of ice (lifted cells) and, frozen inside the clearest ones, shapes: a horse, a shield, a hand (your own sculpts, under a translucent sheet).
An ice-fall cathedral: tall columns of ice in two rows (lifted cells) making a nave toward a frozen altar of runestones, frost motes drifting in shafts between the columns (your own set piece).
The steaming spring: one unfrozen pool of black water in all the ice, steam rising and curling off it (your own set piece), a ring of snow melted back round it to the dark earth, offerings on stones.
A wind-carved yardang field: long streamlined ridges of snow and rock all leaning one way (per-cell lifts), snow streaming off their crests in the wind (your own set piece), the road weaving between them.
The frozen battle: a field where a war froze mid-charge: rows of spears, shields and fallen standards standing out of the snow (your own sculpts), a cold mist creeping between them at ankle height.
