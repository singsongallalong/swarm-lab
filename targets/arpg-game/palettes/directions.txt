# Twelve places nobody has built yet, one per generator.
A black glass coast: a shore of obsidian shingle and wet black sand under sea fog, salt-white rime on the rocks, the accent a cold sea-green of drowned lanterns.
A plague city's outskirts: trodden yellow-grey mud, limewashed walls gone grey, soot, the accent a sickly bile yellow-green on rags and doors marked by the plague-wardens.
A fungal deepwood: a forest floor of dark loam and rot, bark near black, pale caps glowing faintly, the accent a muted spore violet.
A copper-mine terrace: stepped spoil heaps of rust-brown and slag grey, old timber props, the accent verdigris green on every pipe, bell and dome.
A crow-haunted birch wood in first snow: pale bark against dark ground, thin snow, the accent a faded oxblood of berries and the crows' ribbons.
A bone desert: a dead giant's plain of pale dust and bleached rib-stone under a cold moon, wind-cut grey rock, the accent a dry dusk-rose.
A drowned rice terrace under fog: stepped flooded paddies of dark water and silt, mossy dykes, the accent a faded indigo of the farmers' prayer flags.
An iron foundry waste: cinder ground, rust and black iron, cold furnaces, the accent a dull ember orange held inside the iron (muted, under the bible's cap).
A peat bog of standing stones: black peat and wet brown moss, grey stones, still pools, the accent a cold will-o'-wisp blue.
A sunken cathedral marsh: green-black water, reed beds, pale grey limestone tracery breaking the surface, the accent a dim stained-glass amber.
A red-clay badlands at night: eroded mesas of rust clay gone dark under the moon, dry wash beds, the accent a faded turquoise of old shrine tiles.
A frost-killed vineyard: frozen terraces of dark earth, dead vines, a manor's broken walls, the accent a deep muted wine-purple.
