# The Rimelake brief's directions: six forms of map round one frozen lake.
The round lake: a great round lake fills the middle, the walkable ground a broad shore ring round it, the road coming down from the hills on one side, crossing the ice and leaving on the other.
The long fjord: a long narrow lake between two ridges of high ground, the road running along one shore and crossing the ice at a narrows by the runestones.
The lake of islands: the ice is strewn with small rocky islands (outcrops), the road hopping across the ice from island to island to the runestone island at the centre.
The high tarns: a chain of three frozen tarns stepping down the highland, each with its barrows, the road winding past each to the great lake and its ring at the bottom.
The barrow shore: the lake on one side of the map, the far side a field of barrows on rising ground, the road running between the dead and the ice to the ring where they meet.
The frozen bay: the map is a horseshoe of high snowy ground round a bay of ice opening off the map's edge into mist (a drop rim), the ring on a headland pointing out over the bay.
