The hub of glades: a round clearing at the centre with the well, and four or five glades round it like petals, each joined to the hub by a short neck; the road runs in through one glade and out through another.
The pale avenue: a long straight processional road between ranks of standing ruins leading to the well, then the wood closes in and the way out winds away through thick trees.
The two hollows: the glade is two great hollows joined by a single pass, the barrows in the first and the well in the second, so the player crosses the dead to reach the light.
