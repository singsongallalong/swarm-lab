# Six places round one frozen lake, each pushing a different freedom of the loosened job.
The shattered lake: the ice has heaved into pressure ridges, slabs of blue-white ice 6 to 20 studs tall (your own wall cell kind) standing in long cracked lines across the lake; the road threads the gaps between them, and the under-ice glow breathes along the cracks.
The black water: the lake is half thawed, wide pools of black open water (a sunk, dark, unwalkable cell kind) breaking a sheet of pale ice into floes and causeways; the road is a narrow ice causeway over the dark, snow drifting across it, the runestones on the last floe.
The frozen falls: the highland steps down in great shelves (heights, bluffs, ramps), a river frozen mid-fall over each step in tall columns of ice, the road switchbacking down beside the falls to the lake at the bottom under an aurora.
The drowned forest: an old pine forest stands frozen into the lake, black trunks rising out of the ice in hundreds (your own cell kind with a scatter of snapped pines), the road winding through the dead trees to a clearing where the ring stands on the ice.
The crater lake: the lake lies in a vast round crater far below a rim of high ground; the road comes in high on the rim, spirals down the crater's terraced wall, and crosses the ice to a spire of ice at the centre, the aurora over it all.
The ice cathedral: in the lake's middle a forest of ice spires up to 60 studs tall rises from the ice in rings and aisles (your own wall cell kind), a nave of open ice running through them to the runestones at its heart, wisps hanging between the spires.
