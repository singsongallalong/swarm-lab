Pale cliff-dwelling: sandstone walls with thick base courses and battered (sloped-out) corner piers, flat roofs with crenels, bronze door bands.
Basalt forge-house: dark basalt walls banded in iron, chimney-like corner posts, thin red Neon cracks and a glowing slit over doors; ruins glow in the breaks.
Monumental: oversized blocks in alternating pale and dark courses, huge lintels, stepped flat roofs like a ziggurat edge; ruins are fallen giant blocks.
