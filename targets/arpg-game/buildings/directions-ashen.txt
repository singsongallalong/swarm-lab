Half-timbered: a dense black frame of posts, rails and braces with grey plaster infill; ruins keep the charred frame and lose the infill.
Fieldstone foot, timber above: a low dark stone plinth course with sooted plank walls on it; ruins are the stone foot with charred plank stumps and embers.
