Temple-house: thick plinth and cornice courses, square stone pilasters as posts, flat stone roofs with a parapet lip; ivy climbs in long bands.
Shrine-dwelling of the Grashan: stepped stone walls narrowing upward, heavy lintels over doors, steep low stone-slab roofs; roots and leaf mats heaped at the foot.
Swallowed: the stone barely shows under moss carpets, hanging creeper curtains over openings and violet-teal growth; ruins are mounds of green on stone stubs.
