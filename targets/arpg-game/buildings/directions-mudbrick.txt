Adobe: smooth plastered walls, rows of round timber beam ends under the roof line, a parapet lip, poles jutting from the walls; sand heaps on ruins.
Caravanserai: heavy brick walls with a darker brick base course and corner towers as posts, arched doors framed in plaster, cloth awnings over windows.
Plain-folk: low mud-brick walls under thatched pitched roofs, timber lintels, faded indigo cloth at doors; ruins slump into mounds.
