Woven: wattle walls shown as horizontal weave bands and upright stakes, daub patches over them; ruins are bare stake-and-weave frames.
Drowned parish: daub over a mossy stone foot, tall reed-thatch roofs with a heavy ridge roll, green moss and weed bands at the waterline.
