# Coreforged: six takes for wardrobe wave 2. Lines starting with # are ignored.
Core king: a black crowned helm with a molten crack crown, plate split by glowing seams, a heavy cape.
Magma heart: a chest plate with a glowing core at its centre and cracks running outward over every piece.
Godforged: ornate gold-and-black plate with ember-gold trims, horns, a face plate with burning eyes.
Cooling flow: plate shaped like lava frozen mid-flow, glowing only in the deepest cracks.
Vent lord: a tall helm with vent chimneys on the pauldrons, molten seams down the arms.
Ember knight: sleek black plate, a long gold-lined cape, one great glowing gem on the brow.
