# The Blightfen brief's three directions, one per generator.
The drowned farmsteads: fields gone to bog, farmhouse stumps, a well and a haystack rotting in the water, dead orchards in rows; the effect is a low yellow-green miasma drifting off the pools.
The plague pit: a great pit of the dead at the heart (a hollow ring of headstones and crypts), a chapel above it, gibbets on the road in; the effect is foul light bubbling up out of the pit.
The dead orchard: rows of dead, twisted trees across sodden ground, a ruined manor's wall, scattered graves; the effect is sickly wisps hanging low between the rows.
