# The Rimeholt brief's three directions, one per generator.
The barrow field: low mounds and crypts of the hill-kings across a white moor, runestones marking each; the effect is snow drifting low across the field.
The frozen lake: the lake fills the heart of the area (pools of ice), its shore ringed with runestones and dead pines; the effect is a cold blue glow pulsing slowly under the ice.
The pine wood: dense black pines heavy with rime, glades opening on a waystone circle and a hill-fort's broken tower; the effect is a thin ice-blue mist threading between the trunks.
