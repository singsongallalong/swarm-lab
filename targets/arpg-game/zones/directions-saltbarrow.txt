# The Saltbarrow brief's three directions, one per generator.
The giant's ribcage: one colossal skeleton dominates, the tombs of the kings set between its ribs, the salt flat empty around it; the effect is dust streaming low across the flats.
The tomb road: a processional way of broken columns and arches across the salt to a royal tomb (a crypt), with lesser graves along it; the effect is an ochre glow breathing out of the tombs.
The cracked basin: the dry sea bed broken into plates of mud with basalt islands and scattered small bones, a lone watchtower on the rim; the effect is a dust devil turning slowly over the basin.
