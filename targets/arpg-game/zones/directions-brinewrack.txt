# The Brinewrack brief's three directions, one per generator.
The wreck graveyard: hulls lie everywhere on the shingle like the ribs of dead beasts (bones and arches as ribs), with the lighthouse far off; the effect is green phosphorescence in the pools between them.
The drowned village: the ground is half tide-pools, cottages' stone stumps and broken waysides rise from the water, and the effect is a green glow crawling slowly along the waterline.
The lighthouse headland: high, bare and wind-scoured, black rock and thin grass, the dead lighthouse (a tower) as the landmark; the effect is spray drifting inland off the cliffs in green-lit wisps.
