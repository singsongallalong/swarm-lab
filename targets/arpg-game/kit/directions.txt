# The Eternal Domain's kit list: one piece per worker.
A colonnade column of the gods: a colossal fluted column of ivory marble with a gilt capital and base, 40-60 studs tall; some seeds whole, some snapped with the drum lying beside it.
A seated colossus of a god on a throne, 60-90 studs tall, ivory marble with gold crown, armlets and hem, hands on its knees, faceless and serene; the room is built around it.
The Great Gate of the gods: a towering double door of gold set in an ivory frame with a stepped lintel, 30-40 studs tall, panels in relief; seeds closed, ajar, or one leaf fallen.
A triumphal arch of white stone spanning a hall, 25-35 studs, with gilt bands and a keystone of gold, through which a path of light runs; your set piece is the light streaming through it.
A spire of the gods: a slender white tower in tiers, each tier ringed in gold, crowned by a gold finial that burns; your set piece is the flame of light at its crown.
A throne-dais: a stepped round dais of marble with an empty gold throne, braziers at its corners, the seat of an absent god; your set piece is a slow fall of gold motes over the empty seat.
A holy brazier on a tall gold stand, 8-14 studs, a bowl of white light; the dungeon's lamp, placed by the hundred. Your set piece is its fire: pale gold flame and rising sparks.
The face of a god carved into a wall, 20-30 studs, eyes closed, gilt at the brow; seeds vary the expression (serene, grieving, stern) and whether it is cracked.
A tree of gold: a stylised sacred tree with a pale stone trunk and gilt leaves in tiers, 20-35 studs; your set piece is leaves of light drifting down from it.
A pool of light: a round basin of marble steps going down to still water that glows white-gold, 16-30 studs across; your set piece is the light rising off its surface.
Waystones of the gods: a ring or line of tall marble pillars inscribed in gold, each with a band of light; the markers that lead runs through the halls. Your set piece is light running from stone to stone.
A fallen god: a colossal marble statue broken on the floor, its head, a hand and a torso in pieces with gold armour lying among them, 40-70 studs across; the wreck an intrusion left.
