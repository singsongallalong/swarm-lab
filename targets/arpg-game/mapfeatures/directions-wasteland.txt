# The Wasteland's set pieces (2026-10-31, overnight run): one per worker, each a different kind of place in the glass-ash desert.
A glass strike: where a bolt of the gate's storm hit the dunes, a crater of fused violet glass with shattered glass spires thrown up round its rim like a frozen splash, the ash around it scorched dark in rays, violet sparks still crawling over the glass.
A buried caravan: a line of wagons half swallowed by a dune that rolled over them, their ribs and wheels and a broken canopy showing on the dune's lee face, crates spilled down the slope, one wagon's lantern-hook still swinging, ash pouring off the crest above it.
The colossus's hand: the open stone hand of a fallen giant statue as big as a house, rising out of the ash palm-up, its fingers curled into a shelter, glass grown over its knuckles, a dune banked against its wrist.
