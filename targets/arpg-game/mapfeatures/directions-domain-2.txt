# The Eternal Domain, second run: kinds of place the first shelf lacks (halls, thrones, theatres, social spaces, partly organic gardens, figures). One per worker.
The great hall of the gods: a long colonnaded hall, roofless or open-beamed, its floor a gold-inlaid aisle between two rows of colossal columns, a dais at the far end; the road runs its length.
The throne of the absent god: a raised throne terrace with a colossal empty throne (your own assembly sculpt) on a stepped dais, flanked by kneeling statues, gold braziers and banners.
The feasting hall (Valhalla's mead hall): long tables of white stone with gold vessels, benches, a central fire trench burning with holy fire, shields hung on the columns.
The theatre of the heavens: a semicircular theatre with a raised stage and a colonnaded backdrop (scaenae frons), tiered seats rising behind, statues in the backdrop's niches.
The baths of the gods: a thermae court of sunken pools at different levels, steam rising, colonnades round them, water spilling from one pool to the next.
The hall of heroes: an avenue of colossal statues of heroes (your own assembly sculpts: armoured figures with spears and shields), each on a plinth, lining a processional way.
The sculpture garden: a lawn court of clipped turf with pale statues on plinths, gravel paths in a formal pattern, a few gold-leaved trees.
The sacred grove: a ring of tall pale-barked trees with gold leaves round a clearing with an altar, roots breaking the paving, light falling through the canopy.
The orchard of golden apples: rows of small trees heavy with glowing gold fruit on terraces, a low wall, a guardian statue at its gate.
The water garden: long rills and channels of water in a geometric garden of pale paving, lily pools, fountains, a pavilion at the centre.
The cloister: a square arcaded walk round a garden court with a fountain at its heart, the road passing through one side of the arcade.
The hedge labyrinth: a maze of tall clipped hedges with white stone gateways, a gold statue at its centre, paths that branch and dead-end.
The terraced vineyard: steps of terraces planted with low vines, stone retaining walls, a winepress pavilion at the top, light-wine glowing in its vats.
The library of tablets: a court of tall stone shelves holding gold tablets, reading lecterns, a central rotunda with a sealed book on a pedestal (assembly sculpts).
The forge of the gods: an anvil terrace with a colossal anvil and hammer, a furnace glowing white-gold, half-made armour and weapons on racks, sparks flying.
The observatory: a raised platform with a great armillary sphere (assembly sculpt) and star-dials set into the paving, steps up to it, a telescope pillar.
The mausoleum of a god: a sealed tomb building on a stepped plinth with a colonnade and a colossal sarcophagus visible through its door, guardian statues.
The arena of champions: an oval fighting floor sunk below tiers of seating, gates at both ends, weapon racks, a royal box with a canopy.
The bell court: a square with a tall open belfry tower holding a colossal gold bell (assembly sculpt), the bell's light pulsing outward.
The sky docks: piers of white stone running out over the cloud, golden ships' prows moored at them (assembly sculpts), cranes and stacked cargo.
The rainbow bridge's foot: the end of a bridge of prismatic light rising out of sight, a guarded gatehouse at its base, a watchman's horn on a stand.
The garden of fountains: a plaza of many fountains in a grid, each a different statue spouting water, paved paths between low hedges.
The crystal conservatory: a glasshouse of pale blue glass panes on a gold frame (assembly), exotic gold-leaved plants inside, a fountain at its heart.
The choir of statues: a curving terrace of singing stone figures (assembly) in rows, arms raised, a pillar of light where their song meets.
