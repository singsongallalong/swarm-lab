# The Eternal Domain's features: one radical kind of heavenly place per worker.
The sky-causeway: a narrow processional road of ivory paving running out over the cloud sea on tall arches, gold braziers at every span, the cloud far below on both sides; the road crosses it.
The hall of the fallen gods: a roofless colonnaded court where colossal gilded statues lie toppled in pieces across the floor, columns standing and broken, holy light falling in shafts.
The stair of ascent: a vast stepped ziggurat terrace rising in five broad tiers to a crowning platform with a burning golden brazier, the road climbing its axis by ramps.
The mirror court: a great still reflecting pool of pale water in an ivory court, gold-rimmed, with a causeway across it and columns doubling in the water; motes rising off its surface.
The broken orrery: a sunken circular plaza with concentric gold rings and spheres of a fallen celestial machine lying tilted in it, light pulsing in its grooves.
Valkyrie landings: a cluster of round platforms on tall pillars at different heights, joined by ramps and short bridges, each crowned with a winged gold standard.
Islands in the cloud: a scatter of small floating-looking stone islands at the cloud's edge, chained to the ground with gold chains, the gaps between them sheer drops to cloud.
The gate of judgement: a monumental gatehouse of two colossal pylons with a gold lintel spanning the road, the ground before it a paved forecourt, light pouring through the gate.
The hanging gardens: tiered terraces of white stone stepping down from a central tower, each tier planted with gold-leaved trees and spilling light-falls over its edge.
The thousand pillars: a dense grid of tall ivory columns on a vast paved floor, a forest of stone the road threads through, some columns fallen, light between them.
The burning stair: a long straight stair descending into a sunken amphitheatre ringed with gold seats, a pillar of holy fire at its centre.
The cloudfall: the edge of the Domain where the paving breaks off into a cascade of cloud pouring down past broken terraces, a balustrade along the brink, the road skirting it.
