# C: walnut bodice with layered skirt and wheat circlet

**Most important fix: the colour mass.** It is nearly all dark walnut, and from the ARPG camera it sinks into the night and reads as layered lamellar armour, not a dress. Make the skirt or apron wheat-gold or linen so a light, warm shape carries the figure.

2. **Sleeves and shoulders.** The tiered dark sleeve blocks are as wide as the shoulders and stack horizontal bands, like pauldrons. Use slim fitted sleeves with volume only at the forearm.
3. **The skirt has no flare and splits at the legs.** The front view shows dark leg columns and diagonal struts under a short skirt. Close it into one bell that widens to the ground.
4. **Headpiece.** The wheat-ear circlet is a good idea, but it reads as small horns. Fan more ears into a crown or wreath shape.
5. The thin linen-and-green front panel is the only pretty element. Make it the centre of a lighter design.

Fit avatar: same armour read, slightly narrower.
