# A: hearth-red dress, green apron, linen cap

**Most important fix: give the skirt a real bell.** From the ARPG camera the skirt is a straight red box under the waist and the hem block sits on it like a crate, so the figure reads as a column, not a gown. Step the skirt out from the hip in 2-3 widening tiers so the lowest edge is clearly the widest point.

2. **Slim the sleeves.** They are full shoulder-width red blocks with gold bands, the exact broad, bulky top the brief warns against. Fit them to the arm, and put any volume (a cuff or puff) at the forearm only.
3. **Separate it from Studded.** Dark red with gold banding from above is very close to the studded hybrid. Let the linen and wheat-gold carry more area (a wider apron, a pale underskirt band at the hem) so the warm red is an accent, not the whole mass.
4. **Headpiece.** The small linen cap with a sprig is lost from above. Make it a bolder kerchief or a ring of wheat that gives height and a lighter crown.
5. The green apron is a nice homely touch. Keep it, and let it widen with the skirt.

Fit avatar: holds together but looks even more like a tube.
