# E: haystack bell skirt, green sleeves, red kerchief

**Most important fix: the shoulders.** The large green sleeve blocks sit high and stand out past the shoulders, so the outline is a broad-topped pyramid. Make the sleeves slim and fitted, with volume only at the forearm. That alone would turn the silhouette from a mound into a woman.

2. **Refine the bell.** This has the boldest flare in the run, which the brief asked for, but the stacked linen and green slabs read as a ziggurat or a hay bale from above. Use fewer, larger tiers with a smoother step, and keep a clear waist above them.
3. **The headpiece.** The red kerchief with a hooked spike on top reads as a kettle or a lantern. Drop the hook and give it a soft bonnet or kerchief knot, or a wheat sprig.
4. **Spend parts on the outline.** The many thin linen bands across the skirt cost parts and add noise. Merge them.
5. The colours fit the zone (field green, linen, walnut, a hearth-red head), and the X lacing panel is a nice touch.

Fit avatar: the skirt holds its flare; the shoulders still read broad.
