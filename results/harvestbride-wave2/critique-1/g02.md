# B: linen bride dress, red sash, small crown

2. **Pull it away from the Linen family.** All-oatmeal linen with a thin red sash reads as the existing linen set from above. Bring in the zone palette: a wheat-gold or field-green skirt under a linen apron, and a hearth-red bodice or a wider sash.
3. **More flare.** The skirt widens only slightly at the hem step. Widen from the hip in visible tiers so the base is clearly the widest point.
4. **Headpiece.** The tiny circlet barely shows. A fuller wheat or flower wreath would add height and say "village wedding" at a glance.
5. The plain light gown does stand out against the night, and its quiet bridal mood fits the brief. Keep that clarity, and fix the proportions and colour identity.
