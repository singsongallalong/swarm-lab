# F: wheat wreath, walnut bodice, linen sleeves, wheat-gold split skirt

**Most important fix: the sleeves.** The pale linen sleeve blocks are as wide as the shoulders or wider, with red bands. From ARPG they are the brightest, widest shapes, so the figure reads top-heavy. Slim them to the arm, and keep the red-banded cuff as the only forearm volume.

2. **Give the skirt a real flare.** The wheat-gold overskirt split over a linen underskirt is the prettiest idea in the run, but it hangs nearly straight. Flare both layers outward from the hip so the hem is clearly the widest point.
3. **Keep the wreath.** The rising wheat-stalk crown gives height at the head and says harvest festival. Spread the stalks a touch so they read as a crown, not a brush.
4. The palette is the closest match to the brief: wheat gold, apron linen, hearth-red ribbons, walnut bodice. Keep it.
5. The dark bodice centre goes black from above. A linen or red lacing stripe would keep the waist visible.

Fit avatar: holds together. The skirt looks even straighter.
