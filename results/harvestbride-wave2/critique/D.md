# D — straw hat, green tiered country dress

1. **Sleeves are still broad grey-linen blocks with stacked bands**; from above they make the shoulders the widest point before the hem. Slim the upper sleeve to the arm and keep the banded volume only at the forearm. This is the main thing between this and a clear winner.
2. **Mostly dark green from behind and above risks vanishing at night.** The back view is a near-black green cone. Lighten the tiers' top surfaces (wheat-gold or linen hem bands on each tier edge) so the bell reads as rings from the ARPG camera.
3. **Keep the straw hat with red ribbon** — the strongest single read in the wave: from the ARPG camera it says farm girl at once and is unlike any other family.
4. **Keep the tiered bell skirt.** Side view is a proper cone; the flare is real and holds on the slim avatar. Consider one bolder bottom tier rather than many thin steps, to spend fewer parts.
5. Grey sleeves are off-palette; use apron linen or dark walnut. A hearth-red waist sash would add warmth and a waist.
