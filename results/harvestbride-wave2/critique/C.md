# C — dark walnut bodice, ruffled linen cuffs

1. **The dark walnut shoulder slab dominates and swallows her.** From the ARPG camera the figure is a broad dark-brown block with a gold head; the gown nearly vanishes into the night. Narrow the shoulders hard (no blocks past the torso edge) and lighten or redden the bodice.
2. **No flare.** The skirt is a straight column front and back, gold at the back, linen at the front. Give it a real bell from the hip: widening tiers ending well past the hips.
3. **Keep the ruffled forearm cuffs** — layered linen frills at the forearm are exactly where the brief wants sleeve volume, and the best sleeve idea in the wave. But the upper sleeve is still a full-width dark block; slim it.
4. The front linen panel with a green stripe is pretty up close but invisible from above. Move colour to the top surfaces: shoulders, waist sash, hem rim.
5. The circlet is a nice idea but too low to add height. Raise the wheat spikes. Slim avatar holds but reads even more as a dark column.
