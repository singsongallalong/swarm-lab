# B — linen blouse, wheat-gold apron skirt

1. **Sleeves are bulky linen blocks the width of the shoulders**: the upper body from above is one pale slab. Slim the upper arm (walnut or red, close to the arm) and keep any linen fullness only at the forearm.
2. **The skirt flares but stops short and stays flat.** The trapezoid from the waist is the right idea and reads well from ARPG, but the hem tier is just a box plinth. Add one more widening tier and break the front with the white apron dropping lower so the bell carries to the ground.
3. **Too much linen and tan.** Pale blouse plus gold skirt risks reading as the linen family. Push a hearth-red or field-green note big enough to see from above: a red bodice/laced vest over the blouse, or a red hem band.
4. **Headpiece is lost.** The wheat sprigs sit flat on the head and blend with the yellow head. Make it a clear wreath or tall wheat crown with red ribbon so the head reads as height.
5. Back view is a drab brown bell; give it a red sash bow at the waist so the back reads as a dress too. Slim fit holds well.
