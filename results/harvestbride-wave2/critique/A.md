# A — hearth-red pinafore over green apron

1. **The skirt does not flare.** From front and side it is a straight column with one small step at the hem; from the ARPG camera it reads as a red box. Give the Boots slot a real bell: two or three widening tiers from hip to hem, the bottom tier at least 1.6x hip width, and let the side view show a cone, not a tube.
2. **Sleeves are full shoulder-width linen blocks.** Pale blocks on the shoulders are the brightest thing from above and make her broad. Narrow the upper arm to a slim sleeve in dark walnut or red and move the linen volume to a forearm cuff or puff.
3. **Too close to the studded family from above.** Dark hearth red plus pale shoulders reads like the hybrid set. Let the green apron and a wheat-gold accent show from the top: a gold waist sash or hem band would separate it.
4. **Headpiece is too small to matter.** The little linen cap with a sprig gives no height. A taller wheat or flower crown would add the head height the brief wants.
5. Slim avatar: holds together, but the column gets even narrower and more boxy. Spend parts on the hem, not on bodice detail invisible from above.
