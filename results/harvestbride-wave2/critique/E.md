# E — red kerchief, olive bodice, green split skirt

1. **Shoulders are the widest, heaviest thing in the upper body.** Olive-brown shoulder blocks plus stacked linen and gold sleeve bands make a broad, armoured yoke from above. Remove anything past the torso edge and slim the sleeves; keep one linen cuff at the forearm.
2. **The headpiece hides the head as a flat red box.** From the ARPG camera it reads as a helmet, not a kerchief. Make it lower and softer at the back, or add a small height element (wheat sprig, flower), so the head reads as a woman's.
3. **The skirt flare is real and the widest in the wave** — good. But the green panels are boxy and ragged at the hem and the back is near-black green. Tidy the hem into a clean bell and add a light hem band.
4. The linen front panel between green side panels is a good apron idea; let it carry a red hem stripe so it reads as festive.
5. Overall palette is muddy (olive, brown, dark green); one clear warm accent (hearth red at the waist) would lift the prettiness.
