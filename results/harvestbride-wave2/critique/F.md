# F — wheat-sheaf crown, laced bodice, gold over-skirt

1. **Sleeves are full-width pale linen blocks**, the brightest, broadest shapes from above. Slim them to the arm; put fullness only at the forearm (the red-trimmed cuff is a good start, make it the volume).
2. **The skirt barely flares.** The gold over-skirt parts over a linen underskirt — a lovely idea — but the silhouette is still near-column with a ragged hem. Widen from the hip into a clear bell, hem at least 1.6x hip width.
3. **Keep the wheat-sheaf crown**: it gives height at the head and the zone's wheat-gold identity, and reads from the ARPG camera. Add the red flowers bigger so they read too.
4. The dark laced bodice gives a defined waist from the front; good. From above it is a dark square between pale shoulders, so add a red waist sash to tie bodice to skirt.
5. Hem is ragged (bits sticking out on the slim avatar); tidy it into a few bold panels.
