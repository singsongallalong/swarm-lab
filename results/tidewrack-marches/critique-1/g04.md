# A: teal bodice, salt-grey bell skirt, comb crest

**Most important fix: cut the teal by about two thirds.** From the ARPG camera the whole upper half is one wide teal slab (shoulders, sleeve tops, bodice). The brief says teal sparingly. Make the bodice and shoulder tops driftwood grey-brown or wet sand, and keep teal for the waist sash and one hem stripe. Right now the set reads as "teal robe", not "salvage and salt".

What works:
- The front skirt is the best bell shape in the field: it flares cleanly from the rope belt to the shins, and it holds on the slim fit avatar with no gaps.
- The rope belt hanging at the hip is a good shore detail and it shows at the waist.
- The silver comb/fan crest is a decent shell-crest headpiece.

What to change:
- The shoulders are as wide as the sleeves and flat-topped, which makes a boxy, masculine yoke from above. Narrow the shoulder tops and drop them, so the eye goes to the waist.
- The back is a dark grey coat with split panels and a teal stripe. It reads as a mage coat, not a gown. Carry the bell flare round the back and lighten it toward wet sand.
- The pale skirt is close to flat white-grey. Warm it toward ivory/salt and age the hem with a darker sand band.
- The crest is small and grey against the yellow head at pitch 50. Give it more height or a brass rim so it reads.
