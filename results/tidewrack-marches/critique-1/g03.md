# B: slate column, bright-blue shoulders, spiked crown

**Most important fix: remove the saturated blue shoulder tops.** From the ARPG camera they are the brightest thing on the model: two flat cobalt slabs that are off-palette (nothing in the brief is that blue) and that broaden the shoulders, which the brief forbids. Recolour them to driftwood or tarnished brass, or remove them and let the shoulders slope.

What works:
- The ivory bands across sleeves and hem are tidy and read as salt-bleached trim.
- The skirt is continuous on both avatars with no gaps or poke-through.
- The pale spiked crown is a strong, visible headpiece.

What to change:
- The skirt is a straight rectangle from hip to floor with no flare and no waist. With the uniform slate colour, the silhouette reads as a buttoned military greatcoat or a stack of iron, not a dress. Pull in the waist with a sash and step the skirt out at least two widths toward the hem.
- The slate/navy palette is not on the brief (salt, driftwood, sand, brass). Move the body to driftwood grey-brown with salt panels.
- The triple vertical bars on the chest and the back emblem cannot be seen from above. Spend those parts on a hem flare or a draped overskirt.
- The spiked crown reads as royalty or thorns. Make the spikes shell ridges or coral, warmer in tone.
- Overall it is the least feminine and the least "shore" of the six.
