# F: corsair tricorn, pale bodice, boxed skirt

**Most important fix: remove the waist shelf and flare the skirt.** A wide white belt ledge juts out at the hips like a tray, and below it the skirt drops straight down as a box. From the ARPG view that shelf is the widest edge, so the figure reads as a waistcoat over trousers, not a gown. Cut the ledge back to a sash and widen the skirt toward the shins.

What works:
- The tricorn hat with a pale plume is the best theme idea in the field: it says corsair at a glance, and it reads as a strong headpiece from above.
- The brown braces over the pale bodice, with a teal cravat tie, are a nice nautical touch.
- No gaps or floating parts on either avatar.

What to change:
- The bright cobalt shoulder caps are off-palette and add width at the shoulders, against the brief. Recolour them to brass or driftwood, or remove them.
- The back is near-black brown from collar to hem and the skirt splits into two dark leg columns. From behind it reads as trousers. Make the back skirt one continuous flared panel in driftwood or sand.
- The front skirt is plain pale grey. Warm it to salt/ivory and add a sand or teal hem band.
- The sleeves are full-width boxes. Slim them so the waist reads.
