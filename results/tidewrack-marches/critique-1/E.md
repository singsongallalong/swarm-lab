# E: driftwood corset over sand A-line, brass circlet

**Most important fix: give it a headpiece that reads from above.** The low brass circlet disappears into the yellow head at pitch 50, so from the ARPG view the set has no crown at all. Add height: a shell comb, a coral or driftwood spray, or a sailcloth headscarf with a tied tail, in salt/brass.

What works:
- The palette matches the brief best: wet sand front, driftwood grey-brown back and sleeves, a dark corset band, and teal used only as small ties and edge lines. It is properly night-muted.
- The A-line skirt widens smoothly to the shins in banded steps, and it holds on the slim fit avatar with no gaps or poke-through.
- The cinched corset band gives the clearest waist of the six.

What to change:
- The sleeves are tall stacked rings that stand proud of the shoulders. From the ARPG view the shoulder line is as wide as the hem, which flattens the hourglass. Slim the upper sleeves and drop the shoulder tops.
- The skirt flare is modest. Push the bottom band out another step for a bolder, prettier outline (D shows how much reads from above).
- The back is a single dark trapezoid. Add a pale sailcloth panel or a teal hem line so the back still says "dress".
