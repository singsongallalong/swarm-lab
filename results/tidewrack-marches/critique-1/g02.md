# C: pale linen shift with laced bodice and wrap headband

**Most important fix: give the skirt a flare and a waist.** The skirt hangs straight and narrow from the chest to the shins. At ARPG pitch the model is a pale column with two huge cuffed sleeves either side, too close to the linen robe family. Cinch a waist with a driftwood or brass belt, and step the skirt out toward the shins so the outline says "gown".

What works:
- The palette is the most restrained in the field: salt, ash grey, thin teal and brass cuff stripes. It is aged and night-muted, as asked.
- The laced chest panel and the ragged apron hem suggest salvaged sailcloth.
- It holds on the fit avatar with no gaps.

What to change:
- The sleeves are bulky stacked cuffs that stick out past the skirt. From above they form the widest part of the silhouette, which makes it read as armour. Slim them and move the mass to the hem.
- The back is entirely mid-grey and flat, so from behind it reads as a plain tunic. Bring the pale sailcloth round, or add a darker draped back panel that flares.
- The wrap headband sits flat and is hard to see from above. Add a raised element (shell, knot, tied scarf tail) so there is a headpiece silhouette.
- Pale is fine, but the front is close to pure white-grey. Warm it to ivory.
