# B: shell-crest, tan bodice, barrel skirt

**Most important fix:** rebuild the skirt. Right now it is a straight dark-brown box from waist to shin, as wide as the shoulders. That reads as a barrel or kilt, not a flaring or draping skirt, and with the broad tan shoulders the whole figure is a rectangle. Narrow the waist and let the skirt widen toward the hem.

**What works**
- The fanned shell crest is the most "shell" headpiece in the set, and it reads from above.
- The wet-sand and driftwood palette fits the zone.

**What to change**
- The wide salt-white belt band sticks out past the torso on both sides. It is the strongest horizontal on the figure and cuts the body in half right where the waist should narrow. Make it thinner, or make it a slim corset that stays inside the silhouette.
- The shoulders and sleeves are big tan blocks, which pushes against the narrow-shoulder brief. From ARPG the figure reads as a hybrid or studded family member in sand colours.
- No teal is visible at a glance, and there is almost no ivory or bone apart from the belt. Bring a pale hem or a pale under-layer in so the skirt reads as a dress.
- The side view is a dark column with no front-to-back flare.
- The hem trims can't be seen from above. Spend those parts on flare.
