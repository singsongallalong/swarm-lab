# D: salt-grey bodice, teal sash, stepped sand hem

**Most important fix:** fix the inverted silhouette. The torso and sleeves are the widest, heaviest mass and the skirt is a narrow straight column, so the figure is top-heavy. The wet-sand rings at the floor then read as a plinth or cake stand, not a hem. Narrow the bodice and make the skirt widen smoothly from waist to shin, with the sand tiers as its lower edge, not separate steps under the feet.

**What works**
- The palette is strong: pale salt-grey, a single teal sash with a hanging tail, and wet-sand hems. It is muted and on-brief.
- The bone or driftwood spike comb on the head is a bold, distinctive headpiece.
- The back cross-lacing is a nice idea.

**What to change**
- From ARPG it is a big pale box. Nothing says dress until the hem, and the hem is mostly hidden. It reads closer to robe or linen than to gown.
- No waist. The teal sash sits on a straight block. Pull the waist in under it.
- The skirt column is too narrow for the bodice on the slim avatar too.
- The thin horizontal lines across the bodice can't be seen from above. Spend those parts on skirt flare.
- The pale grey is a bit close to pure white in the close render. Age it toward bone.
