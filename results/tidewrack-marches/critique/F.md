# F: corsair tricorn, driftwood coat-dress, ivory skirt

**Most important fix:** flare the skirt. At the moment it is a straight ivory column under a long driftwood coat. From above the coat and tricorn dominate, and the figure reads as a corsair in a coat, which sits close to the leather family, rather than a gown. Widen the ivory skirt toward the shin, and let the coat skirts open and fall back at the hips so the dress shows.

**What works**
- It is the boldest and most distinct identity: the feathered tricorn reads instantly from the ARPG camera and nails the corsair half of the brief.
- The palette is good: driftwood coat, ivory skirt, a teal cravat and teal hem trims. The bright blue shoulders from the earlier round are gone, which was the right call.
- The skirt is continuous with no gaps on the fit avatar.

**What to change**
- The shoulders and sleeves are big brown blocks with stacked pale cuffs. It's masculine and broad on top. Narrow and darken the sleeves.
- No visible fitted waist. Add a cinched sash or corset at the coat's waist.
- From the back it is all dark brown and reads as a coat.
- The cravat and chest lapels are fine detail. Put those parts into the coat-tail and skirt silhouette instead.
