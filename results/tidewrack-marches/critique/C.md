# C: salt-white laced gown, tattered sailcloth drapes, wrapped headpiece

**Most important fix:** add contrast. Nearly everything is the same salt-ivory, so from the high camera it blurs into one pale block and could pass for linen. Give the bodice a driftwood or tarnished-brass corset, and darken the under-layer, so the waist reads and the pale drapes stand out against it.

**What works**
- It has the most theme of the six: torn sailcloth panels hang from the hips and fan into ragged points. It feels like something salvaged from a wreck, and the drape is pretty.
- The cross-laced bodice is a good feminine touch. The wrapped headscarf with a small brass and teal piece suits a shore woman.
- The skirt drapes toward the shin with a clear outward splay.

**What to change**
- In the ARPG and fit views the side tatters splay sideways like loose sheets, and some read as detached from the body. Angle them back to the skirt so they hang rather than stick out.
- The waist isn't defined. Torso and skirt share one colour and width. Add a sash, or step the width in under the bust.
- The headpiece is low and small. From above it is a pale cap. A little more height would help, such as a shell comb or a wrapped knot.
- The back is grey-brown and loses the sailcloth character.
