# A: shell-fan helm, driftwood bodice, ivory bell skirt

**Most important fix:** slim the sleeve cuffs. The pale stacked bands at elbow and wrist sit level with the hips and make the widest point of the figure there. From the ARPG camera the skirt flare gets lost behind them, and the top-down read is mostly shoulders and helm. Pull them in so the bell skirt is widest below the waist.

**What works**
- This is the clearest gown here. A dark driftwood bodice over a salt-ivory A-line skirt gives a fitted waist and a skirt that flares to the shins. It reads as a dress at once, apart from iron, leather and studded.
- The upright shell or fin fan on the helm is a good headpiece. It is feminine and rises from the head, not the shoulders.
- The palette is right: ivory, driftwood, brass lines and one small teal chevron. Teal is used sparingly.
- The skirt holds on the slim fit avatar, with no gaps from thigh to shin.

**What to change**
- The back skirt is dark grey and the front is ivory. From behind and from the ARPG angle it looks like a different garment. Carry some ivory round to the back, or let the dark be an open overskirt with the ivory showing through.
- The brass belt chevron and the small chest marks can't be seen from above. Put those parts into a scalloped or tattered hem edge, which can.
