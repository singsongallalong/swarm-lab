# E: ivory bell gown, dark stomacher, teal trims, fin cap

**Most important fix:** remove or reattach the pale bar that sticks out at the hip and hem. In the ARPG and three-quarter views a white rod, like a hook or oar, juts out from the right of the skirt and reads as a floating part. Hang it flat against the skirt, or drop it and put the parts into the hem.

**What works**
- It reads as a dress from above. An ivory bodice and skirt with a dark driftwood stomacher panel make a clear waist, and the skirt widens to a trapezoid bell with small flared corners at the hem.
- Teal is used just right: thin trims at bust and hem plus a hanging teal cord at the waist.
- The colours are aged ivory and grey-brown, not pure white. They sit well in a night palette.
- It holds well on the slim fit avatar.

**What to change**
- The headpiece is a small pale cap with a little fin. It is too modest for the high camera. Give it a taller shell or fin crest.
- The sleeves are boxy pale blocks with teal bands, and they make the shoulders read broad from above. Narrow them, or darken the upper arm.
- The skirt sides are straight planes. A slight drape or an open front split would add elegance.
