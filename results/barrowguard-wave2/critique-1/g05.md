# E - critique

**Most important fix: darken and bronze the hauberk.** The tan body plus the tan helm reads close to linen (pale oatmeal) from above. Push it to a darker, greener tarnished bronze, and let mould-black show at the joints.

1. **Mantle.** The wide flat moss-green shoulder mantle gives the clearest outline in the batch from the ARPG camera, a broad flat slab. But plain green slabs risk reading as fenscale (grey-green) cloth. Break the edge: make it ragged, step the line or add bronze rim plates, so it reads as a rotted grave mantle over armour.
2. **Helm.** The tall helm with its bone crest is good. The yellow eye band is very bright and fights the night palette. Narrow the eye slit or shade it darker.
3. **Tabard and greaves.** The long green tabard strip down the front, and the greaves covering the legs, give the set a lower-body silhouette that the others lack. Keep them.
4. **Fit.** The mantle overhangs the arms by a lot, so check that it does not cut into the raised arm on the slim avatar. Seat it closer to the shoulder.
5. **Names.** Hauberk, Gauntlets and Greaves are fine and keep the Barrowguard prefix.
6. **Parts.** The thin repeated bands on the gauntlets cannot be read from above. Fold them into fewer, thicker bands.
