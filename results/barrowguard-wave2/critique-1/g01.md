# C - critique

**Most important fix: pull the body out of brown.** The striped brown tunic with its baldric reads as leather (brown and slim) from the ARPG camera. Push the stripes towards tarnished bronze with green verdigris, and make the black under-layer the leather.

1. **Back roundel.** The big round bronze grave-shield slung on the back is a real silhouette idea and the most distinctive back view in the batch. Commit to it: make it larger, give it a raised bone or bronze boss, and let moss creep around its rim. It should be the first thing seen from above.
2. **Helm.** The domed cap with moss spots is quiet and reads as a hat. Add a nasal or cheek guards, or a low crown rim, so it carries the faintly regal note. Right now the yellow face is fully exposed.
3. **Bone.** None of the brief's bone shows. Bone studs on the shield boss or a bone gorget would add it.
4. **Pauldrons.** One shoulder is black and the other bronze. The asymmetry is good, but the black one disappears into the sleeve, so make it a different shape and not only a different colour.
5. **Fit.** Solid. Nothing floats, the shield sits flat on the back and the slim avatar holds.
6. **Parts.** The tunic stripe bands cost a lot and blur together from above. Move some of them into the shield and the helm.
