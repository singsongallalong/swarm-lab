# A - critique

**Most important fix: give the set a silhouette idea and get it out of the green.** From the ARPG camera this is a green box helm on a green-and-black body. Grave-moss green is carrying almost all of the colour, so the set drifts towards fenscale (grey-green). Tarnished bronze and bone hardly show.

1. **Colour.** Make bronze the main colour on the helm and pauldrons, and use moss only as growth on that bronze: patches, drips, one shoulder. Right now it is the reverse. Add a bone note the camera can catch, such as a bone brow ridge, a jaw guard or a bone crest along the helm top. At the moment the only bone is the thin top stripe.
2. **Silhouette.** The bucket great-helm with its pale visor slot is the strongest shape you have, but from above it reads as a plain cube. Push it into something regal and specific: a raised crown rim, a flared crest or a barrow-king ridge. Spend parts there.
3. **Pauldrons.** The small bronze shoulder caps are a nice touch. Make them bigger and asymmetric so one shoulder breaks the outline.
4. **Fit.** Good. Nothing floats, and it holds on the slim avatar. The black sleeve bands look heavy next to the slim arms, so trim their depth a little.
5. **Parts.** The front tassets and small chest buckles are invisible from above. Move those parts into the helm crown and the shoulder line.
