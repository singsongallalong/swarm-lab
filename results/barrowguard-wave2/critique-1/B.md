# B - critique

**Most important fix: open up the colour.** The body is near-black with thin bronze lines. From the ARPG camera it reads as a dark broad block, the same mass as iron (grey and broad) in shadow. In a night-bound world it will vanish.

1. **Colour.** Turn the bronze trim into bronze plates: a gorget, the pauldron tops and a chest plate. Mould-black should be the leather underneath, not the whole figure. Add bone somewhere visible; right now there is none.
2. **Crown helm.** The four-post bronze crown on the helm is the best idea here and reads clearly from above. Keep it and make it bigger. Make the posts taller and uneven, as if some have broken off over centuries, and add a bone or verdigris point to each.
3. **Face.** The helm sits as a band over the eyes and leaves the yellow lower face exposed. It looks like a blindfold, not a war helm. Add a cheek guard or mouth guard (bronze or bone) so the face reads as the dead guard's, not the avatar's.
4. **Shoulders.** The boxy black sleeves widen the outline the way iron does. Either slim them down or give them a distinct pauldron shape, such as a curved scale or a stepped barrow-stone plate.
5. **Fit.** Clean: nothing floats, and it holds on the slim avatar.
6. **Parts.** The thin belt and band lines cost parts that no one sees from above. Spend them on the crown.
