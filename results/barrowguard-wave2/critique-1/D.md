# D - critique

**Most important fix: make the chest plate read as bronze, not grey.** The grey-brown cuirass sits close to iron (grey and broad) from the front. Warm it towards tarnished bronze with green verdigris so the family colour is unmistakable.

1. **Bone crown.** The helm crowned with pale bone tines is the strongest regal idea in the batch and reads clearly from the ARPG camera. Keep it. Vary the tine heights a little more so the crown looks broken and old rather than a neat row.
2. **Moss.** The single moss-green pauldron and the moss patches on the back tell the story of a barrow-dug guard well. Let one moss patch spill from the shoulder onto the chest so the growth feels continuous, not stuck on.
3. **Palette.** You hit all four colours (bronze, moss, bone and black). Hold onto that balance.
4. **Fit.** Good overall. The moss pauldron is a broad flat slab, so check that it does not clip the arm on the slim avatar when the arm swings, and seat it a touch lower onto the shoulder.
5. **Helm face.** The face slot is dark and reads as a closed grave-helm. Good.
6. **Parts.** The small studs on the pauldrons and the thin belt lines are invisible from above. Spend them on crown tines or on a bone gorget at the collar.
