# F - critique

**Most important fix: change the horns.** Angled bone horns on a black helm read as a raider or Viking, which pulls the set towards the Reaver family (hide, fur and bone). Replace them with something regal and funerary: a bronze crown rim, a tall crest or upswept bronze cheek-wings.

1. **Colour.** The black-and-bronze banding works, and the moss pauldron plus the tan bronze pauldron give a nice asymmetry. Make the tan pauldron a darker, greener tarnished bronze; right now it reads as fresh brass or pale leather.
2. **Face.** The open eye-slit shows the bright yellow avatar eyes as a glowing band. Close it down, or shade the gap black, for a dead-guard face.
3. **Silhouette.** From the ARPG camera you only see a flat tan helm top with two small spikes. The horns are lost from above, so whatever replaces them needs height or width that is visible from overhead.
4. **Body.** The banded chest and gloves are busy with many thin bands that blur at distance. Use fewer, bolder bands.
5. **Fit.** Clean. Nothing floats, and the slim avatar holds. The boots read well with their bronze panels.
6. **Parts.** Too many parts go into thin banding. Move them into the helm idea and the pauldrons.
