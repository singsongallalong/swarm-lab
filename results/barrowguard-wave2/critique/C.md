# C: Barrow-disc guard (round bronze boss on the back)

**Most important fix:** remove or recolour the pale white block on the right shoulder. It is the brightest thing in the ARPG view and reads as a stray part, not as armour. If it is meant to be bone, make it bone-ivory, and match it on the left shoulder or move it to the helm.

- Silhouette idea: the round bronze grave-disc on the back is a genuinely new read from above and from behind. Commit to it. Make it a touch larger, and echo it with a small boss on the chest or helm so the front isn't plain banding.
- The tan rounded helm with glowing yellow eyes is effectively undead, but the yellow is hot for a muted night world. Dim it toward a pale bone glow.
- Colour: black and tarnished bronze separate well from every reference. The moss-green bands are thin. Make one band wider so the grave-moss note shows from above.
- Fit: the bulky black upper arms look heavy and block-like on the slim avatar. Tighten them.
- Parts: the stacked horizontal chest straps cost parts but read as noise. Keep two.
