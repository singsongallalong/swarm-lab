# D: Moss-grown crowned guard (bone crown spikes)

**Most important fix:** shift the base colour away from flat brown. Brown with green patches sits too close to leather and to the Reaver hide sets from the high camera. Push the metal to tarnished bronze with a gold edge, and let the moss stay as patches over it.

- Silhouette idea: the bone-spike crown is good and reads from above. Make it taller and more irregular (a broken crown), since that is the brief.
- Fantasy: the moss-camouflage patches are the best "dug out of the barrow" storytelling in the run. Keep them, but use fewer, larger patches. The tiny teal verdigris squares vanish beyond close range.
- The front and back don't match: a brown front over a dark green back reads like two different sets. Unify the back with the front's bronze.
- Fit: solid on both avatars. Nothing floats.
- Parts: the small verdigris studs and edge trims are spent on detail no one sees from above. Move them into the crown.
