# E: Green hauberk guard

**Most important fix:** the set reads as a green blob from the ARPG camera, much too close to fenscale's grey-green. Bring tarnished bronze in as the dominant metal (shoulders, helm and belt) and keep green as an accent of moss, not the base.

- Silhouette idea: there isn't one. Pauldrons, helm and skirt are all the same broad olive blocks. Pick one: a crested helm, a high bronze gorget, or a ragged burial cape.
- The yellow visor band is the strongest note on the figure, but it reads as a hi-vis stripe, not as regal. Dull it to bronze, or swap it for a bone face plate.
- Fantasy: there is little to say "barrow king" or "buried centuries". Add bone and some breakage.
- The renamed slots (Hauberk, Gauntlets, Greaves) are fine and on-theme.
- Fit: clean, and it holds on the slim avatar. The long tabard strip between the legs is good.
- Parts: the thin horizontal trim bands on the chest don't read from above. Spend them on the silhouette.
