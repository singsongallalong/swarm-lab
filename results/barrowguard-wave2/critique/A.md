# A: Crowned barrow guard (bone face, bronze crown)

**Most important fix:** pull the chest and pauldrons off mid-brown and push them to a greener, tarnished bronze (more olive-gold, plus verdigris). At the ARPG camera the torso reads as brown, which is the leather family's colour. The crown and bone face save the read, but the body is doing nothing to help.

- Silhouette idea: the crenellated bronze crown over a pale bone face mask. It is the strongest top-down read in the run, because the pale helm top pops in the ARPG and three-quarter views. Make it the one idea: raise the crown points a little and drop anything that competes with it.
- The layered dark pauldrons widen the shoulders well. Keep them, but cut the stacked edge strips you can only see in close-up and spend those parts on the crown.
- The moss-green flecks are too small to read from above. Merge them into one or two larger moss patches on the pauldrons.
- Fit: it holds on the slim avatar. The helm sits a touch high in the slim side view, so check the neck gap.
- The gloves and boots are dark and plain, which is fine, but add one bronze band on each so the set ties together at the feet.
