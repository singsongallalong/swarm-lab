# B: Black-and-bronze horned guard

**Most important fix:** give the set one clear silhouette idea. At the moment the two short helm prongs and flat bronze shoulder caps read as a generic dark knight. Turn the prongs into a proper barrow-king crest or crown, or make the pauldrons a bold, distinct line.

- Colour: the mould-black body with tan-bronze shoulder tops separates well from iron, leather and studded. But the bronze is too pale and clean. Tarnish it toward olive and add grave-moss green, which is almost absent apart from the avatar's own legs.
- Fantasy: "buried and regal" barely comes through. There is no bone, no decay and nothing kingly. A bone face plate or a bronze circlet would carry the story.
- The yellow band at the back of the helm is the brightest note on the figure and draws the eye to the wrong place. Dull it.
- Fit: clean. Nothing floats, and it holds on the slim avatar.
- Parts: an economical, tidy build. Spend the saved parts on the helm crest.
