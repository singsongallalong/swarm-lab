# F: Winged-helm lamellar guard

**Most important fix:** bronze is missing. The set is green and black, so from above it drifts toward fenscale, and its lamellar, swept-wing helm reads as samurai rather than barrow king. Make the lamellar plates tarnished bronze with moss-green lacing, not the other way round.

- Silhouette idea: the flared, winged helm is a strong, readable shape. Keep it as the one idea, but bend it toward a burial crown (upright points, a bone face) so it stops looking like a kabuto.
- The stacked lamellar bands on the pauldrons and chest give a good broad outline. They are banded so finely, though, that from the ARPG camera they turn to noise. Halve the number of bands.
- Fantasy: no bone and no decay. A bone face plate or a skull boss on the chest would sell "undead guard".
- Fit: it sits well. The striped boots are nice, and nothing floats.
- Parts: heavy on repeated bands. Recover parts there and spend them on the helm.
