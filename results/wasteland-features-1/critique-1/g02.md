# B: the colossus hand

**Keep:** the idea and the plan: from above every seed reads as a hand (splayed, curled, two-finger, clenched), rotated and placed differently, with violet glass on the knuckles and the motes cupped over the palm.

1. **Make the hand read from the side.** At eye level and from the game camera the fingers are a crowd of square dark pillars, a ruined city block rather than a hand. Taper each finger, step it down from knuckle to tip, give it two clear joints and a curl toward the palm, and keep the thumb lower and separate; the wrist should rise out of the dune as one broad ramp.
2. **Pull the camera shots back.** The "over the fingertips" and "game camera" shots sit inside the pillars and show only walls. Frame the whole hand with the palm as the subject and a person standing in it for scale; the seed 2 wrist shot is the only one that sells it.
3. **Light the palm.** The palm floor is the same grey as the dune; tint it violet glass, spread the knuckle glass farther along the fingers, and make the motes column taller and brighter so the palm is the one bright place in the frame. The ash and glint effects are too faint to matter; fold them into the motes.
