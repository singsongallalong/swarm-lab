# C: the glass strike

**Keep:** the violet glass bowl with its scorched dark rays and the spires thrown round the rim like a frozen splash (seed 2 "the frozen splash"), and the crawling sparks with their glowing nodes; it is the Wasteland's accent made into a place.

1. **Thin the rim so the bowl is the subject.** In the splash shots the spires crowd the foreground and hide the floor; mass them on one side of the rim (the side the bolt came from) in two or three big tilted clusters, leave the road side open, and keep a few lone needles inside the bowl for scale.
2. **Make the glass one sheet.** The floor reads as banded terraces of violet and grey; flatten the bowl floor to one or two levels with a smooth lip, and let the grey crack path cross it as the only line.
3. **Give the strike a centre.** Put one tall fused spire or a dark scorched pit at the point of impact, with the scorch rays and the sparks running out from it, so the game camera sees where the bolt landed; vary the crater's shape more across seeds (an elongated gouge, a double strike) so the layouts are not all one round bowl.
