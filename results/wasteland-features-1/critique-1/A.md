# A: the buried caravan

**Keep:** the wreck line read from the road with the gate behind it (seed 1 "along the wreck line"), and the wagon-ribs sculpt, which is the one prop that says "buried" on its own.

1. **Bury the wagons.** In every shot the wagons stand whole on top of the terraces, parked rather than overrun. Sink them axle-deep into the lee face, tilt them to the slope, and let ribs and a single wheel rim break the ash instead of full carts; the ribs prop should be the main piece, the whole wagon the rare one.
2. **Make the dune one landmark, not a field of terraces.** The plan shapes (two lobes, slabs, M-teeth, three islands) vary well, but at eye and game height they all read as a stack of fine flat steps with no crest. Give each seed one clear crest line with a steep slip face toward the road and a long shallow back, so the silhouette is a dune; keep fewer, taller steps.
3. **Give the scene a point.** The banked ember is the only warm light allowed in the Wasteland and it shows as a tiny orange dot. Put it at the foot of the slip face under the lantern hook, ring it with spilled crates, and aim the ashfall at the crest above it so the fire and the pouring ash frame one spot to fight around.
