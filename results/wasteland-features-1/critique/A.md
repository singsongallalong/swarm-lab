# A: buried caravan (critique, pass 2)

Keep: the wreck kit. The ribbed wagon, the half-sunk wheel, the crates and the lantern hook are the best-made props in this round, and "the fire on the lee" (seed 3) gives a real story read: canopy ribs, crates, the hook, the one warm spot.

1. **The dune is a pile of flat-topped rectangles.** On top views every seed shows a dark slab or two notched boxes, and from the game camera you see striped terraces with the wreck lost on them. Break the crest's outline: a curved or crescent plan, a taller crest stepping down toward the fire on one side only, and a long low tail on the wind side, so the slip face reads as one face and not a set of stairs. The silhouette matters more than the smoothing.
2. **The fire can't be found from above.** From the game camera the fire, crates and hook are a scatter of tiny brown chips. Clear a ring of flat ash around the fire wider than the crate ring, set the tipped wagon and a long rib line pointing into it, and put the ribs that break the ash on the crest's face so they frame the spot.
3. **Seeds are the same block shuffled.** Slab, two lobes, three stubs, a notched M: all rectangles on one axis. Let some seeds bury the caravan along the road and others across it, give one a broken double crest with the fire in the saddle, and turn the whole thing off the grid's axis now and then.
