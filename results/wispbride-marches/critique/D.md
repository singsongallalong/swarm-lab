Keep: the pale mist-grey base with willow-green trim; the palette is on brief.

1. The green bands wrap the arms and shoulders as horizontal hoops that stand proud of the body, so from above it reads as segmented armour with pauldrons: remove the banding from the shoulders and upper arms entirely and keep the arms slim and plain.
2. The skirt is a straight-sided box with a frill, no fitted waist, no flare: cinch the waist with a single dark sash and let the panels widen steadily to the shins.
3. The helm is a closed pale block like a knight's coif: replace it with an open veil or circlet with strands falling behind, and add one cold pale-blue accent that reads from the camera.
