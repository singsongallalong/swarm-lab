Keep: the dark willow strands hanging down the back of the skirt and the grey-green palette; it is the most "of the fen" set here.

1. The skirt is a straight column of vertical panels that never flares, and the bodice is a square block, so from above it reads as a robe or a tower, not a dress: nip the waist and splay the leg panels outward to the shins so the outline widens below the waist.
2. The crown of dark spikes rises well above the head and reads as reeds or a thorn crown, not a bride: lower and lighten it into a pale circlet with a short veil or strands falling behind, so the headpiece reads feminine.
3. The set is all mid grey-green with no pale and no glow, so it disappears into the mist: lift the bodice and veil to mist/bone so she is pale against black water, and add one cold pale-blue accent at the waist or hem.
