# A

Keep: the wisp-blue tipped prongs on the headpiece and the pale skirt over black hem bands. That is the right "pale against black water" idea, and the glow tips read from above.

1. The black sleeve blocks are the widest, darkest thing on the figure. From the ARPG camera they form a square armoured yoke past the shoulders and the body reads as iron, not a bride. Narrow the sleeves to the arm's width, keep them in the shoulder line, and make them mist grey or translucent-looking pale, with black only as a thin cuff band.
2. The bodice is a grey rectangle with a black frame and no waist. Pinch a narrow black or willow-green sash at the natural waist so the pale skirt visibly flares out below it, and start the skirt's widening right there, not at the knee.
3. Swap one of the black hem bands for willow-green drooping strands that hang from the waist over the skirt (long thin panels, slightly longer at the back). That brings in the willow line, which the set has none of now, and breaks up the stacked-stripe look.
