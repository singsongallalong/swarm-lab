# F

Keep: the pale mist gown with fine vertical pleats in the skirt and the willow-green laced bodice. It is soft, bridal and in palette.

1. The headpiece is a tall green box hat, which reads as a stovepipe or hood, not a bride's headdress. Replace it with a low willow-green circlet and a pale veil of strands that falls behind the shoulders to mid-back.
2. The skirt runs straight down from the hip to the shin. Flare it: make each lower panel wider than the one above so the hem is clearly broader than the hips from the front and from above, and keep the thigh-to-shin join closed on the slim avatar.
3. The wisp-blue glints on the back belt are too small to read from above. Move the cold blue to a large shape: a pale-blue hem edge all round, or a blue-tipped strand curtain on the back of the skirt. Narrow the pale sleeves slightly so they don't square the shoulders.
