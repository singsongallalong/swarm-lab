# E

Keep: the pale skirt that spreads into a wide triangular train at the feet, with the willow-green belt. It is the clearest flaring dress shape in the run from the ARPG camera.

1. The dark slate sleeves are wide blocks that stand past the shoulders and frame a narrow pale column. From above they read as an armoured coat over the dress. Bring the sleeves in to the arm's width and turn them mist grey, or make them long hanging sleeves that fall behind the arm, so the narrow shoulder line comes from the pale bodice.
2. The hem flare is bright white and pokes out in sharp separate points at the feet. Tone it to bone or mist, join the points into one continuous scalloped hem, and start the widening from the hip so the skirt reads as one bell rather than a column with a flare added at the bottom.
3. The back is dark with a face-like pattern that reads as a mask or armour plate. Carry the pale veil from the headpiece down the back as long drooping strands with wisp-blue tips, so the back is as bridal as the front.
