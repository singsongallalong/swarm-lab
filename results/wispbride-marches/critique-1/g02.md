# C

Keep: the reed-and-willow crown and the dark back panel of hanging strands. That is the most fen-like idea in the run and the truest willow grey-green palette.

1. Below the waist the gown splits into two dark legs, front and side. It reads as robe-over-trousers, not a skirt. Close the front with a continuous sage panel from waist to shin and widen it downward so the hem is broader than the hips from every angle, and on the slim avatar too.
2. The sleeves are boxy sage blocks with white cuffs that stick out at elbow height and widen the figure from above. Slim them to the arm, carry the strand idea onto them as long drooping hanging sleeves that fall behind the arm, and drop the bright white cuffs for bone or mist.
3. The bodice has no waist. Add a narrow dark (black-water) sash with a single pale wisp-blue clasp at the front. It fits the waist and gives the set one cold glow accent that reads from above.
