# D

Keep: the laced X bodice and the pale, tiered skirt layers. There is a dress in here.

1. The khaki wide-brimmed hat reads as a sun hat or ranger's hat, and from the ARPG camera it is the first and loudest thing you see. Replace it with a pale mist veil or a low circlet of drooping willow strands in grey-green, with a wisp-blue point, so the head reads as a bride of the fen.
2. The skirt is a set of square, equally wide tiers, and from above it is a box that hides the waist. Make the tiers grow wider going down, with the top tier hugging the hips, and cut the hem into uneven hanging strips so it droops like willow instead of stacking like shelves.
3. The whole set is one mid grey and has none of the brief's colours. Bring in a willow green for the overskirt strips, a black-water sash at the waist, and a cold pale-blue trim at the hem or neckline.
