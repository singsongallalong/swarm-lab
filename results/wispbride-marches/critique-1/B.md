# B

Keep: the pale mist body with the willow-green front panel and the thin strands hanging from the headpiece down the back. That is a calm, bridal palette that belongs to the fen.

1. The shoulders are one flat slab that runs well past the arms. From above it is the biggest shape on the figure and makes a broad box, not a woman. Cut the mantle back so nothing sits past the shoulder edge, and let the pale cloth fall from the neckline as a narrow capelet or veil instead.
2. The skirt drops straight in stacked layers and reads as a block from above. Fit the waist tighter with a darker sash and widen each tier going down so the hem is clearly broader than the hips, ending at the shins.
3. Push the cold wisp blue somewhere it reads from above: a pale-blue edge on the hem or on the veil strands' tips. At the moment the palette is all grey and green, and the glow idea from the brief is missing.
