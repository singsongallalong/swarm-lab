# D: notes for the rework

Keep: the long crossing, a road straight over broken floes toward a gate. It has depth and a clear direction.

1. The eye-level whole-map camera in seed 1 sits inside something and shows a blank grey frame, and "the ice spires" is filled by a dark slab at the lens. Fix both: move the spires camera onto the lake about 25 cells from the nearest spire, lifted 10. Look up past one spire in the foreground to the cluster behind it.
2. The lake is a round bowl with a ring of shore and sprinkled dark patches, too close to the moor. Give it height: break the floes into slabs tilted at different heights, and make the western plateau a real cliff above the lake rather than a flat strip. Have the road climb from the ice up onto the plateau.
3. "The last floe" ruin is good but sits on a plain block. Set it on a floe broken away from the shore with a dark water gap all round, and frame it from the near shore with the gap in the foreground. Put the under-ice light in that gap.


Across the whole run, the critic also noted: No candidate has a store-page photo: every set has at least one camera jammed against a slab, and the seeds of each map barely differ. Four of six reuse the same faint under-ice line as their effect, and nobody built a single tall subject (a tower or arch) that the best shot is framed around.
