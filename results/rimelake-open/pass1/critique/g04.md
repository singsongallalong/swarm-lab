# B: notes for the rework

Keep: the crater. Concentric terraces stepping down to a spired isle is a shape of its own, and "the crater from the rim" is the best idea in your set.

1. Make "the crater from the rim" the hero shot and give it a foreground. Today the frame is mostly flat terrace grey, and the spire is small and centred. Put the camera on the rim lip with a broken parapet or a leaning stone filling the near left third. Lift it about 10 and tilt down so the terraces fall away as stacked bands, and let the spire break the horizon line.
2. The aurora curtain cannot be seen from the game camera: both game-camera frames are black. Bring it down so its lower hem hangs inside the crater, just above the isle, and in view from pitch 50. Let it light the spire, instead of hanging above where nobody looks.
3. The frozen falls shot and the seed-1 eye-level shot both look into a pale slab or box close up. Remove the white building at the road's entry, or turn it into a ruin you can see past. Stand the falls camera far enough back (about 25 cells) to see the full drop between two terraces. The seeds are also near-identical: vary the number of ramps cutting the rings and where the isle sits.
