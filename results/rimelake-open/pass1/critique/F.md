# F: notes for the rework

Keep: the dead trees. "The frozen crown" and "the drowned road" have the gothic mood this game wants, with a road curling through black, twisted woods.

1. The map reads as the moor repainted: an oval bowl, a band of shore, a winding road, props sprinkled evenly. Give it a shape of its own. Sink the drowned road below the ice level so it runs in a cut with banks either side. Raise the ring of stones on a knoll the road must climb to.
2. The eye-level whole-map camera sits inside a dark block in seeds 1 and 3, and the landmark eye-level frame has a tree trunk at the lens. Move those cameras at least 10 cells clear of any trunk or block.
3. "The frozen crown" is your best frame. Lift it about 10 more and pull it back so the road's full S-curve shows and the ring of stones sits at the end of it. Thin the trees near the camera to two or three big silhouettes in the foreground. The under-ice light is a single thin line: run it along the drowned road under the ice so the road glows from beneath.
