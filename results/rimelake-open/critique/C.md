# C: notes for the rework

Keep: the dead trees. Twisted black trees against pale frozen ground are the strongest silhouette in this map, and "the drowned road" seed 2 starts to look like a place.

1. This is the moor repainted: an oval bowl, a band of ground round it, a sine-wave road and things sprinkled everywhere. The road is the same S-curve on all three seeds. Give the ground real height. Let the road climb onto a raised causeway or ridge above sunken ice, or drop through a cut, and let the trees crowd one low hollow instead of covering the whole field.
2. The shots have no subject. "The king on the ice" is a field of standing stones receding into nothing, and "the frozen dead" looks down at scattered stumps. Pick one landmark and frame it. Set the camera low on the road 30 cells back, looking past two near trees in the foreground, with the king or the landmark against the skyline behind. The landmark close-up is a plain checkered slope with a band through it: give it a structure that rises.
3. The under-ice light is a thin line floating in black, with no context. Run it under the actual frozen ground along the drowned road, visible through the ice from the game camera, so it reads as something moving beneath the player's feet.
