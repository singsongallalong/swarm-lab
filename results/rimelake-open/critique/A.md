# A: notes for the rework

Keep: the terraced crater. The rings of ice-steps going down to a frozen pool, cut by spoke causeways, is a real shape of its own, and "the crater from the rim" is the closest thing to a photograph here.

1. Your aurora can't be seen from the game camera: both game-camera frames (t 20 s and 24 s) are plain black. Bring the curtain down and into the frame, so it hangs low over the crater's far rim and shows behind the spire at pitch 50, and let its light touch the pool. Right now it's decoration nobody will ever see.
2. The three seeds are the same map. The terraces, the five spokes and the central pool sit in the same places every time. Let the seed move the pool off-centre, change how many spokes there are and where they run, and break one or two terrace rings into a fallen slope, so each seed reads as a different descent into the same crater.
3. "The frozen falls" shot is a dark band of wall with a strip of sky, with no subject and no depth. Stand the camera on a lower terrace across the crater and look up at the falls, with a spoke causeway leading the eye to them. Then the falls tower over the frame instead of filling it. The eye-level road views are also slabs filling the lower half: lower the near terrace edges along the road, or step them, so the road looks down into the bowl.
