# D: wind-carved yardang field

Keep: the fins are now near-black, fewer and shaped (seed 3 "the field from the south-east" is a single prow falling to a long tail with the arch on its crest), and the crest-wind set piece streams in one direction.

1. The flanks are still a staircase of identical cubes; from "the arch on the crest" they read as stacked crates. Larger steps (fewer, taller) on the prow and a smooth taper to the tail would read as wind-cut rock.
2. The bright snow line is broken into separate pale squares on most crests; make it one unbroken pale ridge so the fin has a lit edge from the game camera.
3. From the game camera (seeds 1 and 3) a black wall fills half the frame with nothing on it; frame the camera on the tail and the road, with the prow behind, or lower the fins nearest the south edge.

Last round: answered the grey-on-grey (granite now near-black) and fewer fins with prow and tail; partly answered the spindrift (a long streaming streak in the set piece); not answered the bleacher rhythm of the flanks.
