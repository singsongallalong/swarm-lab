# A: glacier tongue over a granite ledge

Keep: the slab-lintel cave mouth with its dark opening and crossed timbers, now framed by tall seracs in "the pour over the cave mouth" seeds 2 and 3; and the meltwater set piece, now a dense bright column of streaks.

1. There is still no glowing black pool: the pool at the cave mouth is a flat grey-green patch with a ring of cyan cubes, the same value as the snow. Make it the darkest thing in the frame with the light around its rim, so the eye lands on the cave.
2. The ice itself is a city of near-identical grey-green boxes; from the game camera (all three seeds) it reads as rooftops, not a pour. Cut the box count by half and give the tongue one continuous top edge stepping down to the cave.
3. The seeds sheet is one rectangle-in-a-notch with a T road in every seed; let some seeds have the road pass under or across the foot of the pour instead of beside the box.

Last round: answered the seracs framing the cave (taller, grouped round the lintel) and the meltwater (now strong in the set piece); not answered the glowing pool or the road crossing the foot, and the "road under the ice" shots still show only a dark slab in front of distant boxes.
