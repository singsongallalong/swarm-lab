# G: barrow field with leaking doors

Keep: the bright teal light fans spilling across the snow, now in every seed and every game-camera shot, and the winding road between mounds.

1. The barrows are still stacked grey block piles; in "the lit door" shots they read as a wall of crates with a single cube on it. Lower them to two or three broad steps and make the great barrow clearly the biggest.

3. Put the door itself in the photo: "the lit door" should look straight at one granite door with its fan running toward the camera.
