# B: crevasse with a lashed-timber bridge

Keep: the timber plank bridge with rope rails across a cut is the most instantly readable subject in the run, and "the crossing from the south" is a good photo.

1. The crevasse floor is still a checkerboard of blue-grey squares; fewer, deeper drops to one dark floor would make it read as a fall, not paving.
2. The glow along the bottom is a handful of tiny cyan dots; make it a continuous pale strip or line of lights along the floor so the drop has depth and night mood in the photos.
3. Bridge length barely varies on the seeds sheet; make some seeds a short hop over a narrow cut and others a long span over a wide one, and vary the bend more (several seeds are the same diagonal slash).

Last round: partly answered the glow (a few lights now sit on the floor, too small to read) and the snow-pour placement (only judgeable in the set piece); not answered the checkerboard floor or the bridge-length variety, which still look much the same seed to seed.
