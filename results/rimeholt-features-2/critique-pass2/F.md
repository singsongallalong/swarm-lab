# F: frozen burial longship

Keep: the tilted hull with ribs and mast is the strongest single subject in the run; "the wreck from the ice" reads at once as a ship caught in a lake.

1. The hull glow is now broad and soft, but it spreads as a wide cloud over the whole lake rather than pooling under the keel; tighten it to a line under the hull so it outlines the ship from above.
2. From the game camera the mast and spars become a tangle of black lines (seed 2) over speckled ground; widen the pale ice collar right around the hull and calm the stippled lake surface so the ship separates.
3. The shots often frame the ship small against a busy middle ground of slabs; bring the camera closer or clear the foreground blocks so the ship fills the frame.

Last round: answered the glow (no longer a pipe; now a soft broad field) and the ship states (seeds show whole hulls, broken hulls and rib-only wrecks); partly answered the ice collar; not answered the lake stipple, which still reads as noise from the top.
