# A: glacier tongue over a granite ledge

Keep: the dark cave mouth set into the notch under the slab lintel; it is now visible in "the pour over the cave mouth" shots and is the piece worth walking to.

1. The tongue is still one flat rectangle of pale ice with a carpet of small boxes on it; from the game camera it reads as a raised platform, not a frozen pour. Make three or four seracs much taller than the rest and drop them in big clear steps to the cave, with bare dark granite around them so the ice has a silhouette.
2. The meltwater is now a column of streaks rather than drips, but it is faint and there is no glowing black pool in any photograph; put a pool that glows right at the cave mouth so the eye lands there.
3. Every seed still puts the ice in a box with the road wrapping its rectangle; let at least some seeds have the road cross the foot of the pour or pass under the cave lip so the layouts differ in kind.

Last round: answered the move off the north wall (pours now come from top, left and right edges) and partly the meltwater (a streaky column, still weak); not answered the subject point (the seracs remain a field of near-identical boxes and the cave is small in frame).
