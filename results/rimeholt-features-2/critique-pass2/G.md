# G: barrow field with leaking doors

Keep: the road now winds between separate mounds, and "down the winding way" has a real path leading the eye into the field.

1. The doors' light is still a single small cyan cube; there is no pale fan on the snow in any shot. The fan is the whole idea: make it a wide, bright wedge of ground spilling from each door toward the road.
2. The barrows read as stacked grey block piles, not mounds; lower and broaden them, and make the great barrow clearly bigger than the rest.
3. Hang the grave-marks and cairns along the road edge instead of scattering them, so the winding way is lined.

Last round: answered the separate mounds and the bending road (seeds show several rings of mounds with a road winding between); not answered the light fan, so the photographs still have no lit subject.
