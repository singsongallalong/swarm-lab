# D: wind-carved yardang field

Keep: the seeds really do differ in fin count, angle and how the road threads between them, and the arch on the crest gives the shot a landmark.

1. The fins still read as bleachers: rows of regular dark and pale squares in a stepped grid. They need much more height at the prow and a long low tail, and far fewer of them, so each fin is a shape rather than a pattern.
2. Grey on grey: at game camera the fins vanish into the snow and the props (shrubs, rocks) outnumber the feature. Make the granite near-black and leave only bright snow on the crests.
3. The spindrift is a few tiny flecks at game camera, invisible against the night; make longer, brighter streaks peeling off each crest.

Last round: partly answered the striping (pale crest squares now appear) and the arch shot improved; not answered the bleacher rhythm, the grey-on-grey at game camera, or the spindrift, which is still near invisible.
