# F: frozen burial longship

Keep: the tilted hull, ribs and mast are the strongest single subject of any candidate; the "wreck from the ice" shots read at once as a ship in a lake.

1. The hull-glow set piece is a single thin curved tube with zigzag marks and reads like a pipe or cable; make it a soft broad glow pooling along the keel line beneath the hull instead.
2. From the game camera the mast and spars become scribbled black lines over speckled ground; give the ship a pale ice collar around the hull so it separates, and calm the stipple of the lake surface.
3. Seeds vary the ship's position and heading, but a few lay the glow in loose loops that read as noise from the top; keep the glow tied to the hull and vary instead the ship's state (bow up, broken in two, only ribs).
