# B: crevasse with a lashed-timber bridge

Keep: the plank bridge across a stepped cut is a clear, instantly readable subject, and the crevasse's angle and bend change seed to seed.

1. The crevasse floor reads as a checkerboard of grey-blue squares; make the terraces fewer and deeper toward one dark, glowing bottom so it reads as a drop rather than paving.
2. Bring the "breathing cold glow" into the photographs: right now nothing at the bottom glows, so the shot is grey timber over grey ice. A line of cold light along the floor would give it depth and night mood.
3. The snow-pour set piece is a good streaky sheet; place it on the lip next to the bridge so the bridge shot and the effect frame together, and vary bridge length (some seeds a short hop, some a long span).
