# E: frozen geyser basin

Keep: dark vents crowned with ice teeth in terraced blue ice, plus the frost-steam column, add up to the most complete new place in the run; seeds scatter 3-5 vents in different clusters with the road finding its own way.

1. The ground between vents is so crowded with spikes and shard cones that there is little clean floor to fight on; thin the cones to the vent rims and leave open ice between them.
2. From the game camera the terraces read as many small flat blue steps; fewer, larger terraces stepping down into each vent would read as a basin.
3. Let the cracks between vents glow brighter so the vents connect visually in the photo and lead the eye down the road.
