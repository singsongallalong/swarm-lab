# C: aurora shaft ringed by runestones

Keep: the aurora column set piece is the strongest effect in the run, and the stepped teal shaft with its leaning spire makes a real vertical subject.

1. Every seed is one round well with a road around or into it; the shaft changes size and position but not shape. Try an elongated or broken rim, a second smaller well, or a road that drops in on one side and climbs out the other.
2. The teal rim is a solid flat band from every angle and flattens the shaft from the game camera; break it with darker steps and let the light come from the floor upward.
3. The runestones and spire are scattered rather than composed: ring the lip with them at even-ish spacing so the shot from the snow has a foreground frame.
