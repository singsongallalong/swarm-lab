# D: wind-carved yardang field

Keep: the seeds really do differ (fin counts, angles and how the road threads between them), and the arch on a crest gives the field a landmark.

1. The fins read as grandstand bleachers: regular stepped rows of dark boxes. Taper them along their length (high blunt head, long low tail) and break the step rhythm so they read as rock sheared by wind.
2. Everything is grey on grey; at game camera the fins blur into the snow. Darken the granite and stripe crests with bright packed snow so the shapes separate.
3. The spindrift set piece is a muddy smear; make it thin bright streaks peeling off each crest in one direction.
