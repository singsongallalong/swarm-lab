# G: barrow field with leaking doors

Keep: the idea of each barrow door leaking cold light down the snow is atmospheric, and the wisp set piece rising from a threshold is pretty.

1. The barrows don't read: from every shot they are dark flat terraces, and the seeds show the same single dark oval barrow mass crossed by the road, jittered. Build several separate, distinct mounds of different sizes with clear doorways facing the road.
2. The photographs are almost black with only tiny cyan squares; light spilling from the doors needs to be a large pale fan on the snow so the shot has a subject.
3. Give the road a reason to bend between barrows instead of crossing the field in a straight plus.
