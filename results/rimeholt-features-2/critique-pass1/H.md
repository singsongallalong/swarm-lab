# H: open-air ice cathedral

Keep: two rows of tall ice columns leading to an altar under an arch make a strong axial composition in "down the nave".

1. All twelve seeds are the same plan: the nave aligned with the north-south road, columns in the same two rows. Rotate the nave, let it sit beside the road, ruin different columns per seed, collapse part of a row.
2. The trench cut along the road beside the nave is a dark slot that dominates the game-camera shot; remove it or make it the nave floor.
3. The frost-shaft set piece is a faint smear with a dot; make tall pale shafts of light between the columns, which is what would make this a cathedral at night.
