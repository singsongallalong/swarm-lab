# E: Basaltplate Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

The best of the wave. The red seams are visible from the ARPG camera, and the raked horn/shard rising off the helm is a single, readable silhouette idea that no other family has.

1. **Make the horn read as basalt, not bone or iron.** Its pale grey tip edges toward the Reaver bone sets and toward iron. Make it a dark, faceted shard with a red seam at its root where it breaks from the helm.
2. **Lose the yellow eye slit and the small pale square on the chest.** Both draw the eye in the wrong palette. Use a red slit if the face needs a mark.
3. **Weight the red toward cracks between slabs.** Right now some red outlines whole blocks like trim; a few seams across the chest face (and fewer edge outlines) would sell "the mountain's body".
4. Fit holds on the slim avatar. Check that the shoulder blocks don't swallow the upper arm in the fit three-quarter view.
5. If parts are spare, a smaller echo shard on one pauldron would strengthen the line. Keep the horn dominant.
