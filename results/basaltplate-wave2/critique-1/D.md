# D: Basaltplate Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

The weakest against the brief.

1. **Remove the gold diagonal baldric and the yellow trim lines.** Gold strapping reads as studded or brass, and as a sash rather than plate cut from a mountain. Replace it with red seams between the slabs.
2. **Darken the top plates.** The light grey helm top and pauldron tops are what the ARPG camera sees most, and they read as the iron set. They should be basalt black.
3. **There is no silhouette idea.** It is a standard box helm with stepped pauldrons. Choose one: a slab-stacked helm, a jagged rock pauldron, or a heavy stone collar.
4. The many thin trim strips are parts no one sees from above. Merge them.
5. Fit is fine.
