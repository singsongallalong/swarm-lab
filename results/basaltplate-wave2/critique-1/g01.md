# A: Basaltplate Hood / Cuirass / Gauntlets / Greaves

Second place. The pitched, peaked hood with its slab cowl and the single raked shoulder spur give a real outline, and the darker slabs keep it apart from iron's grey.

1. **Make the seams read from above.** In the ARPG view the red seams almost vanish into the black. Widen two or three of them (between the cuirass slabs, under the hood's brim) so a magma line shows at camera height. Keep it as colour, not Neon.
2. **Commit to the asymmetry.** The lone shoulder spur is the best silhouette beat, but it looks like a stray part. Make it a larger stepped basalt shard rising off one pauldron, and seat it on the plate so it doesn't hover off the edge, as it seems to in the shoulder view.
3. **Lighten the stack of tiny slabs on the chest.** The small layered plates on the front cost parts and turn to noise at distance. Merge them into two or three large slabs and spend the parts on the shard.
4. The pale grey bevel highlights on the hood pull toward iron. Darken them to basalt.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar. Check that the gauntlet slabs don't clip the forearm in the three-quarter view.
