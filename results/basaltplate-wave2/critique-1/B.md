# B: Basaltplate Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

Big shoulders and a tall helm crown give a heavy, golem-like mass, and the red edge seams show better than in A.

1. **Remove the yellow visor band.** It is the brightest thing on the figure from every camera, and it reads as brass, which belongs to studded, not to this set. Make the slit red (a seam glowing through) or plain dark.
2. **Pick one silhouette idea.** The crown peak and the boxy shoulders compete and neither dominates. Either raise the helm into a clear tapered basalt crown or make the pauldrons stepped rock ledges, then simplify the other.
3. The red seams trace outer edges like piping. Run some across the slab faces too (a cracked chest), so they read as seams between plates, not as trim.
4. Fit is fine on both bodies. The boots are plain; one thick slab cuff would tie them to the set.
