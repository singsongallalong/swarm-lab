# C: Basaltplate Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

The idea is right on brief: raw rock outcrops breaking out of the plate. The execution undersells it.

1. **The rock chunks are too small, too pale and look loose.** From the ARPG camera they read as tan debris resting on the helm and shoulders, not as part of the armour. Make them basalt-dark, two or three times larger, and seat them into the pauldrons so they grow out of the plate, with a red seam where rock meets iron.
2. **One outcrop, not a scatter.** Put a single big crag on one shoulder, or a jagged crown on the helm, and drop the rest. Spend the freed parts there.
3. The vertical red lines on the chest are good. Make them thicker so they survive the top-down view.
4. The chest and shoulders are a plain black box that reads like dark iron from above. The outcrop has to carry the family's identity.
5. Check that no chunk hangs off the shoulder edge in the side view.
