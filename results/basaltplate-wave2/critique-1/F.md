# F: Basaltplate Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

Clean, well-fitted and on palette for seams, but it reads as stacked crates.

1. **Give it one silhouette idea.** Everything is a box of the same weight. The helm, a flat-topped bucket, is the obvious place: taper or step it into a slab crown, or give it a heavy jutting brow ledge.
2. **Remove the yellow visor slit.** It reads as brass and studded. Make it red.
3. **Break the slabs.** Rock is irregular; offset or tilt one or two of the shoulder slabs and run a red seam into the gap, so the armour looks cut from stone, not built from boxes.
4. Red edge lines are fine but thin from the ARPG camera. Make one or two of them thicker.
5. Fit is the best of the wave. Keep it while adding shape.
