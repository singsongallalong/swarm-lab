# E: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots

1. **It is too black.** Helm, chest and pauldrons are all near pure black with thin seams, so from the ARPG camera the set is a dark blob with no internal shape. Bring the large plates up to basalt charcoal, keep the deepest black for the seams' shadow side, and widen the red seams so they read.
2. **The horn spikes read as Reaver, not mountain.** The single angled spike on the helm and the small spikes on the pauldrons are horns, which the Reaver sets own. Swap them for a rock form: a jutting slab crest or a broken basalt column.
3. The chest rune/crack (the red T shape) looks drawn on; make it a seam between two chest plates instead.
4. Red belt band is a good anchor; keep it.
5. Fit holds; the helm spike is attached. Close the green hip gap.
