# F: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots

1. **The red outlines on every box edge read as sci-fi trim, not magma.** Seams should sit between plates, in the gaps, not trace each block's rim. Remove most edge outlines and put red only in the gaps between pauldron and chest, chest and fauld, helm and collar.
2. **The cube pauldrons are massive and golem-like, which suits the brief, but they are plain cubes.** Give them a rock idea: offset stacked slabs, a sloped top, a broken corner. As is it reads "black crate shoulders".
3. The helm's stacked bands are nice from the front but read as a small box from above; a wider brow slab would help.
4. Fit holds well on the slim avatar; nothing floats.
5. Close the green hip gap with a short fauld.
