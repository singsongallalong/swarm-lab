# D: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots

Clean, well fitted and the red seams read best of the wave from the ARPG camera (the red undersides of the sloped pauldrons). But it reads as a black-and-red knight, not a mountain.

1. **Give it one rock idea.** The sloped slab pauldrons are smooth and regular, close in outline to iron's broad grey shoulders. Break them: stack two offset slabs, chip a corner, or make one rise as a basalt column. Without that it is "iron in black paint".
2. The barred visor is detail no one sees from above; fine as is, spend no more there.
3. The front fauld/apron leaves a very large green panel at the hips that reads as a gap in heavy plate; extend the slab tassets down to mid-thigh.
4. Keep the red undersides; they are the best seam treatment here.
5. Fit on the slim avatar holds well.
