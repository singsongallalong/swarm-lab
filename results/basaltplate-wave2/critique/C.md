# C: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots

The jagged crag on one shoulder is a real mountain idea and the asymmetry gives an outline no other family has. Good direction.

1. **Make the crag bigger and darker.** It is the only silhouette idea, yet it is roughly head-sized and its lighter brown-grey rocks read as a separate stone from the black armour. Grow it upward (it should break the head line from the ARPG view) and pull its colour to basalt black with red seams between the shards.
2. **The other pauldron and the chest are flat black boxes.** A stepped basalt edge on the plain pauldron, or the vertical chest seams continuing up into the crag, would tie body and outcrop together.
3. The red seams are thin vertical lines; from above they barely read. Widen them or add one horizontal seam across the shoulders.
4. Ridged helm top is a nice touch from above; keep it.
5. Fit holds; nothing cuts the arm. Close the green hip gap with a short fauld.
