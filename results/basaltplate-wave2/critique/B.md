# B: Helm, Chest, Gloves, Boots

Strongest of the wave. The terraced pauldrons (stacked basalt strata, red seams between the layers) are exactly "plates cut from the mountain" and read from the ARPG camera as stepped rock, unlike any reference family.

1. **Push the terraces a step further.** Make each tier visibly narrower than the one below (more stepping), and let the top tier tilt slightly, so the pauldrons read as columnar basalt, not stacked crates. This is the set's one idea; give it the parts.
2. **The chest crack is too thin to read from above.** Thicken the zigzag seam or run it across the shoulders into the pauldron seams, so the red reads as one fissure through the body.
3. The pale grey collar under the helm reads as a light patch from above; darken it to iron grey.
4. The helm is a plain tall block; fine as a quiet partner to the shoulders, but one red seam ring at the brow would tie it in.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar. Big green hip gap front and back: a short slab fauld would close it.
