# A: Hood, Cuirass, Gauntlets, Greaves

1. **The grey trim bands on arms, waist and boots pull it toward iron.** From the ARPG camera the light grey stripes read as polished steel, the iron set's signature. Turn those strips to iron-grey only where they are metal, and swap most of them for the red seam colour so the eye reads "black slab, red crack" not "grey band".
2. **Commit to one silhouette idea.** You have a slanted hood top, a small crag on one shoulder and broad flat slab pauldrons on both. The crag is the best idea but it is small and sits on a plain slab; make it a bigger, stepped outcrop (2-3 rising blocks) and keep the other pauldron plain.
3. The hood's slanted top is nice in side view but vanishes from above; fine to keep, do not spend more parts there.
4. The silver cross on the face and the round chest medallion are invisible from the high camera; spend those parts on the crag.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar; nothing floats. Hip gap shows a lot of green; a short slab fauld at front and back would close it.
