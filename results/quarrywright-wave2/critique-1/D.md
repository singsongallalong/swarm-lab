# D: slate helm, chest, gloves, boots

1. **This reads as the iron set.** Charcoal-slate plate with pale trims and square pauldrons, from pitch 50, is the iron reference with a different trim colour. The brief's dominant colour is dust-white; slate is meant to be the one accent. Invert it: pale stone body, slate on one feature.
2. **No quarry or funeral story.** A cross seam on the chest and round knobs on the shoulders could be any knight. If the knobs are meant as skulls or stone pegs, make them big enough to read, or replace with tools (wedges, chisels) set into the pauldrons like pegs in a quarry face.
3. The pale-capped shoulders are the only thing that reads from above; that is a start, but there is no silhouette idea. Pick one: a stone-block helm, a yoke, a slung pick.
4. Fit is the cleanest of the batch: tight to the body, holds on the slim avatar, nothing floats. Keep that discipline through the rework.
