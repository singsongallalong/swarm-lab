# F: stone mask, mantle, bands, wraps

1. **Hang the tools.** The brief says tool-hung and this set carries nothing. One mallet or chisel at the belt, or a pick across the back, finishes the quarryman read; this is the only gap in the fantasy.
2. The stone-block mask with carved eye and mouth slots is the best single idea of the batch: it is the abandoned colossus's face on a dead worker. Push it: slightly larger than the head, a chipped corner, the face carved a touch deeper so the slots read at pitch 50.
3. Rust-iron mantle on one shoulder against a pale stone pauldron on the other is a good asymmetric read from above and puts rusted iron exactly where the eye lands. Keep it.
4. The black upper arms are the strongest dark mass; at a glance they echo iron. Make them the cold slate (bluer, a step lighter) rather than near-black, and keep them that one accent.
5. Warm pale stone is close to linen's oatmeal in the haze; cool it a little toward grey. Fit holds on the slim avatar, nothing floats or cuts.
