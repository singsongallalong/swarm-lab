# E: niche hood, dust tunic, mason grips, cutter boots

1. **Fix the hood's proportion first.** The niche hood is a tall wide box that makes the head the biggest block on the figure; from the side it reads like a crate on the shoulders. Bring it in a little and shape the front as an arched niche opening (a stepped top lip over the face slot), so it says "burial niche" rather than "box".
2. The palette is the closest to the brief: dust-white body, one cold dark slate on the shoulder slabs, rust specks. Keep it. The slate slabs read well from the ARPG camera and separate it from linen and iron.
3. Tool-hung works: the mallet hanging at the belt is right. Make it a touch larger, and hang a second tool (chisel or wedge) on the other hip so it reads as a habit, not one item.
4. The many thin pale stripes on arms and legs blur into noise from above; merge them into two or three broad bands and spend saved parts on the hood shaping.
5. Back: the small slate square on the back floats off-centre and reads as a stray part; make it a grave tag on a cord or remove it.
Fit holds on the slim avatar.
