# A: dustmask, yoke, handwraps, treads

1. **Linen risk is the first fix.** From the ARPG camera the whole figure is warm pale banded wraps with thin slate stripes: at a glance it is the linen set wrapped tighter. Cool the pale toward stone grey (less oatmeal), and make the slate bands fewer and thicker so the read is "stone and iron" not "bandage".
2. **Give it one silhouette idea.** The shoulders are big bandaged blocks and the head a banded box; nothing breaks the outline. Spend parts on one thing a quarryman would carry: a carrying yoke across the shoulders that actually sticks out past them (the item is called Yoke, but no yoke reads), or a pick/mallet slung on the back at an angle.
3. The crossed chest straps and belt tools are the right fantasy (tool-hung, dusty dead labourer) but they are too small to survive pitch 50: thicken the X straps or drop them for the yoke.
4. Mask: the slate eye-band with dust-white cap is good, keep it.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar, nothing floats. The arm banding is stacked so densely it reads as noise in the back view; cut a few bands and spend those parts on the yoke.
