# C: brimmed helm, pick tabard, gloves, boots

1. **Dark sleeves pull it toward iron.** The charcoal upper arms and back plate are the heaviest mass from the ARPG camera, and with the grey hat the figure reads iron-with-a-tabard. Turn the arms pale dust grey and keep slate to one band per arm, so the pale tabard and hat own the read.
2. **Funerary cue missing.** The pick emblem and rust scraps say "quarry guard", which is half the brief. Add one burial note: a linen grave-wrap down the tabard edge, or a niche-shaped (arched) front to the tabard.
3. The brimmed helm with a pick handle sticking up is a nice invention, but the handle is a thin fin that vanishes from above; make it a real pick head crossing the crown, which would be the strongest single idea in the set.
4. Rust-coloured scraps on the tabard and belt are on palette; group them so they read as rust staining, not confetti.
5. Boots: pale studded cuffs look good. Fit holds on the slim avatar; the tabard sits flat to the torso with no gaps.
