# B: brimmed helm, strapped chest, gloves, boots

1. **The fantasy is an overseer in a sun hat, not a dead quarryman.** Nothing is funerary: no wrap, no niche, no burial. Add one death cue: a dust-white cloth hanging from the brim over the face (veil or dust-rag), or a grave-tag / name-plate on the chest.
2. **Grey too even.** Mid-grey tunic, grey hat, grey gloves: at pitch 50 it sits next to iron, only lighter. Push the tunic to stone-dust white and put the one cold dark slate in a single place (the hat crown or a sash), not as thin stripes.
3. The brimmed hat is a real silhouette idea and reads from above; make the brim wider and flatter so it is the whole outline, and lose the little dark patch on the crown.
4. Exposed yellow face is fine, but the neck shows a bright band between hat and collar in side view: close it with a collar or the veil.
5. Belt pouches are good tool-hung detail; hang a chisel or mallet off one so they read as quarry tools, not generic satchels.
Fit is clean on both bodies.
