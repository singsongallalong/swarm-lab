# F: Mask / Mantle / Bands / Wraps

The closest to iron of the six. Grey grilled helm, dark slate arms, broad shoulders: from the ARPG camera the outline and most of the colour say iron, with one rust shoulder as the only difference.

1. **Lighten the whole set toward dust white.** The dark slate arms and torso pull it into iron. Keep slate for one accent only.
2. **The rust mantle is the right idea, too small.** Make it a real mantle: a rust-and-linen cape or burial shroud draped over both shoulders and down the back, so the outline changes.
3. **The grilled mask reads as a knight's visor.** A quarryman's dust wrap or a carved stone death mask would serve the fantasy better.
4. **The hammer at the hip** is good; make it bigger and hang a second tool.

Fit: holds on the slim avatar and sits on the body. The shoulder pieces are lopsided (rust on one side only); if that asymmetry is meant, make it bolder so it reads as intended.
