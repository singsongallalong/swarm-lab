# A: Dustmask / Yoke / Handwraps / Treads

The best silhouette of the six. The carrying yoke over the shoulders is the one strong idea and it reads at once from the ARPG camera: a dark wooden bar across a pale body that no reference family has. Keep it.

1. **Give the yoke more weight in the outline.** From pitch 50 the beam reads, but the hanging hooks and tools at its ends are tiny. Hang one or two clear loads (a bucket, a chisel bundle, a coil of rope) at the ends so it reads as a quarryman's burden and not just a stick.
2. **Push the funerary note.** Right now it reads as a working labourer. A linen burial wrap trailing from the yoke, or a small slate grave-tag hanging from the mask, would make him a dead quarryman.
3. **Body colour sits close to iron.** Pale grey with slate bands is near the iron set once the yoke is hidden (side and back views). Warm the torso toward stone-dust white and use rust, not slate, for the bands.
4. **The dust mask is lost** under the helm from above. Make the mask cloth paler and larger so the face reads as wrapped.

Fit: holds on the slim avatar; the yoke rests on the shoulders and clears the head. Nothing floating.
