# B: Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

Strong colour: the most dust-white set here, with a slate flat-brimmed cap that reads as a clear dark mark on top from the ARPG camera.

1. **Linen risk.** A pale, wrapped, strapped body is close to the linen family (pale oatmeal). Push the colour colder (stone white with a grey cast, not cream) and let the slate cap and rusted iron carry more of the set, for example rusted iron bands at the wrists and shins.
2. **The crossed straps are too thin** to read from above. Make one broad bandolier, or hang a real tool from it (a pick head over the back, a mallet at the hip) so the set is visibly tool-hung.
3. **The cap is the idea; make it bigger.** A wider brim, or a stone-cutter's slate hood that drops past the shoulders, would give the outline the one shape it lacks. Right now the body is a plain box.
4. **Funerary note is missing.** Add a burial element: a face wrap under the cap, or a niche-tablet on the chest.

Fit: holds on the slim avatar. The small grey square on the back reads as a stray part; attach it to something or remove it.
