# C: Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

The parts are tidy, but the set has no single idea that reads from above. At pitch 50 it is a pale grey box with a hat, very close to D and E.

1. **Cover the face.** The open yellow face under the brim breaks the funerary read and makes the head look unfinished. A dust wrap or a slate death mask would fix the fantasy and the colour at once.
2. **Pick one silhouette idea and spend parts on it.** The small hammer emblem on the chest and the tiny rust patches don't read from the game camera. Move those parts into one big shape: a pick over the back, a tall stacked brim, or a heavy apron.
3. **Back reads as iron.** The back and the shoulder view are dark grey and broad, which is the iron family. Carry the pale dust colour around the back.
4. **The rope belt is good;** thicken it and let rusted tools hang from it.

Fit: sits on the body; holds on the slim avatar. The flat plate on top of the hat looks separate from the crown; close the gap.
