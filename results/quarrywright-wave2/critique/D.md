# D: Helm / Chest / Gloves / Boots

A clear idea: the dark slate box helm on a pale, heavy body. From the ARPG camera the dark block on top reads well and is distinct, and the skull charm at the hip is the only real funerary note in the wave.

1. **Make the helm read as a burial niche, not a bucket.** Give it a stepped or arched lintel and a pale stone frame round the face slot, so the slate block reads as a tomb cut in the quarry wall.
2. **The body is broad, pale grey and plain**, which risks iron. Dust the colour whiter, add linen wrapping at the arms or a hanging burial cloth, and keep the slate only on the helm.
3. **Tools are too small.** The hanging chisel and skull are good ideas but read as specks. Make one of them big: a mallet at the hip or a chain of grave-tags across the chest.
4. **The rust rivets on the pauldrons** are a nice touch; group them into a band so they read from above.

Fit: the best fit of the six. Everything sits on the body and holds on the slim avatar.
