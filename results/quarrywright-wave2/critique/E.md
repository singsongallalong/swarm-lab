# E: Nichehood / Dusttunic / Masongrips / Cutterboots

Good names and a good idea (a hood cut like a burial niche), but the render reads as a grey bucket helm with dark pauldrons: the iron family's outline.

1. **The niche hood needs to be a hood.** It is a square helm with a visible yellow face slot. Make it hang: a cowl falling onto the shoulders with an arched opening, pale dust over a dark slate lining, and cover the face.
2. **Slate angled pauldrons read like iron plate.** Swap them for something a quarryman wears: a leather or linen shoulder pad for carrying stone, rust-coloured.
3. **The bar across the chest** reads as a stray stick from the front. Turn it into a strap that holds a real tool, or drop it.
4. **Spend parts on the outline,** not on the many thin bands on the arms, which vanish from above.

Fit: sits on the body and holds on the slim avatar; nothing floats.
