# C: critique

This reads as a stack of boxes, not a gown. The skirt is a straight column of stepped tiers that never widens past the hips, so there is no flare or bell, and from the ARPG camera it is a squat armoured block.

1. **Skirt (most important).** Rebuild the lower half as a flared bell: angled panels hinged at the hip and splayed outward, at least 1.5x the hip width at the hem. Today the hem is narrower than the sleeves.
2. **Sleeves.** These are full-width pale blue arm blocks with heavy cuffs, which is exactly the broad, bulky look the brief rules out. Slim them.
3. **Back.** The back is all dark slate and will vanish at night. Bring the light grey and white round the back.
4. **Zone.** There is no wind and no flight in it, and the helm is a small grey box with a fin. Give it wings or a swept crest, and add a streamer or a lifted hem.
5. The chevron chest mark is a good small motif. Keep it.
