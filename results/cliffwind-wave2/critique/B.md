# B: critique

A strong second. The white, ragged, wind-cut skirt flares hard from the hip with blue edge trim, and the helm has white wing ears. From above the skirt is the brightest shape on the field and reads as "dress" right away.

1. **Sleeves (most important).** Dark navy full-arm blocks with stacked cuffs. They are the widest and darkest masses on the figure, they frame the bodice like pauldrons, and from above the figure looks broad-shouldered and armoured, not feminine. Slim them and lighten them to sky blue or steel.
2. **The skirt panels look broken, not windblown.** The shards splay in every direction, so the hem reads as torn glass. Sweep them all one way, leeward, so the wind has a direction, and close the gaps at the hip.
3. **Bodice and back.** The flat grey chest and back slab are dull. Add a sky-blue bodice panel or a laced V, plus a warm tan belt in place of the thin blue line.
4. **Parts.** Many small shard parts go into the hem. Fewer, longer panels would read better from the high camera.
