# E: critique

A modest, neat robe with a tan chest plaque, pale sleeves, a white-grey skirt and a small feather helm. From the ARPG camera it reads as a light tunic. The skirt is too narrow to say "gown", and the back is all dark slate.

1. **Skirt (most important).** The flare starts too low and stays too narrow. Hinge the panels at the hip and splay them to about 1.5x the hip width. Right now the hem barely clears the sleeves.
2. **Sleeves.** These are full-width pale arm blocks, the broad, bulky look again. Slim them.
3. **The back vanishes.** The back and the top-down view are dark grey. Bring cloud white or sky blue round the back, because the overhead camera sees mostly the back and the shoulders.
4. **The chest plaque** reads as a crate or leather patch. Either make it a steel sun or wind emblem, or drop it.
5. **Helm.** The feather is a nice touch but tiny. Make it bigger and white so it reads from above.
