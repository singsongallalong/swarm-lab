# F: critique

There is real zone invention here: a wind streamer blowing off the helm and the horn-like twin spires. But the skirt is a stepped stone ziggurat, and it reads as a plinth or a cake, not a dress.

1. **Skirt (most important).** Replace the stacked rectangular tiers with angled panels that flare from the hip. The current hem is a flat, square step pyramid with no flow and no femininity, and from above it looks like the figure stands on a pedestal.
2. **Sleeves.** These are full-width pale blue arm blocks, and with the boxy bodice the upper body looks very broad. Slim them.
3. **Keep the streamer.** It is the only element on the whole sheet that says wind on a ridge. Lengthen it, make it cloud white instead of steel blue so it reads, and consider a second streamer trailing from the waist.
4. **Back.** The back is dark grey and loses the figure at night.
5. **Parts.** The tiers spend many parts on horizontal bands that add nothing to the outline.
