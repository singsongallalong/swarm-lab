# A: critique

Best gown on the sheet. A true A-line bell from the hip in pale sky-blue steel, with a bow/wing helm. From the ARPG camera it reads as a dress on outline alone, and it is clearly apart from iron, leather and studded.

1. **Sleeves (most important).** The sleeves are still the full arm block with a cuff and a shoulder cap. From above, the two pale blue boxes are as wide as the bodice, and the top-down view reads square and broad. Inset the sleeve panels, drop the shoulder caps, and use a narrower, darker steel bracer so the waist and the skirt bell lead.
2. **Back and side are a flat dark grey slab.** At night the back half disappears. Carry the pale blue and cloud-white panels round the back of the skirt, or add a white back panel or train that the wind lifts.
3. **Zone spirit.** Nothing moves yet. One or two hem panels kicked out to one side, or a short streamer from the helm, would sell the wind on a ridge. Add a warm rock-tan belt or hem trim: the palette is missing tan.
4. **Helm.** The bow wings are pretty but small and grey from above. Make them whiter and a touch larger.
5. **Parts.** The silhouette is economical. Spend the parts you save from the sleeves on the wind kick.
