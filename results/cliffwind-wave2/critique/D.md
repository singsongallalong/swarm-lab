# D: critique

Pale, pretty colours with a sky-blue robe, steel panels and a white plume crest. It reads as a light robe or tabard more than a flared gown, and the split front panels hang straight.

1. **Skirt flare (most important).** The skirt falls straight from the waist in narrow panels, with no bell. Splay the panels outward from the hip into a real A-line, and make it continuous so the open legs don't show between panels.
2. **Sleeves.** The arms are full blocks, and the broad white cuff rings stick out further, so from above the white rings make the widest line on the body. Drop the rings or move them inside the arm, and slim the sleeve.
3. **Back.** The back is a dark grey tabard and will vanish at night. Carry the sky blue round.
4. **Helm.** The plume is a good idea and reads from above. Push it higher and whiter, and sweep it back like wind.
5. Add rock tan in the belt or trim. The brown boots are a good start.
