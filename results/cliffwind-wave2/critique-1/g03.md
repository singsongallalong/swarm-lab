# E - critique

**Most important fix: this reads as lamellar armour, not a gown.** The skirt is a stack of short horizontal tiers that ends above the knee, with dark legs showing beneath. From the high camera it looks like a soldier's tassets. Replace the tiers with a long skirt that flares from the hip and reaches the ankle. Keep one or two plate bands over it if you want the "armoured" read.

Second: the chest and upper arms are shoulder-width boxes. Slim the sleeves and narrow the bodice.

Other notes:
- The single straight plume spike on the helm reads as a knight's spike. A swept or curved crest would be more feminine and windy.
- The palette (steel blue, slate) is cold and uniform. Add cloud white and a warm tan accent so it doesn't sink into the night.
- The back view is almost all dark slate.
- Many parts go into the horizontal tier lines, which vanish from above. Spend them on the skirt outline instead.
