# A - critique

**Most important fix: slim the sleeves.** The upper arms are still full shoulder-width blocks in steel blue; from the ARPG camera they widen the torso into a box and fight the bell below. Narrow each sleeve to roughly half its current width, tuck it against the bodice, and let a small cuff or wrist flare carry the "wind" idea instead.

What works: this is the only candidate with a real bell from the hip. The floor-length pale-blue skirt opens into a clear A-line in front, side and three-quarter, and survives the slim (fit) avatar. From above it reads as a gown at once, and nothing like iron/leather/studded.

Other notes:
- The skirt is a single dark-to-pale blue mass. Add one cloud-white or warm tan band at the hem (or a split front panel) so the wind-caught flare reads, and to lift it off the night ground.
- The back view goes almost all slate; carry a lighter panel down the back of the skirt.
- The helm's small crown/crest is pretty but tiny from above. One swept-back wing or crest pointing with the wind would give the family a signature.
- The bodice is plain; a narrow tan waist belt would mark the hip where the bell starts.
- Parts look well spent on the silhouette. Don't add trim at the cost of the bell.
