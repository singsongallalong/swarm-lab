# C - critique

**Most important fix: remove the shoulder slab.** The bodice is a wide pale block that sits higher and wider than the shoulders, which is exactly the broad, bulky read the brief forbids. From the ARPG camera it reads as a crate on a body. Rebuild the bodice to the torso width, with slim sleeves.

Second: there is no skirt flare. The lower body is a straight grey column with a blue band at the hips, so it reads as trousers or tassets rather than a gown. Add a bell that starts at the hip and reaches the ankle.

Other notes:
- The helm is a yellow-topped bucket with a ring handle. It reads as a lantern or pail, not a valkyrie headpiece, and the yellow fights the palette. Try a swept crest or circlet in steel and white.
- The palette (white, steel blue) is right. Keep it once the shape is fixed.
- The back view is flat slate. Add a skirt panel or train.
- With the current shapes, parts aren't building a silhouette. Spend them on the skirt first.
