# B - critique

**Most important fix: slim the sleeves.** The white/grey upper-arm blocks are as wide as the shoulders and are the brightest thing on the figure, so from above the set reads as broad white pauldrons, not a dress. Halve their width and darken them a step toward steel so the eye goes to the skirt.

What works: the cloud-white palette with steel-blue trim really feels like the zone and stands out from every reference family. The layered, split-panel skirt has some movement and a blue hem edge, and the helm's swept ears/wings point toward a cliff-valkyrie idea.

Other notes:
- The skirt flares only near the hem and stops around the shin. Start the flare at the hip and run it to the ankle so it reads as a bell, not a tasset fringe.
- The back is a dark slate block with a thin skirt; the white disappears. Put white panels on the back of the skirt too.
- On the slim avatar the skirt panels separate and the legs show through. Close the gaps.
- The waist band is a good place for one warm tan accent.
- The helm wings are your best invention. Make them bigger and swept back so they read from the high camera.
