# D - critique

**Most important fix: give the skirt a bell.** The lower body is a straight pale-blue column under a wide, flat waist plate. From above, the plate is the widest thing on the figure and reads as a knight's fauld, not a dress. Narrow or remove the plate and flare the skirt out from the hip to the ankle.

Second: the bright royal-blue shoulder caps rise and stand out at the shoulders, which the brief rules out for a feminine figure. Drop them and keep the sleeves slim and pale.

Other notes:
- The royal blue is too saturated for the muted night world. Pull it back to sky blue or steel.
- The open front coat panels over the skirt are a nice idea (a riding-coat dress). Lengthen them into wind-caught tails behind the figure.
- The helm's small white crest is fine but tiny. Sweep it back.
- The back is dark slate with a heavy belt. Lighten the skirt back.
- The current parts go into horizontal bands. Move them into the skirt's outline.
