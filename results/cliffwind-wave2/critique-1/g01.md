# F - critique

**Most important fix: the skirt doesn't flare.** The lower body is a set of straight split panels with the legs visible between them, so from above it reads as tassets over trousers. Close the panels into a skirt that bells out from the hip to the ankle, on the slim avatar too.

Second: slim the sleeves. The pale upper-arm blocks are shoulder-width and make the top half broad.

What works: there is real invention here. The swept twin "ear" crest on the helm and the streamer blowing back from the head (clear in the side view) are the only elements in this wave that actually show the wind. Keep them and enlarge the streamer so it reads from the ARPG camera.

Other notes:
- The diamond chest motif and the white/steel/blue palette suit the zone. Add a warm tan accent at the waist.
- The back is dark slate. Run the streamer or a light panel down the back.
- Parts are spread over small trim. Move them into the skirt.
