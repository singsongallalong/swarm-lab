1. From the ARPG camera, the head is a flat bright-yellow square with a small sprig, which reads as a bare head and is the loudest colour on the figure. Give the headpiece a real crown that covers the top of the head: a ring of fern fronds or a moss-and-bone circlet with a veil falling down the back, in muted moss and lichen.
2. The sleeves are full-width dark blocks that make the upper body boxy and as wide as the skirt hem. Taper them close to the arm at the shoulder and give them a small flared cuff at the wrist, so the outline goes narrow shoulder, fitted waist, wide hem.
3. Nothing reads as moss or fungus yet. Add a frilled hem (two stacked ledges like fungal gills, lichen over bone) and a ruffled collar at the neckline, so the brief's idea shows from above.
