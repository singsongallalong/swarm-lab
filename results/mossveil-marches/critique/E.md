Keep: the spotted mushroom cap (bone dots on moss green) is the most charming accent in the run and reads instantly from above as a fungus lady.

1. The skirt is a stepped stone ziggurat, wide flat ledges like a plinth, so from above it reads as a pedestal rather than cloth. Make the steps shallower and more numerous, curve the hem outward, and edge each tier with a thin lichen or bone frill so it reads as ruffles or gills.
2. The sleeves and bodice are wide dark blocks, the ribbed black chest is busy, and the shoulders are as broad as the hem. Cut the sleeves to arm width at the shoulder, and replace the ribbing with one clean fitted bodice and a defined waist band.
3. The palette is almost all one mid moss green. Add contrast: a black underlayer showing at the hem, a bone neckline frill, and keep the lichen yellow-green for the frills, so it isn't a single green mass at night.
