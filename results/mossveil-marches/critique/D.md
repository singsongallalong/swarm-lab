Keep: the round mushroom-cap hat with its bone rim and the tiered skirt with lichen-yellow gill-like ruffle bands. Both read as fungus from the ARPG camera, and no other family looks like this.

1. The bodice and sleeves are a dark, full-width block, so the top half reads as a coat and the waist disappears. Narrow the sleeves at the shoulder, and add a fitted waist band (bone or lichen) just above where the tiers start, so the figure goes narrow, then flares.
2. The skirt is short and squat, like a stacked cake at knee height, and the legs show as two columns inside the tiers from the front. Lengthen it to the shins, flaring a little more at each tier, and close the front so it reads as one skirt rather than rings around trousers.
3. The bone X lacing on the chest is too pale and is the brightest thing on the torso. Tone it to greyed bone or lichen, and move the extra pale into a thin rim on the hat brim and the lowest hem tier, where it frames the silhouette from above.
