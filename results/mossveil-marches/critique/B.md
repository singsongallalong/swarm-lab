Keep: the dark green A-line skirt with its bone hem is a real flared skirt from every angle, and the black bodice over moss green gives a strong, elegant two-tone read.

1. The headpiece is a tall black stovepipe with a pale quill. From above it reads as a soldier's shako, and it is the first thing you see. Replace it with something that reads as forest: a low wreath or a cap of fern fronds or bracket fungus in moss and lichen, no taller than half a head.
2. The bright gold-yellow cuffs and hip blocks look like courtly braid, not lichen, and they sit at hip level, so they widen the waist from above. Push them toward a sickly yellow-green, move them to the wrists only, and keep the waist the narrowest point.
3. The skirt is plain from above. Spend parts on two or three ruffle tiers at the hem (thin stepped ledges in deep moss, lichen and bone, like fungal gills) so that from the ARPG camera the skirt ends in a frilled ring instead of a flat edge.
