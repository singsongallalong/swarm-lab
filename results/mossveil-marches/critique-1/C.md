Keep: the light moss-green A-line skirt falling from a fitted bodice reads as a dress front-on, and the small fern crown is a good feminine headpiece.

1. The dark sleeves are big square blocks that stand out past the shoulders and dominate the ARPG view. Make them slim and fitted, ending in a small fern or gill ruffle at the wrist, so the shoulder line reads narrow.
2. The skirt is only a front panel: from the side and back it is a straight dark column and it stops around the knee. Wrap the flare round the back and sides, and carry it down to the shins so it reads as a full skirt from above.
3. Give the skirt a frilled edge that reads as moss or fungal gills: a stepped lichen or bone ruffle at the hem, the one bold accent the top-down camera will catch.
