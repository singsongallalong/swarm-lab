Keep: the pale ruin-stone body with moss-green banding is the only set that really uses the bone colour, and the tiered spire headpiece is a strong silhouette idea.

1. From the ARPG camera the bodice reads as a broad pale box: the shoulder/sleeve blocks run out past the shoulder line in bone. Pull the sleeves in flush with the arm, make them dark moss or black, and keep bone only as trims so the shoulders read narrow.
2. The skirt is a straight column the width of the legs; there is no waist and no flare. Cinch a dark green waist band, then flare the skirt outward panel by panel so the hem at the shins is clearly wider than the hips from the front, side and above.
3. The spire hat reads as a pagoda or stone finial, not a lady's headpiece. Lower it, and turn its tiers into stacked fungus brackets or a moss-and-bone crown that sits on the head rather than rising high above it.
