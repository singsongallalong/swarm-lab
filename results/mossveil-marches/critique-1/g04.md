Keep: the fern-frond crest on the head and the mottled moss and lichen patches on the skirt carry the overgrown-forest idea.

1. There is no skirt silhouette: the legs read as trousers under a tunic, and the feet show beneath. Build a continuous skirt that starts at a fitted waist and flares outward to the shins all the way round, covering the legs from front, side and above.
2. The torso and sleeves are wide green blocks with the widest point at the shoulders. Slim the sleeves to the arm and narrow the shoulder line so the figure reads feminine from the ARPG camera.
3. The patchy colour blocks look like camouflage rather than finery. Use them in a deliberate order: deep moss bodice, a lichen yellow-green ruffled hem, and bone trims at the neckline and waist.
