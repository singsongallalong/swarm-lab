Keep: the stepped, tiered moss-green skirt from the front and the small cap with pale mushroom buttons on top are good ideas that read from above.

1. The black bodice and sleeves are wide boxes and the whole top half is near-black, so from the ARPG camera it reads as a heavy dark coat. Slim the sleeves to the arm, narrow the shoulders, and add a fitted lichen-coloured waist band.
2. The skirt only steps out at the front; from the side and back it is a straight column. Carry the tiers all the way round and widen each step so the skirt flares to the shins as a full bell.
3. Make the headpiece bigger and more feminine: grow the mushroom cluster into a crown of pale caps and moss around the head so it reads as a lady's headpiece, not a small cap.
