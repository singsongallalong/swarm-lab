1. The skirt panels splay out as separate wedges with dark gaps between them and points at the hem, so from the side and on the slim avatar it reads as tent legs. Close the gaps so thigh panels hand over to shin panels as one continuous bell, and square off the hem at shin height.
2. The black bodice and sleeve blocks are wide and heavy, the widest thing seen from the ARPG camera. Slim the sleeves to the arm, drop the shoulder caps, and add a fitted waist band so the dress reads narrow-shouldered and cinched.
3. The palette is almost only black and deep green. Add lichen yellow-green and bone as a few bold accents that read from above: a frilled bone or lichen hem ruffle and a lichen trim at the neckline.
