# A: sunburst crest sentinel

Best of the six. The radiating gold sunburst on the helm is the one strong idea, and it reads at the ARPG camera before anything else; with the pale stone slab pauldrons over dark old-bronze it is its own family, not iron, not fenscale.

Fixes, most important first:
1. **Age the crest.** The rays are a clean tan-gold and read a little like a feather headdress. Push them toward dull sun-gold with darker bronze roots, and make them thicker and fewer (5 broad rays rather than many thin ones) so they read as cast metal, stone-carved, not plumage.
2. **Close the thigh gap.** Bright avatar green shows between the pale waist fringe and the boots in front, back and side, on both bodies. Lengthen the fringe/tassets or add a bronze thigh plate; that green is the loudest colour on the figure from above.
3. **The waist fringe of pale strips** reads as teeth at ARPG distance. Fewer, wider strips, or one stone skirt plate.
4. Arms are a flat dark box; one sun-gold band at the bracer edge would tie them to the helm.
5. Holds on the slim avatar; keep the slab pauldrons that wide, they carry the "massive" read.

Palette (olive-bronze, pale stone, gold) is right; keep it muted.
