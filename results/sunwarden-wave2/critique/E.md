# E: pale sage plate, ridge crest

Reads the weakest from the ARPG camera: pale sage plate with pale stone pauldrons is low contrast, so from above it washes towards linen/oatmeal and has no single shape to hold onto.

Fixes, most important first:
1. **Pick one silhouette idea and push it.** The ridge crest on the helm is the only distinct shape and it is small; make it a tall, wide stone or gold fan crest, or give the pauldrons a much bigger, stepped statue form.
2. **Darken the metal.** Pale celadon reads washed and faintly pastel; old bronze should be darker and warmer so the pale stone and gold actually separate from it.
3. The thin gold lines on the arms disappear at distance; fewer, thicker gold elements.
4. Back is a plain dark slab; add something.
5. Fit holds; bright avatar green shows at the thighs and hips, cover it.
