# D: pale stone slabs on bright green

The pale stone slab pauldrons and stone helm block are strong and read clearly from the ARPG camera; the shape language (big flat stone blocks) is right for "statue".

Fixes, most important first:
1. **The green is far too saturated and young.** Lime/grass green reads as a toy or a fresh plant, not aged bronze in a night world, and it collides with fenscale. Replace with dark olive-brown bronze; keep green only as verdigris in seams.
2. The helm has no idea of its own beyond a box; the little green sprig on top looks like a leaf. Give it a sun crest or a tall carved crown.
3. The gold neck band and chest ring are good touches but thin; one bigger sun motif would do more.
4. Legs are boxy green tubes with small grey studs; simplify and darken.
5. Fit is fine on both bodies.
