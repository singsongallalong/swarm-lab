# B: dome pauldrons and bucket helm

The idea is the round stone dome pauldrons with gold rims, but they are too small to carry the silhouette: from the ARPG camera one dome reads and the other shoulder reads as a plain box, so the outline is lopsided rather than deliberate.

Fixes, most important first:
1. **Make the domes the silhouette.** Double their size, make both equal and clearly round from above, sitting on the shoulder (no float on the slim body, where they hover slightly proud of the arm).
2. **Warm the green.** The body is cool grey-green, which sits too close to fenscale. Shift to yellow-brown old bronze with verdigris only in recesses.
3. The gold half-disc on the bucket helm is a nice sun note but tiny; either enlarge it into a real crest or drop it and spend the parts on the domes.
4. The back is one undifferentiated dark slab; add a stone or gold element so the set reads from behind.
5. Green avatar thigh shows between chest and boots; cover it.
