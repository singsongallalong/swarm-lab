# C: barrel pauldrons, cross crest

The round barrel pauldrons capped with gold bosses read well from the ARPG camera (two clear circles) and give real mass. That is a good silhouette line.

Fixes, most important first:
1. **Colour is too green.** Mid moss green across body and pauldrons puts it next to fenscale. Move the metal toward brown old bronze (verdigris as accent, not base) and let more pale stone show.
2. **The helm crest reads as a cross / plus sign,** which suggests a cleric or crusader, not a sun sentinel. Replace with a sun disc or a short radiating crest, or a tall plain stone crest.
3. The barrel pauldrons have stacked ribbed bands that look like padding at close range; simpler, heavier forms would feel more like cast bronze.
4. Chest stone plate with gold disc is good; enlarge it slightly so it carries from above.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar; thighs show bright avatar green, cover with tassets.
