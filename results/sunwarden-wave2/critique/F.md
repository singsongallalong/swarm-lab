# F: flat-top helm and back sun-wheel

Second best. Dark olive-bronze with pale stone slab pauldrons and a big sun-wheel on the back: colour is right (aged, muted, warm) and the back disc is visible from the ARPG camera.

Fixes, most important first:
1. **The sun-wheel reads as a cog.** The square teeth around the rim make it a machine gear (steampunk), not a sun. Turn the teeth into tapered rays, or make it a plain disc with a ray ring; keep it big.
2. **The front has no idea.** From the front and three-quarter the flat-top box helm and slab pauldrons read close to iron's boxy knight. Carry the sun forward: a sun disc on the chest, or a sun crest on the helm.
3. The grey-white stone pauldrons are a touch cool; warm them to pale ruin stone so they don't echo iron grey.
4. Gold trim strips are many and thin; fewer, heavier bands.
5. Fit holds on the slim avatar; cover the green thigh gap.
