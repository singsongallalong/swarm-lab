# B: critique (rank 6)

B has the right material instinct: a verdigris teal is a true old-bronze colour, and the pale stone pauldron caps are a good pairing. But there's no silhouette idea: a box helm, box shoulders and a box chest. And teal-green is the colour most likely to read as "fenscale again" from the pitch-50 camera.

1. **Find one strong shape.** A crest, domed pauldrons or a sun disc. Today the outline is the same as the iron family's, broad and square.
2. **Shift the hue off fenscale.** Warm the verdigris towards bronze (more olive and brown under the green), and let sun gold show in more than the chest disc and a thin helm stripe.
3. The pauldrons are asymmetric (the left plate is tilted and smaller). Unless that's a deliberate choice, make them a matched heavy pair.
4. The thin crack and slash lines on the visor and back plate read as marks or errors at distance. Drop them.
5. Fit is clean on both avatars.
