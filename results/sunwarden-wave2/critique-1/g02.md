# F: critique (rank 3)

The great disc on the back is a strong silhouette idea, and from the ARPG camera (which mostly sees backs) it is the most readable single shape after C's domes. But it is grey, so it reads as an iron buckler, not a sun.

1. **Make the back disc bronze-gold with a sun rim** (rays or a toothed edge). Then it becomes the family's mark. Grey loses it to the iron family.
2. **The helm is a stacked top hat.** The stepped tower with a cap reads as a chess rook or a stovepipe hat. Make it one massive, simple helm (a flat-topped great helm or a short crest), not three stacked boxes.
3. The khaki-olive chest with pale stone pauldrons is a good palette split. Keep it, and drop the small scattered dark rectangles and studs on the chest: they read as noise or damage marks.
4. Fit holds on the slim avatar. The disc sits on the back without floating.
5. The yellow collar band shows in the side view. Fine, but make it the same gold as the trim.
