# D: critique (rank 4)

D is the most statue-like: pale stone dominates, with massive block pauldrons and a block helm. As "a statue come to life" it lands. As "bronze sentinel" it does not: the bronze is gone and there is almost no sun gold.

1. **Bring the bronze back.** From the ARPG camera the set reads pale grey-beige blocks, which drifts towards linen (pale) or bleached iron. Make the arm plates, greaves and belt clearly green-bronze, and keep stone for the pauldron tops and the chest.
2. **Add one silhouette idea.** It is all boxes: box helm, box pauldrons, box chest. Give it one shape that isn't a cube: a crest, a sun-disc gorget, or flared pauldron edges.
3. **Sun gold:** today it is only the knob on the chest. A gold ring round the chest disc and a gold helm band would do it.
4. Fit is clean on both avatars, and the massive shoulders hold on the slim body.
5. The inner-bevel tops on the pauldrons and helm are a nice carved-stone touch. Keep them.
