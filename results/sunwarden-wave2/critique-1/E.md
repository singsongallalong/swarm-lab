# E: critique (rank 5)

The winged/horned helm gives an outline, and the gold trim bands are the strongest sun-gold of the wave. But the whole set reads samurai (kabuto wings, banded lamellar chest, tabard) rather than ancient Grashan sentinel.

1. **Rework the helm wings into a sentinel shape.** The thin swept horns read as a kabuto. Make them a broad, heavy crest or flat cheek-wings in bronze, simpler and thicker, so the outline says "statue" and not "samurai".
2. **The brown tabard on the back pulls it towards the leather family** from the ARPG view, which mostly sees backs. Make it green-bronze plate or pale stone.
3. **Too dark overall.** The body is near-black olive. Lift the bronze tone and use more pale stone so it stands apart from fenscale and Reaver.
4. The many horizontal gold bands are good colour but busy. Keep two or three strong ones.
5. Fit is fine, with nothing floating. On the slim avatar the helm wings stay attached.
