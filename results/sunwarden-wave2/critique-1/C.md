# C: critique (rank 1)

The dome pauldrons are the idea, and they work: two round boulder shoulders are the clearest outline in the wave, they read from the pitch-50 camera as their own family, and they feel carved from a statue. Keep them.

1. **Fix the helm crest first.** The tan plank on top reads as a stick or a book from the ARPG view and the three-quarter view. Make it a proper sentinel crest: a wider, deeper fan or a transverse ridge, in sun gold, so the helm and the domes make one shape.
2. **Push the sun gold.** Gold shows only as thin trim and the chest disc. Put a gold boss or rim on the crown of each dome. From above, the domes are what the camera sees most.
3. **More pale stone, less olive-on-olive.** The back and the domes are all dark olive. Pale stone tops on the domes or a stone gorget would bring in the second palette colour and keep it apart from fenscale grey-green.
4. Fit holds on the slim avatar: the domes stay on the shoulders and nothing floats. The thigh lames are thin; if parts are spare, a heavier stone-and-bronze skirt would add weight.
5. Do not add small details. The stud row and the small square on the dome front add nothing at distance.
