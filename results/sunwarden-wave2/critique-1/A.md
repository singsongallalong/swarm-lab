# A: critique (rank 2)

The sun-ray crest on the helm is a real idea (a sunwarden wearing the sun), and the slab pauldrons give mass. But the rays are hairline spokes that shrink to a smudge from the ARPG camera.

1. **Make the crest carry the set.** Use fewer, thicker rays (5 to 7) in sun gold, longer, fanned as a half-disc behind the helm, so the outline reads from pitch 50. Spend the parts the stud rows use here.
2. **The palette is too dark olive.** From above the body reads dark green-black, too close to fenscale and to the hide of the Reaver sets. Lift the bronze to a warmer, lighter green-bronze and give both pauldrons the pale stone top: today only one has it, which reads as an error rather than a choice.
3. The back sun sigil is good. Make it larger and gold so the back view, which players see most while running, says "sun".
4. Fit is fine on both avatars, with nothing cutting through. The thigh tassets sit well.
5. The chest stone plate with its gold disc is the right note. Keep it.
