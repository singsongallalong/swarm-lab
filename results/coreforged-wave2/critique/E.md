# E: Coreforged thin-spike crown with brow gem

Close cousin of A: a ring of thin gold spikes, an orange gem on the brow, orange slashes on the pauldrons. The palette is right, but the spikes are so thin they disappear from the ARPG camera, leaving a black box with orange stripes.

Fixes, most important first:
1. Thicken and shorten the crown spikes, fewer of them, so the crown survives the high camera; that is the whole silhouette idea and right now it is lost.
2. The pauldron orange strips are the loudest thing on the set; with the crown weak, it reads as "black armour with orange tape". Tone them to a thin seam or move the light up into the crown.
3. Olive legs show at the waist; add a hip plate.
4. Chest and back are flat black with only a faint vertical seam; one bigger crack would carry the "split by magma" brief.
5. Gold belt bands repeat on the legs where no one sees them; spend those parts on the crown.

Fit is fine.
