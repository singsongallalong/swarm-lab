# B: Coreforged helm with molten plume, core-gem chest

Clear idea: a flame-orange crest on the helm and a glowing gem at the sternum, the "heart of the mountain god" worn on the chest. Reads as black-and-ember, apart from every reference family.

Fixes, most important first:
1. From the ARPG camera the plume is a small orange stub; the silhouette is otherwise a square black box. Make the crest taller and swept back (a stepped fin, two or three parts) so it reads as a flame from above, or commit to a shoulder line instead.
2. The chest gem is small and flat; seat it in a raised dark socket with gold rim so it holds the eye and gives the later aura an anchor.
3. Olive legs show at the waist front and back; add a hip plate.
4. The back is a flat slab with one thin crack; one diagonal molten seam across the backplate or a small cape stub would help the set read on the walk-away.
5. Pauldrons are generic boxes; one stepped edge or upturned lip would give them a forged, dangerous line.

Neon use is restrained and right. Fit is clean.
