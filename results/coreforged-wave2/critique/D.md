# D: Coreforged furnace knight (vent-post pauldrons, T-visor)

Idea: pauldrons topped with little orange-capped posts like gas vents, a gold-framed helm with a T visor, many vertical cracks. The vents read well from the ARPG camera as orange squares, which suits the zone's gas vents.

Fixes, most important first:
1. Too much Neon: vent caps, helm top, visor and many chest and leg cracks. The brief says two or three small parts; it risks looking garish and leaves no room for the aura. Keep vent caps and one chest seam, darken the rest.
2. Gold trim is everywhere (helm frame, belts, cuffs) and starts to read as gold-and-black royalty generally rather than basalt. Cut gold to the helm frame and one belt.
3. The helm is a plain box; the vent posts are the one idea, so make them bigger and fewer (one chimney per shoulder) rather than several small posts.
4. Olive legs show at the waist; add a hip plate.

Fit is clean; nothing floats.
