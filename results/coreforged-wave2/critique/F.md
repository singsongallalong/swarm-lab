# F: Coreforged plain helm, chest seam

Weakest read of the six. A black helm with a small fin, a single vertical orange crack down the chest, gold bands. From the ARPG camera it is a dark blob; the helm fin and pauldrons do not make a silhouette.

Fixes, most important first:
1. Pick one silhouette idea and commit to it: a crown, horns, a crest or a stepped pauldron line. Right now there is none that reads from above.
2. The chest crack is the one good thing; it could run up through the gorget and helm visor so the set reads as one cracked shell.
3. Pauldrons are large plain boxes; give them a forged edge or a molten seam on top so the high camera sees light.
4. Olive legs show at the waist; add a hip plate.
5. Gold bands across the chest and arms are noise; trim them.

Fit is clean; nothing floats.
