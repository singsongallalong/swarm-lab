# C: Horned Coreforged helm, molten diamond heart

A demon-knight read: tall V horns on the helm, glowing eye slit, an orange diamond on the chest, black plate with gold edges. The horns give the most distinctive outline in the batch from front, back and shoulder views.

Fixes, most important first:
1. From the ARPG camera the horns nearly vanish against the helm top; the read falls back on the box. Angle them outward more and thicken the base, maybe tip them with ember gold, so they show from above.
2. The chest diamond is good; give it a dark raised frame so it is not just a flat patch. Keep Neon to the eyes, the diamond and one crack.
3. Waist gap: olive legs show front and back; add a hip plate.
4. Back diagonal crack is nice but thin; widen it slightly so it reads at distance.
5. Horns lean "samurai/demon" more than "mountain god's forge"; a small molten seam at the horn root would tie them to the Core.

Fit holds on the slim avatar; nothing floats.
