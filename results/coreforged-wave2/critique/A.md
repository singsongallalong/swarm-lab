# A: Coreforged Crown set (crown, cuirass, gauntlets, greaves)

Strongest single idea in the batch: the gold spiked crown reads from the ARPG camera as a king's crown, and the flared black pauldrons with orange top strips frame it. Black plus ember gold plus molten orange is clearly its own family, far from iron's grey.

Fixes, most important first:
1. The crown is the brightest mass on the body and almost a flat gold block from above. Thin it to fewer, taller tines with dark gaps between them, and put one small molten gem or neon seam at its base so it becomes the "place for light" (halo seat for the later aura) rather than a gold lid.
2. The waist: olive avatar legs show between cuirass and greaves on the front and back views. Drop a black tasset or faulds plate over the hips; that also lengthens the regal line.
3. The chest cross-crack is thin and nearly invisible from the high camera; widen the vertical seam a touch or let it run up into the gorget so the crack links to the crown.
4. Pauldron orange strips are good; keep Neon to them, the crack, and one crown gem, no more.
5. Gold banding on gauntlets and greaves is busy detail no one sees from above; spend those parts on the hip plate.

Fit on the slim avatar holds; nothing floats.
