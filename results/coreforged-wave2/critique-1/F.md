# F: Blue-panelled plate

1. **The bright blue panels break the brief.** Large saturated blue blocks on the shoulders and arms are the dominant colour from every angle, including the ARPG view. The palette is basalt black, molten orange-red, ember gold; replace all blue with black plate or molten red.
2. Once blue is gone the set has no silhouette of its own; build up the helm (crest or crown) or the pauldron line.
3. The orange chest crack and boot slits are good places for light; widen the chest crack so it reads from above.
4. Pauldrons are wide flat slabs; fit otherwise holds on slim.
