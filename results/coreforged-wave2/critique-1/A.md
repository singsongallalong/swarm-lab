# A: Coreforged Crown set

1. **Make the magma read from above.** The gold spiked crown is the strongest silhouette idea in the wave and reads at once from the ARPG camera, but the cracks are hair-thin orange lines on the chest front only. From the top the set reads "black and gold", not "molten". Put one wider glowing seam where the camera sees it: across the pauldron tops or a molten band at the crown's base, so the aura has a home.
2. **Tone the gold down a step.** The crown and every trim edge are the same bright ember gold; the crown plates are so large they read as a solid gold block from above. Darken the crown body slightly and keep the brightest gold for the spike tips, so it reads regal rather than toy-like.
3. **Pauldrons.** They are broad flat boxes much like iron's; tilt or step them so the shoulder line differs from iron at a glance.
4. Fit holds on the slim avatar; thigh gap shows green trousers (same as every set, no leg slot), acceptable.
5. Spend parts saved from trim lines on the glow seams above.
