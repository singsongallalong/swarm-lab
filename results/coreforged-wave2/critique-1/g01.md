# B: Core-gem chest set

1. **Find one silhouette idea.** The glowing gem in the sternum is a good place for light, but it only reads from the front; from the ARPG camera the set is a dark box with gold trim, close to a blackened iron set. Give it an outline: a raised crown or crest on the helm, or pauldrons that rise into ridges.
2. **Move light where the camera looks.** Add a second glow point the top view catches (helm crest, shoulder seam). Keep Neon to two or three small parts.
3. **Pauldrons are oversized flat slabs** (very visible in the close view); they widen the body like iron. Narrow them or shape them.
4. The thin diagonal cracks on chest and back are lost at distance; one bold crack beats several thin ones.
5. Fit is fine; nothing floats.
