# C: crevasse and bridge
Keep: the rope-and-timber bridge over a dark stepped cut; "span from the near bank" is a real picture.
1. The crack is too shallow and too narrow to read as a drop. Make it deeper, with each step darker down to near-black, so the bridge has something to cross.
2. The three seeds are one shape. Change the crack's angle, its width and where the bridge crosses.
3. Make the snow spill and the cold light bigger and brighter; at game distance neither shows.
