# F: glacier tongue
Keep: the fluted serac wall seen from the top view; the stripes of ice coming through the notch are the right idea.
1. In every shot it is too dark to read. Make the ice a clearly paler blue than the granite, so the tongue stands apart from the massif.
2. Light the meltwater cave and the pool much more strongly, so the foot of the glacier has a bright subject.
3. The meltdrip effect is a few faint streaks. Make it denser and gather it into one fall from the cave mouth.
