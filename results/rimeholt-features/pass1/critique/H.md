# H: frost-giant court
Keep: the four mounds round a glowing pool; the ring reads clearly from the top and from the game camera.
1. The giants read as shrines or houses, not bowed bodies. Give each a distinct hooded head and shoulders that lean in toward the pool, and keep them clear of the mound clutter.
2. Shots from the ice are filled by a pale slab in the foreground. Frame up the pool toward one giant with the others behind it.
3. Make the snowfall flakes bigger and fewer, and confine them to the court, so the column shows at game distance.
