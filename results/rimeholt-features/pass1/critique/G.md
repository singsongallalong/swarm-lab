# G: yardang field
Keep: the spindrift effect, streaming one way; it sells the wind.
1. The ridges read as stepped, dark terraces with no clear shape. Make fewer ridges, but longer and taller, with a sharp crest and a clear contrast between the dark stone flank and the pale snow back.
2. The shots have no subject. Frame one ridge end-on as a prow, with the road curving past it and spindrift tearing off the crest.
3. Push the seeds apart: change the ridge count, their spacing and the wind direction.
