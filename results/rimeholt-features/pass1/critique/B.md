# B: pressure ridge
Keep: the pale ice pans with the frozen horse, shield and hand in them; that is the idea, and it reads from above.
1. The ridge never reads as two long sails. Make it one continuous, tall, jagged wall of tilted slabs running right across the pad, with a clear notch where the road goes through.
2. The buried shapes are lost in clutter. Space them out along the dark pan, one per window, with bare dark ice between them.
3. The frost-seep glows are scattered dots. Line them up under the ridge so the pass carries the light.
