# J: frozen longship
Keep: the tilted hull, mast and ribs breaking out of the ice crown; it is the best single silhouette in the run.
1. The hull glow is a vague smear on the surface. Lay it as a long, narrow cold strip that traces the drowned hull's outline beside the ship.
2. The seeds are the same wreck at the same angle. Change the tilt, the heading and how much of the ship is above the ice.
3. The ice crown is a grey mess of boxes. Make fewer, larger, paler shards that splay outward from the hull.


Across the whole run, the critic also noted: J and D tie on total; J ranks first for the better photograph, D has the stronger effect and reads best from the game camera. Across the board the seeds are weak: most features repeat one layout three times. Close shots are mostly too dark to have a subject. The features whose effect is their idea (A, D, G) only show it in the set-piece panel.

The biggest gain for most features: make the three seeds genuinely different versions of the idea (shape, size, arrangement), and give every close shot a lit subject.
