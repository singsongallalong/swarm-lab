# D: aurora well
Keep: the stepped round shaft with its ringed stones, and the aurora column, the strongest effect in the run.
1. All three seeds are the same bowl. Change the terrace count, the shaft's width and whether it sits off-centre or breaks one side of the rim.
2. The shaft floor is dim. Make the lit floor clearly brighter than everything round it, so the hole reads as the light source even without the aurora.
3. The rune stones are thin posts lost against the terraces. Make them fewer, taller and darker, so they ring the lip against the pale snow.
