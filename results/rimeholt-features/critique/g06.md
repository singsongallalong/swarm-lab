# I: barrow avenue
Keep: the barred doorhouse with its pale lit threshold, which is a clear point of interest.
1. The mounds don't read as barrows. Make them taller, rounder snow heaps that clearly swallow each doorhouse, lined up as an avenue toward the great barrow.
2. The shots are flat and murky. Frame down the avenue, with doors receding and the lit thresholds stepping into the distance.
3. Push the seeds apart: change the avenue's curve, the number of barrows and the size of the great barrow.
