# E: frozen battle
Keep: the two ranks of black spear-hedges and the tall frosted standard; from the top they read as armies meeting.
1. The standard is a plain grey column. Give it a crossbar and a hanging, rime-stiff banner shape so it reads as a standard and not a pillar.
2. Shots taken along the road are blocked by the snow banks. Frame the best shot on the standard over the clash line, with the spears in the foreground.
3. Change the seeds: bend the clash line, change how far apart the ranks are and where the knoll is.
