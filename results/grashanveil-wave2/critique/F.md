# F: beehive crown, terracotta A-line gown

**1. Fix the blue at the shoulders (most important).** Bright blue blocks show at the top and outside of each shoulder and run down the side of the torso. This looks like the bare avatar's shirt showing through gaps in the sleeves. It is the brightest colour on the figure from the ARPG camera, it is off-palette, and it makes the shoulders look raised and wide. Cover it fully with slim pale-stone sleeves.

**2. Slim the sleeves.** Even without the blue, the sleeves are block-width. Halve them so the shoulder line is narrow.

**3. Keep the skirt, which is the best in the wave.** The terracotta A-line flares cleanly from a belted waist. It has a real bell, holds together on the fit avatar and puts the width low. Two improvements: lighten the terracotta a step (it reads dark brown at night), and add a pale stone or gold hem band so the flare edge catches the eye from above.

4. The stepped beehive crown gives good height and a gold, sun-temple feel. A sun disc or finial on top would make the sun worship explicit.

5. The bodice's centre lacing is too small to read. Spend those parts on a gold collar or a sun pendant.
