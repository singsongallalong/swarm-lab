# D: rayed sun halo, pleated linen gown

**1. Flare the skirt (most important).** The pleated, fringed skirt is the prettiest lower half of the set, but it hangs straight down from the belt and is no wider than the hips, and it is narrower still on the fit avatar. Splay the pleat panels outward so the hem is clearly wider than the hip. A stepped stone hem band under them would also add weight and ruin character.

**2. Slim the sleeves.** They are still full shoulder-width pale blocks, so the top view shows a broad horizontal bar across the shoulders. Halve their width and let the halo be the widest thing at the top.

**3. Keep the halo, and give it more contrast.** The rayed sun disc behind the head is the best sun-priestess idea in the wave. It puts height and identity at the head and reads well from above. In the night palette it is a muddy brown disc. Use dull sun-gold for the face and pale stone for the rays.

4. The V neckline, terracotta belt and front tassels define the waist nicely. Keep them.

5. Pale linen with pleats risks reading as the linen family. Add one stone or carved element, such as a stepped hem or a geometric belt plate, to anchor it in Grashan.
