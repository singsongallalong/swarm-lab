# A: sun-hand crown, stone shift, dark block sleeves

**1. Slim and lighten the sleeves (most important).** The sleeves are full shoulder-width blocks in dark umber against a pale bodice. From the ARPG camera they are the two darkest, biggest masses on the figure, so the top view reads as a broad, square-shouldered warrior. Bring them in to roughly half the arm block and use pale stone or terracotta so they sink into the gown.

**2. Make the skirt a bell, not a staircase.** The stepped tiers spread outward, but they start at the waist as a wide ledge and come out as a squat stepped plinth. Narrow the first tier at the hip, then widen steadily to the hem so the skirt looks like a flare and not a pedestal. On the fit avatar the tiers barely flare at all.

**3. Keep the sun motif and make it read from above.** The fan crown and the round sun-disc pendant are the best ideas here and they belong in this zone. From pitch 50, though, the crown is a small brown tuft. Make it taller and gold so the height sits at the head.

4. The waist is a single thin gold line. A terracotta sash would define it more clearly.

5. The green vine flecks are too small to read. Spend those parts on the hem or the crown.
