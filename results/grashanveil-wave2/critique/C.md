# C: diadem, sash, column gown

**1. Give it a skirt (most important).** Below the waist the dress is a straight column with gold grid lines. There is no flare from the hip on either avatar, and from the ARPG camera the lower half is a box. The brief's first requirement is a real flare or bell, so build an A-line or stepped bell from the hip out to about 1.5x the hip width at the hem.

**2. The sleeves are the widest thing on the figure.** They are wide pale slabs with flared terracotta cuffs that stick out at elbow height, so the width sits high. Slim them to a narrow tube and drop the cuff flare.

**3. The figure lacks a sun-priestess focal point.** The small diadem barely shows from above, and the diagonal sash is the only shaping line. Add a raised sun disc or rayed crown for height at the head, plus a gold or terracotta waist band.

4. The gold grid trim reads as panelled armour or a robe and pulls it toward the iron and studded families. Use fewer, bolder trims.

5. The colour is flat stone throughout. Bring in terracotta and a little plains green on the hem.
