# E: sun-disc crown, tiered stone gown

**1. Slim the sleeves and narrow the bodice (most important).** The bodice and sleeves form one wide pale block with terracotta cuffs at waist height, so the widest line on the figure sits at the elbows. From the ARPG camera it reads as a boxy torso. Use narrow sleeves and a narrower bodice.

**2. Push the skirt flare further.** The stepped tiers with a terracotta hem are on the right track, but the first tier is as wide as the torso and each step only adds a little. Start narrow at the hip and step out boldly so the hem is clearly the widest point. On the fit avatar it currently reads as a stack of boxes.

**3. Make the crown read from behind and above.** The flat sun disc looks good from the front, but from the back and the ARPG camera it is a grey slab. Add rays or a rim so it reads as a sun from every angle, or angle it upward.

4. The green dangle at the waist is a nice plains-green touch, but it is too small. Add a green sash end or a band on the hem.

5. Good palette discipline overall: stone, gold and terracotta.
