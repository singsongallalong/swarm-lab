# F: stepped crown, pale bodice over terracotta bell

**Best of the wave: the outline reads as a gown, and the height sits at the head.**

1. **Sleeves are still shoulder-width blocks (most important fix).** In the front, close and ARPG views the pale arm blocks run straight out from the torso edge, so from above the top half is a wide pale slab, the same flaw as every earlier gown. Narrow them to about half the arm width, tuck them against the bodice, and drop any trim ledge at the shoulder line.
2. **Waist.** The bodice drops straight into the skirt. Pull in a narrow sun-gold or terracotta sash and step the bodice inward just above it, so the bell starts from a visible waist.
3. **Push the bell further.** The terracotta skirt flares in front and back, and it holds on the slim avatar. Add one more outward hem tier so the widest point is clearly the hem, wider than the shoulders when seen from pitch 50.
4. **Back.** The back reads as flat dark grey. Carry the terracotta skirt and a gold line up the back so the colour split also reads from behind.

Keep: the stepped ziggurat crown. It is the strongest Grashan motif on the sheet, reads clearly from the ARPG camera, and gives height without width at ear level. Keep the pale stone over faded terracotta palette as well. Parts are mostly well spent; move any trim from the shoulders to the hem.
