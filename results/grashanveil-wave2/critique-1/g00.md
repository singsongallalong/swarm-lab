# D: sun-disc halo, fluted pleat skirt

**The truest sun-priestess, but the skirt does not flare.**

1. **The skirt is a column (most important fix).** The fluted pleats are lovely and ancient, like a carved column, but they hang straight down. From the ARPG camera the lower half is no wider than the hips. Splay the pleat bands outward in two or three tiers so they form a real bell from the hip, with the hem clearly the widest point. Check that it holds on the slim avatar, where it currently reads as a pillar.
2. **Sleeves are broad.** The pale arm blocks are as wide as the shoulders and carry a ledge at the top. From above they make a wide pale cross. Slim them down and remove anything at the shoulder line.
3. **Colour.** The whole set is pale stone and grey, which risks reading as linen from above. Bring in more faded terracotta (a wider sash, a hem band) and a plains-green accent so it belongs to its zone.
4. **Back.** The back is plain grey. Show the halo rays and a pleat panel there.

Keep: the radiant sun-disc halo behind the head. It is the best sun-worship idea in the wave, it gives height at the head and it reads from pitch 50. Keep the V-neck and the terracotta belt.
