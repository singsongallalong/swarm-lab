# E: terracotta robe with sun-disc head

**The colour collides with studded, and the outline is a robe, not a gown.**

1. **Palette (most important fix).** Almost everything is terracotta and brown. From the ARPG camera it sits right next to the studded reference family (red-brown blocks). Make pale ruin stone the dominant colour and keep terracotta for bands and a sash, with sun gold at the head and waist.
2. **No flare.** The skirt hangs straight and splits at the legs, so from above it reads as trousers or a tunic. Build a closed bell from the hip that widens to the hem, and check it on the slim avatar, where it is narrowest.
3. **Sleeves.** Broad shoulder-width blocks with trim ledges. Slim them and clear the shoulder line.
4. **Waist.** There is no defined waist. Add a narrow gold sash with a step inward above it.

Keep: the sun-disc head ornament. It is a good, simple sun motif with height at the head. The thin gold trim lines are elegant, but they are lost on the terracotta field.
