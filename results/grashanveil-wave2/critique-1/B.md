# B: terracotta shoulder blocks, tall mitre

**The shoulders are the exact flaw the brief forbids.**

1. **Remove the shoulder blocks (most important fix).** The big terracotta cubes on the upper arms are the brightest, widest thing on the figure. From the ARPG camera they make two broad pads, which reads as armour (close to studded or iron), not feminine. Remove them and use slim pale sleeves with at most a thin terracotta cuff.
2. **Mitre.** The tall headdress leans and kinks in the side and three-quarter views. Make it upright and symmetric, ideally with a sun motif, so the height reads as a deliberate crown.
3. **Skirt.** There is a slight layered flare at the hem, but the widest point is the shoulders. Widen the hem tiers and pull in the waist so width sits low.
4. **Back.** The back is dark grey with brown blocks and looks like iron armour.

Keep: the layered pale-and-terracotta hem bands and the front chest medallion. With slim sleeves, the pale body would read as stone-carved.
