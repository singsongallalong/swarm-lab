# C: grey coat with gold trim, small diadem

**From above, this reads as iron or a long coat.**

1. **Palette and sleeves (most important fix).** Dark grey sleeves and side panels with dark pad blocks dominate the ARPG and back views. It sits in the iron family. Make the whole outline pale ruin stone, slim the sleeves to well under shoulder width, and remove the dark shoulder pads.
2. **Skirt.** The pale front panel hangs straight between dark side panels. There is no bell, and the slim avatar shows a column. Build a flared skirt from the hip.
3. **Head.** The diadem is small and close to the head, so there is no height and no sun reading. Add a radiating sun crown or halo.
4. **Waist.** Add a gold sash and a step inward.

Keep: the thin sun-gold trim lines and the diagonal terracotta sash. They are the most refined detailing in the wave and would suit a pale gown.
