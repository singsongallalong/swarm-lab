# A: stepped stone ziggurat

**The most distinctive outline, but it reads as a statue or a cake, not a woman.**

1. **No figure (most important fix).** The arms are lost inside a wide stepped mass, and the shoulders are as wide as the hem tiers, so the outline is a solid trapezoid with no waist. Carve it back: narrow shoulders, slim visible sleeves, and a pinched sun-gold waist. Then let the stepped tiers start from the hip and widen downward, so the stepping becomes a skirt rather than a plinth.
2. **Shoulder width.** In the ARPG and close views the top tier is a broad flat slab. Width belongs low. Drop the upper tiers and keep only the skirt tiers.
3. **Colour.** The pale stone and terracotta bands suit the zone, but there is no plains green, and the gold is used only on the face area. Add a gold sash and a green hem line.
4. **Back.** The back reads as dark grey blocks.

Keep: the radiating sun crown, which reads well, the tiered carved-stone idea, and the medallion. The skirt flare from the tiers is real and survives the slim avatar. It just needs a body above it.
