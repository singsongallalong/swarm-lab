# Notes for set D

Keep: the wide, curved, flaring skirt of overlapping plate bands; it is the fullest skirt outline in the run and reads as a battle skirt.

1. The block pieces on the shoulders and upper arms stand out past the shoulder line and make the top as wide as the hem, so from above the figure reads as a broad warrior. Remove or flatten them to the arm, and keep the cuirass narrow at the shoulders.
2. The muddy brown cuirass and skirt blur together and the set reads dull at night. Make the skirt bands alternate fen green and reed straw, with a pale bone edge on each hem, and keep the bodice dark, so the colour carries the gown.
3. There is no visible waist between bodice and skirt. Put a narrow belt of bog iron at the natural waist and have the cuirass taper into it before the skirt flares.
