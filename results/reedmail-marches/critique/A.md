# Notes for set A

Keep: the stepped, tiered skirt widening tier by tier to the hem; from above it reads as a full skirt, and the reed-plume crest is a good fen headpiece.

1. From the ARPG camera the whole set is one dark bottle green, so the tiers merge into a single block. Alternate the tiers between fen green and reed straw (or bog-iron brown), and edge each hem with a pale bone or reed line so the flare reads by colour.
2. The tiers are hard, even steps and look like a wedding cake or a pagoda. Make the upper tiers shorter and tighter and let the lowest tier drop longer and wider, so the skirt flows into a bell instead of stacking. Bring the hem down to the ankles.
3. The cuirass and sleeves are as broad as the shoulders and give a square top. Cinch the waist with a narrow straw or bog-iron belt and keep the sleeves slim and close to the body, so the top reads fitted and the skirt does the widening.
