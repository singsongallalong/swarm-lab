# Notes for set B

Keep: the strong colour split of a green cuirass over a reed-straw skirt; from above it is the clearest two-tone read in the run.

1. The skirt is short, nearly straight-sided and ends near the knee, so from above it reads as a kilt or tassets over trousers, not a gown. Lengthen it to the shins or ankles and flare it out a good step at the hem, so the outline is a clear A shape.
2. The grey box helm with a single spike reads as a soldier's cap, not a woman's headpiece. Replace it with something lighter and prettier: a reed or willow circlet, a soft hood, or a crest of reed plumes that stays inside the head's width.
3. The green coat is boxy and runs straight from shoulder to hip. Add a fitted waist: a narrow dark belt with a bog-iron buckle and a cuirass that tapers in toward it, so the skirt flares out from a visible waist.
