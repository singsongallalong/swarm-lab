# Notes for set C

Keep: the laced X bodice front and the pale reed-straw bands in the skirt hem; they are the most feminine detail in the set.

1. The skirt is a stack of flat, wide discs that stops above the ankles and sits under a broad, square green torso, so from above it reads as a top-heavy box on a plinth. Taper the torso in to the waist and turn the skirt into fewer, longer tiers that flare steadily from waist to ankle.
2. The light green sleeves stand out as the widest, brightest thing from the camera and widen the shoulder line. Darken them to the bodice green and slim them, so the eye goes to the skirt and lacing instead.
3. The tan box helm with a reed spike looks like a crate. Replace it with a reed-woven circlet or a soft hood in black water dark with a straw trim, so the head reads as a headpiece and not a block.
