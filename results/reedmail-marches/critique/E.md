# Notes for set E

Keep: the bell skirt of long vertical reed and green panels falling to the ankles, with the pale V neckline; it is the clearest and prettiest gown in the run and belongs to the fen.

1. From above, the shoulders and sleeves are still full blocks as wide as the skirt hem, so the top is heavy. Slim the sleeves close to the arm and narrow the bodice toward a belted waist, so the outline goes narrow-narrow-wide.
2. The skirt panels are all one dark mid tone at a distance. Make every other panel a lighter reed straw and add a thin pale bone hem along the bottom, so the bell shape and its flare read from the high camera.
3. The antler-like reed crest rises high and forked and reads a little like horns. Make it a softer fan of reed blades, lower and leaning back, with a straw or bone band around the brow, so the headpiece reads as finery.
