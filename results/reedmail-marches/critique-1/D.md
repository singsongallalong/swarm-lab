# Notes for set D

Keep: the layered lamellar skirt of reed-straw and bog-brown plates is the most literal armoured skirt in the run and the idea is right.

1. The skirt is a straight barrel from waist to ankle with no flare, and the dark-green shoulder blocks are the widest thing on the figure, so from above it reads as a column with pauldrons. Shrink the shoulder blocks to the arm's width and make each lamellar band wider than the one above it so the hem is clearly wider than the waist.
2. The palette is almost all brown and tan, which reads as the leather and studded families and not fen. Bring fen green into the skirt (every other band, or the lowest band) and add one pale bone trim at the waist and hem.
3. The big X badge on the chest is the only bold accent and it reads as a target. Drop it and spend those parts on a fitted waist: a narrow dark belt with the cuirass tapering into it, so the waist pinches before the skirt flares.
