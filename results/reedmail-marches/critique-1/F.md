# Notes for set F

Keep: the single reed spike on the grey helm and the green cuirass with a pale trim line are a sound start for the headpiece and bodice.

1. There is no skirt: the grey-green plates hang only to mid-thigh and the legs show below as trousers and boots, so from every camera this reads as a soldier in a tunic, not a gown. Build a full skirt of plates from the waist to the shins that flares out past the hips, with thigh plates overlapping shin plates so there is no gap.
2. The upper body is wide and square with sleeves the width of the chest, so the shoulders are the widest point. Narrow the shoulders and sleeves to the arm and pinch the waist with a belt, so the outline goes narrow shoulder, small waist, wide hem.
3. Grey and dark green with only a thin trim give it no zone and no finery. Make the skirt the colour statement: reed-straw or eel-scale plates over fen green, with a bone trim at the hem and on the helm's spike.
