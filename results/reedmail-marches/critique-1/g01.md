# Notes for set E

Keep: the tiered skirt that widens toward the floor gives a real flare, and the reed plume on the helm is a good headpiece idea.

1. The whole set is one dark green, so at night and from the ARPG camera it is a black block with no waist or hem to read. Lift the skirt tiers to a mid fen green, keep the cuirass darker, and add reed-straw or bone edging on each tier and a band at the waist so the shape reads.
2. The lowest tier spreads into a flat slab at floor level that reads as a plinth the figure stands on, not a hem, and the tiers are all the same depth so it looks stacked. Cut the bottom tier back to just beyond the tier above it, lift it off the ground to the boot tops, and make the tiers deeper toward the hem.
3. The shoulders and sleeves are as wide as the skirt's top tiers, so the figure reads as a rectangle from above. Narrow the sleeves and shoulder blocks to the arm, and pinch the cuirass at the waist above the first tier.
