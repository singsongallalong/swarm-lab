# Notes for set C

Keep: the smooth olive cone skirt that flares cleanly from a belted waist to the ankles is the most dress-like outline in the run, front, side and from above; keep that shape.

1. It reads as cloth, not armour, and as quiet olive rather than fen finery: there is no lamellar, scale or plate on the skirt at all. Lay two or three overlapping hoops of bog-iron or eel-scale plates round the skirt (thigh, knee, hem), each a shade darker than the cloth, so it reads as a battle skirt and the flare gets rhythm from above.
2. The upper body is a wide flat green box with sleeves as wide as the chest, so the shoulders read broader than the hem from the ARPG camera. Narrow the shoulder blocks and sleeves to the arm, and pinch the bodice at the waist belt so the waist is visibly the narrowest point.
3. The coif is a square gold-edged box that reads as a crate on the head. Replace it with a small reed crown or a close cap with a single upright reed plume, in bone or reed straw, so the headpiece reads pretty and adds height without width.
