# Notes for set A

Keep: the reed-straw skirt that steps outward in tiers from the waist is the clearest flare in the run and reads as a skirt of plates from above; keep it and the laced green cuirass.

1. The straw sleeve cuffs and the shoulder blocks stand out past the shoulder line, so from the ARPG camera the top half is as wide as the skirt and the figure reads square. Pull the sleeves in to the arm's own width, drop the straw cuffs to the wrists only, and keep nothing above or outside the shoulder.
2. The skirt is a stack of flat shelves that stops at mid-shin and then shows bare dark legs and boots; it reads as a cake, not a hem. Add one more tier so the lowest plates reach the shin tops of the boots with no gap, and slope each tier's edge down (longer at the front and back than at the sides) so the flare looks like it falls rather than steps.
3. The skirt is all one straw tone over a mid-green cuirass, so the set reads two-tone and loud. Break the skirt tiers with a darker bog-iron or black-water band every second tier and repeat one pale bone trim at the hem, so the colour reads fen and night rather than haystack.
