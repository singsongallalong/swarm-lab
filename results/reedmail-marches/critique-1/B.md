# Notes for set B

Keep: the long A-line fen-green skirt of vertical reed-straw panels is the prettiest and most on-brief skirt in the run; it reads as a dress from every angle and holds on the slim avatar. Keep it, and keep the twin reed-plume headpiece.

1. The straw belt bar runs straight across the arms and sticks out past both sleeves, so from above the waist is the widest point of the figure. End the belt at the body's sides, make it narrower, and let the sleeves hang inside it so the waist reads cinched.
2. The cuirass is a plain dark-green box with a grey plate in the middle, and from the high camera it and the shoulders are what you see most. Shape it: narrow the shoulder blocks, add a straw or bone line down from each shoulder to the waist point (a V) so the bodice reads fitted and tapers into the skirt.
3. The back of the skirt is a flat dark panel with none of the front's reed stripes, so from behind and from above it goes dead. Carry the vertical straw panels round the back and sides, and add a darker bog-iron band at the hem so the skirt has an edge.
