# D — spire helm, stained-glass panel column

**Most important fix: give the skirt a gown shape.** It is a straight, segmented tube of stacked blocks (very visible in side view), with no waist and no flare, so from the ARPG camera this reads as a robed priest or armoured figure, not a dress. Pull it in at a crimson waist, then flare it out over the thighs and shins in two tiers.

What works:
- The strongest theme of the batch. The spire headpiece echoes the leaning church tower, and the column of violet, amber and crimson panes down the front is real stained glass. That stays visible as coloured specks even in the ARPG view.
- Crimson edge trims on the sleeves are tasteful and on palette.
- Holds on the slim avatar.

To change:
- The spire is tall and thin. That is great for the theme, but keep it from reading as a spike/helmet: thin it further, or give it a fall of veil behind.
- Stained-glass panes are scattered down the whole body as small squares. Gather them into one larger rose-window or lancet panel on the bodice, plus a pane border at the hem, so they read as glass rather than buttons.
- Lots of stacked bands on the skirt and sleeves read as armour plates. Merge them.
- Sleeves are broad; slim them.
- A green-tinted belt line appears on the fit render; check its colour.
