# A — hooded choir gown, crimson waist, bone hem

**Most important fix: it disappears into the night from the ARPG camera.** From above it is one charcoal slab: the stepped hood, black shoulders and black skirt all merge, and the crimson waist cord and bone hem are too thin to show. Give the top a lighter mass the camera can catch: a slate or wet-stone-grey shoulder capelet/wimple fitted close to the neck (kept low, nothing rising), or a broad candle-amber or crimson stole down the front. Something has to break up the black when seen from overhead.

What works:
- Gown read from the front and three-quarter views is honest: fitted crimson waist, A-line skirt to the shins, hem flares slightly with a bone/gold trim. It holds on the slim avatar with no gaps.
- The stepped hood is nun-like and on theme; the button placket is a quiet clerical touch.
- Palette is correct and muted, with no off-palette colours.

To change:
- Sleeves are wide, boxy default blocks that make the shoulders look broad. Slim them, or let the cuffs bell out lower down instead.
- The hood's top steps read as a pagoda/helmet from the side. Soften them into a fall of veil down the back so the silhouette feels like cloth.
- The skirt from the back is plain. One crimson or violet panel or ribbon at the back would cost little and read from above.
- Small hem studs and buttons are invisible at pitch 50; spend those parts on the veil or the stole.
