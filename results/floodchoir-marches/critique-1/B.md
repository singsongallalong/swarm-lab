# B — tiered A-line gown, violet stole, bun veil

**Most important fix: swap the cyan collar and shoulder trim for candle amber or bone.** The bright blue at the neck is the loudest colour on the set and is not in the palette (black, slate, stone, amber, crimson/violet). From the ARPG camera that blue wedge is the first thing you see, and it reads as "court gown", not flooded parish.

What works:
- Best gown silhouette of the batch: clearly fitted at the violet sash, then a stepped A-line skirt flaring all the way to the floor. It reads as a dress from above and is easy to tell apart from iron, leather and studded.
- The violet stole/lattice down the bodice and the V on the back read as a choir stole: churchy and feminine.
- Holds on the slim avatar: tiers stay attached, no gaps between thigh and shin.
- Bone-coloured cuff trims are the right ivory tone.

To change:
- The skirt steps look like a staircase/plinth, especially in side and three-quarter views. Soften with an extra intermediate tier, or vary tier depth so it falls like fabric.
- Sleeves are broad blocks; narrow them or bell them at the wrist.
- The tall bun/hood is fine but blends into the body from above. A thin bone or amber band at the brow would separate head from body.
- Add one stained-glass or candle note at the hem (a crimson/violet border on the bottom tier) to tie it to the Drowned Parish.
