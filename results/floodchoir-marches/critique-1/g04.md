# E — wide hoop skirt with grey flood hem

**Most important fix: the lower hem tiers are hollow frames that look detached.** In the ARPG and shoulder views the bottom grey tier is an open rectangular ring with gaps, and from above it reads as a floating fence or crate around the feet. Close it into a solid sloped hem that hands over cleanly from the upper skirt, with no visible see-through.

What works:
- The biggest, most readable skirt from above: wide stepped flare, clearly a gown, and the most distinct outline of the batch.
- The slate/wet-stone lower tier is a lovely idea: a waterline where the gown is "drowned". Keep that concept.
- The violet chest panel with a small candle is on theme.

To change:
- The skirt is too wide and squat for an elegant figure; it reads as a bell or plinth. Make it a bit narrower and longer so the shins are covered and the waist looks taller.
- The shoulders and arms form a wide horizontal slab. Slim them; the brief wants narrow shoulders.
- The flat cap with a red edge looks like a mortarboard. Swap it for a veil or wimple.
- The many thin red pinstripes on the sleeves and skirt are invisible from above and add noise. Use one crimson hem line instead.
- The back is a plain black mass; add a veil or ribbon to break it up.
