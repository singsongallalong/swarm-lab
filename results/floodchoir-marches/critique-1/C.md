# C — studded veil hat, banded column skirt

**Most important fix: the skirt is a straight, stacked column of horizontal bands, so it reads as lamellar armour, not a gown.** Taper it in at the waist, then flare it outward toward the shins. At the moment the outline from the front and ARPG views is a rectangle from shoulders to feet, which sits too close to the iron and studded reference sets.

What works:
- The crimson reliquary or lamp on the chest is a nice candle/stained-glass idea.
- The veil/cap with a small top finial is distinctive as a head silhouette.
- Holds together on the slim avatar, with nothing floating.

To change:
- Gold studs around the headpiece and on the skirt read as "studded", the family you most need to avoid. Remove them, or turn them into a candle ring with an amber glow.
- Cyan bands at the collar and waist are off-palette. Use crimson, violet or amber instead.
- The many thin horizontal ridges on the skirt and sleeves are detail no one will see from above, and together they read as plates. Spend those parts on one or two wide flared tiers.
- Shoulders and sleeves are wide and heavy; slim them so the figure looks feminine.
- The headpiece needs to read as cloth (a veil falling behind), not a helmet.
