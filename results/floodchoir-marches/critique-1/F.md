# F — cassock gown, violet front panel, crimson jewel

**Most important fix: flare the skirt.** The bodice, palette and trims are the most elegant of the batch, but the skirt drops nearly straight to a slight step at the hem, so from the ARPG camera it reads as a priest's cassock rather than a gown. Widen it from the hip to the shins (two tiers that step outward), and keep the violet front panel running down the centre of the flare.

What works:
- Palette is spot-on: black, slate hem, a bone/ivory cuff trim (correct off-white), violet panel, crimson jewel and hem line. Solemn and beautiful.
- The violet centre stripe is the best night-readability device here. It stays visible from above, so the gown doesn't vanish into the dark.
- The V-shaped gorget with a crimson jewel reads as a choir or abbess ornament.
- The belted waist with a hanging tassel is a good touch, and the set holds cleanly on the slim avatar.

To change:
- The pillbox cap is small and reads as a fez/zucchetto. Add a short veil falling to the shoulders behind, kept low, for a nun/mourner silhouette.
- Sleeves are broad boxes; narrow them or bell them at the cuff.
- Add a candle-amber note somewhere (a small lantern at the belt or a pale amber band at the cap) so the parish lamplight theme lands.
- Fine bone trim stripes on the sleeves are lost from above. Spend those parts on the skirt flare instead.
