# A: hooded choir cassock

**Most important fix:** give it something that reads from above. From the ARPG camera A is a flat charcoal cross shape: low hood, square shoulders, and a skirt hidden under the wide sleeve cuffs. It looks like a monk's robe or the iron set in black, not a gown. Add a pale bone or candle-amber band around the hood crown or the top of the coif, and widen the hem so the skirt shows past the sleeve cuffs from above.

**What works**
- The palette is the most restrained of the six: charcoal, a slate hood, a muted crimson waist sash, and thin ivory hem trims that read as bone, not white.
- The crimson ribbons down the back of the skirt are a nice mourning touch.
- It holds on the slim fit avatar. Thigh and shin panels meet cleanly and nothing floats.

**What to change**
- There is no fitted waist. The sash sits at the bottom of a boxy bodice and the skirt barely flares. Pull the bodice in and let the shin panels step out by a stud or two.
- The sleeve cuffs end at hip height and merge with the skirt into one block. Shorten them or narrow them at the wrist.
- The headpiece has almost no outline (a small flat cap). A taller wimple or veil falling to the shoulder blades would separate the head from the shoulders.
- There is no candle or stained-glass hint anywhere. The buttons on the front are detail nobody sees from above. Spend those parts on a small amber pendant or a violet panel instead.
