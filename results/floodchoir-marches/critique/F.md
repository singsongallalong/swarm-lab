# F: violet-stole cassock gown

**Most important fix:** feminise the outline. Right now F reads as a priest's cassock with a biretta. The skirt is a near-straight column and the cap is clerical. Flare the shin panels clearly past the thigh panels (a gown, not a robe), and swap or soften the cap into a veil-backed coif so it reads as choir or nun, not cleric.

**What works**
- It is the most legible colour composition from above. The violet front panel running from waist to hem and the violet bow at the back waist both show at the ARPG pitch, so the figure does not vanish into the black.
- The palette is spot on: black, slate, violet, a crimson diamond jewel with an amber 'V' at the breast, an ivory (bone) collar and layered ivory-edged sleeves, and a thin crimson hem.
- It has a defined waist band and narrow shoulders. It holds well on the slim fit avatar, and the thigh-to-shin handover is clean with no floating parts.

**What to change**
- The skirt flares only at the very hem step, so the side view is a pillar. Give it one more step of width at the knee.
- The cap's tiny cross and violet knob are invisible from above. Use those parts for a short veil instead, which would also add a head outline.
- The candle-amber is limited to the small breast chevron. An amber hem trim or a little lantern at the hip would bring in the lamp-lit theme.
- The stacked ivory sleeve ribs are busy detail. One trim would do.
