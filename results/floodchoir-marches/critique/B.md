# B: wimpled choir nun

**Most important fix:** soften the stair-step skirt. The tiers flare out in equal, hard 'ziggurat' steps that look like a pedestal from three-quarter and ARPG. Use fewer, taller shin panels with one wide hem step and a thin violet trim, so it reads as a falling skirt rather than a staircase.

**What works**
- This is the clearest gown outline in the set. Front and side both show a fitted bodice, a belted waist and a full A-line skirt to the floor. The side view especially reads as a nun's habit.
- The candle-amber band on top of the wimple is the beacon from the ARPG camera, so the figure does not vanish into the night. It is on theme too (a lamp in an upstairs window).
- The violet stole and pendant with its stained-glass 'M' panel, the violet tie at the back waist and the 'V' on the back are well-judged accents in the right palette. The ivory trims stay muted.
- Narrow, sloping shoulders, nothing rising. Holds well on the slim fit avatar, with no gaps at the thigh-to-shin handover.

**What to change**
- The amber is used on the head only. A faint amber line at the hem or cuffs would tie it together and lift the skirt from above.
- The wimple's small rear spike (side view) looks like an ear or horn. Round it off or turn it into a soft fall of veil.
- The black bodice merges with the black sleeves from above. A slate-grey tone on the sleeves would separate the waist.
