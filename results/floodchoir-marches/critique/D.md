# D: spire-hat stained-glass vestment

**Most important fix:** make the skirt full in profile. Front-on, the flared triangle side panels suggest a gown, but the side and ARPG views show a straight black column with no flare front-to-back. Push the shin panels outward front and back as well as at the sides, so the skirt reads from every angle and from above.

**What works**
- The stained-glass window on the chest, with amber, crimson and violet quarter panes in a slate frame, is the most literal and most beautiful 'church' motif in the run. The palette is exactly the brief's.
- The spire headpiece echoes the leaning church tower. It is a strong, unusual outline and clearly its own family.
- The crimson waist band with the small chevron and the amber/violet hem strips are good accents. The narrow shoulders keep it feminine.
- It holds on the slim avatar.

**What to change**
- The stained glass sits on the vertical chest, so it is mostly hidden by the head from the ARPG camera. Repeat a smaller pane or a coloured band on top of the shoulders or the hat brim so the top view shows colour. Otherwise it is a black block at night.
- The spire is very thin and reads as an antenna or pin from three-quarter. Thicken the base into a stepped steeple and lean it slightly as a nod to the tower.
- The hat is tall and rigid, which reads bishop, not choir girl. Add a short veil falling behind it to soften it.
