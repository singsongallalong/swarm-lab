# E: boxed helm, plinth skirt

**Most important fix:** replace the skirt. The lower body is a wide slate-grey box with a hollow, open top, and from ARPG and three-quarter you look straight into a tray around the hips. It reads as a figure standing in a crate or font, not wearing a skirt. Build a closed, flaring skirt that hangs from a fitted waist down to the shins.

**What works**
- The violet chest panel with its little amber candle-and-flame drawing is a nice stained-glass idea.
- The red cross motifs on the back and cuffs give it a churchly note, and the colours stay muted.

**What to change**
- The headpiece is a square bucket with a gold frame around the face. It reads as a knight's great helm, which pulls it toward the iron family and away from choir or mourner. Swap it for a veil or coif.
- The slate-grey skirt base is lighter than the bodice and much wider than the shoulders, so the silhouette is a top-heavy torso on a plinth. There is no waist and no fall of fabric.
- The panels step in hard ledges (see the side view) instead of handing over smoothly.
- The candle panel sits on the chest and is invisible from above. The ARPG view shows the open box and the helm frame only. Spend parts on the outline: a skirt, a veil, a pale trim at the hem.
