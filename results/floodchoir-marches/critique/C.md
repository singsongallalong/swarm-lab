# C: candle-crown veil

**Most important fix:** rebuild the skirt and lower body. Right now the sleeve cuffs, the hip band and two wide skirt tiers stack into a broad stepped plinth, so from ARPG and three-quarter the figure looks like a statue on a pedestal or a tiered cake, not a woman in a gown. Pull the waist in hard, drop the hip-level cuff shelf, and let one smooth skirt flare reach the shins.

**What works**
- The candle crown is the best idea in the whole run. Amber-tipped candle stubs ring the veil and read clearly from the ARPG camera. It is pure Floodchoir: a mourner carrying her own vigil.
- The crimson stained-glass reliquary panel on the chest and the violet waist band fit the palette.
- The thigh and shin panels are continuous. It holds together on the fit avatar and nothing floats.

**What to change**
- The cyan-blue lines under the waist band (very visible in three-quarter) are off-palette. Shift them to slate or violet.
- The torso is a black slab with no waist, so it is barely feminine. Narrow the bodice and add a pale bone collar or neckline edge.
- The back is almost featureless. A dropped veil tail or a crimson ribbon down the back would give the ARPG view something.
- Parts are spent on the boxy tier edges. Move them into the crown, which is the set's identity.
