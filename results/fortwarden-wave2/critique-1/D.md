# D: quartered tabard and crossed cap

1. **The pauldrons are too bright.** The pale steel slabs are the lightest value on the figure and pull the eye away from the heraldry, and in a night-muted world they glare. Drop them two steps darker or trim them blue, and narrow them slightly so they don't echo iron's broad grey outline.
2. **The cream quarters read as khaki or tan patches.** Push the cream warmer and lighter, toward a true cream, so the quartering reads as blue-and-cream arms rather than patchwork.
3. **The cream cross on the blue cap top is the best top-down mark in the run.** Keep it. It is exactly where the ARPG camera looks. Consider making the cap slightly taller (a shako) so the outline also says "garrison".
4. **Brass badge.** Only a tiny brass fleck is visible on the cap front. Enlarge it, or place one at the belt buckle.
5. **Fit.** It holds on the slim avatar. The side view shows a white band at the chest edge that looks like a seam gap, so check it closes.

The heraldry reads well and this set has the strongest family read from above.
