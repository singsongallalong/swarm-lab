# A: brimmed hat and baldric

1. **The hat crown reads as leather from above.** From the ARPG camera the most visible thing is a brown box on a thin steel brim, which says "leather set". Make the crown steel (a kettle hat) or town blue, keep the brim steel, and widen the brim one step so the hat outline carries at distance.
2. **Give the set one idea, not a uniform.** The blue coat, the cream baldric and the brown cuffs make an honest soldier, but nothing in the outline is Fortwarden's alone. Push the hat into that signature: a broad kettle brim with a blue band.
3. **Shoulders.** The flat steel ridge on top of the shoulders is fine but thin. Either thicken it into a real spaulder line or spend those parts on the hat.
4. **Brass badge.** I can't find it from above. Put a brass plate on the hat band or on the baldric's crossing point, where the top-down camera sees it.
5. **Fit.** The set holds on the slim avatar, with no floating parts. The brown gloves and boots bulk out a little at the wrist, which is acceptable.

The palette is right and the night-muted world fits. The read is what needs work.
