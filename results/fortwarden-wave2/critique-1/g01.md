# F: crested helm and brass badge

1. **The crest reads as a bent cream finger.** It is the one strong silhouette idea and it works in profile, but it is a single thick bent block. Thin it, give it two or three stepped segments sweeping back, and colour it town blue or blue-and-cream so the crest itself is heraldic.
2. **From the ARPG camera the crest foreshortens to a nub.** Lay it more fore-and-aft, along the head's length, so it draws a stripe from above.
3. **The helm box is plain grey.** Add a blue band or a brass brow so the head joins the coat's palette.
4. **The brass chest roundel is right on-brief.** It is the clearest brass badge in the run. Its parts are visible only from the front, so a second, smaller brass mark on the shoulder or on the helm would help it read from above.
5. **Fit.** It holds on both bodies. The cream pauldron edges and the brass trims sit flush.

This is the best mix of heraldic, sturdy and night-muted. Fix the crest's shape and it leads clearly.
