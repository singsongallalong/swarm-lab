# C: tower helm with blue-capped pauldrons

1. **The colour is iron and charcoal.** The torso, helm, arms and boots are dark grey or black, and town blue appears only on the pauldron caps and as thin trims. At distance this is the iron family with blue epaulettes. Make the coat or tabard blue and keep steel for the helm and pauldrons only.
2. **The tower helm is the boldest silhouette idea here.** A crenellated, keep-shaped great helm is a lovely tie to the fort and tower. Commit to it: make the crenels larger and cream or steel, and add a blue band, so it reads as a battlement rather than a stack of blocks.
3. **Cream and brass are almost absent.** The small cream chevron on the chest is lost from above. Put cream on the crenels and add a brass badge on the helm front.
4. **The stacked pauldron tiers** (three layers) cost parts and read as one block from above. Two tiers would be enough.
5. **Fit.** It holds. The tall helm stays seated on the slim avatar and the legs cover cleanly.

The idea is strong; the palette is off-brief.
