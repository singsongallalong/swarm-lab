# E: coif, pavise and bracers

1. **The side profile shows a grey frame around the torso.** The pavise's edges wrap the body like a cage and read as cutting through the arms. Make the pavise one flat board on the back, with no side rails, and seat it close to the body.
2. **The dome coif reads as a soft cap.** It is the strongest town-blue read of any helm, but its rounded blob outline isn't "sturdy". Add a steel brow band or a nasal with a brass boss to give it a soldier's edge.
3. **The pavise is the silhouette idea, so make it show.** From the ARPG camera it reads only as a cream outline on the back. Give it a blue field with a cream charge (cross or tower) so the town's arms ride on the soldier's back.
4. **The asymmetric single grey pauldron is a nice garrison touch.** Keep it, but match its value to the cap's steel.
5. **Fit.** The slim avatar holds, and the brown bracers and boots fit cleanly.

The blue dominates nicely, apart from leather and iron. The renamed slots (Coif/Pavise/Bracers) suit the idea.
