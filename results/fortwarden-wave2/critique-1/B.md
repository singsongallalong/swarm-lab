# B: visored cap and slab pauldrons

1. **From the top it reads as iron.** The grey visored cap and the two big grey slab pauldrons cover most of what the ARPG camera sees, and the blue survives only as a strip down the chest. Recolour the pauldron tops town blue with a steel edge (or cream with blue), or shrink them to half width and let the blue coat show.
2. **The helm is a flat grey block with a slot.** It is neither heraldic nor a garrison shape. Give it one idea: a blue crown with a cream band, a raised comb, or a brim.
3. **The cream V chevron is a good heraldic mark, but it only reads from the front.** Repeat it on the back, or move it up onto the shoulders where the camera looks.
4. **The back is plain navy and grey.** It needs a cream or brass element so the figure reads when it is walking away.
5. **Fit.** It holds on both bodies. The slab pauldrons hang square off the arm with a visible gap at the top on the slim avatar, so seat them lower.

The parts are spent on bulk that copies the iron family's broad grey outline.
