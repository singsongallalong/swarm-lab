# F: sallet, plume and brass badge

**Most important fix: make the helm read from above.** The plume and spike are a strong side-view idea, but from the ARPG camera they shrink to a sliver and the head is a pale grey box, which edges toward iron. Widen the plume into a blue-and-cream crest running front to back, or turn the helm crown town blue, so the head is the family mark from the high camera.

What works: this is the brief most fully met. Navy coat, cream-edged pauldrons, a brass roundel badge on the chest and a crested helm together read as a town's own soldiers, heraldic, sturdy and honest. The cream trim on the shoulder line is visible from above and is the best use of cream in the run.

Other notes:
- The brass roundel sits on the chest, invisible from above. A second, smaller brass boss on each pauldron would carry it to the ARPG view.
- The plume leans back hard in side view; check that it stays on the helm when the avatar turns.
- Trim the back-of-coat detail; nobody sees it.
- Fit holds on the slim avatar.
