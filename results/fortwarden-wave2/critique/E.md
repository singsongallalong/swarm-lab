# E: coif and pavise

**Most important fix: make the "pavise" idea visible.** The chest is named for a pavise, but from the ARPG camera there is no shield shape, only a navy back with a thin brown cross and one pale pauldron. Hang a real town-blue pavise with a cream field and the cross on the back, broad enough to break the outline from above, and that becomes the set's one silhouette idea.

What works: the blue mail-coif hood suits a night-bound gothic world and fits the head cleanly. The crossbelt and brown bracers are honest garrison kit.

Other notes:
- The single pale-grey pauldron is the brightest shape from above and pulls the eye off-centre without meaning anything. Either make it a deliberate blue-and-cream heraldic shoulder or match both sides.
- Cream is missing almost everywhere; the set reads navy and brown, close to a dark leather read at distance.
- The brass badge is absent; put it on the coif brow or the belt.
- The bracer names are fine; keep the Fortwarden prefix.
- Fit is good on both avatars.
