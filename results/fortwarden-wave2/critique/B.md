# B: kepi and chevron

**Most important fix: swap the peaked cap for something from the gothic world.** The flat-topped kepi with a forward visor, the white belt and the chest chevron read as a modern policeman or postman, not a fort garrison in a night-bound gothic town. A steel cap with a brim, a kettle hat or a mail coif in blue would keep the rank feel without the 19th-century uniform.

What works: the colour family is clean. Blue with cream chevrons stands apart from iron, leather and studded, and the cream V on the chest is a readable heraldic mark from the front.

Other notes:
- From the ARPG camera the chevron is hidden; you see a blue cap top and blue shoulder blocks. Put the heraldry on the pauldron tops (a cream stripe or quartering) where the camera actually looks.
- The pauldrons are blue boxes with grey undersides. Give them one strong edge (a steel rim or a stepped lame) so they read as armour, not padded coat shoulders.
- The visor sticks out a long way in side view; shorten it.
- The white belt is too bright for the muted palette; take it to cream or brown leather.
- Fit is fine on both avatars.
