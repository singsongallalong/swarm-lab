# D: quartered garrison

**Most important fix: lift the pauldrons out of dark iron grey.** The chest quartering and the cream cross on the cap crown are the strongest heraldic read in the run, but the big charcoal pauldrons on either side sit next to them in iron's grey. Give the pauldrons a town-blue face with a cream or brass edge, so from above the whole shoulder line carries the colours.

What works: the quartered blue-and-cream torso is unmistakably heraldic and its own family; nothing in the references is chequered. The cream saltire on the cap crown is the smartest part in the run, because it puts the town badge exactly where the high camera looks.

Other notes:
- The cross on the crown reads a little Templar or hospital. A cream bend or a brass boss would say "town" more than "crusade". Keep it if the town's arms really are a cross.
- The quartering is busy on the back. Simplify it to two halves there.
- Add one brass badge to the front of the cap or chest; the brief asks for it and it is absent at this scale.
- Fit holds on the slim avatar.
