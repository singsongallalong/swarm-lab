# C: bucket helm with crest

**Most important fix: get the steel off the top of the silhouette.** From the ARPG camera the set is a grey bucket helm and two wide grey pauldron slabs, which is the iron family's read (grey and broad). The blue is only visible below the shoulders. Paint the pauldron tops town blue with a cream or steel edge, or make the helm a blue-enamelled cap, so the top-down read is blue-and-cream.

What works: the tall flat-topped helm with a cream crest block is a bold head shape, and the cream chevron on the blue tabard is good honest heraldry from the front.

Other notes:
- The cream crest reads as a stack of boxes from above. One clean cream crest ridge, front to back, would read as a plume or comb.
- The helm is very tall and narrow, a stovepipe on the slim avatar. Lower it one step or widen it.
- The brown gloves and boots are fine but plain. That is right; spend nothing more there.
- Fit is clean, nothing cutting the limbs.
