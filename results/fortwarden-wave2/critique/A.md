# A: kettle-hat watchman

**Most important fix: bring cream heraldry up where the camera looks.** From the ARPG view the set is a big grey brim over a navy block, and the navy and brown crossbelt sink into the night. Put a cream or town-blue band, or a quartered cream panel, on the shoulder tops and the hat crown, so the town colours read before the steel does.

What works: the wide steel kettle-hat brim is a real silhouette idea. It reads from above as a flat square cap, which no reference family has, and it suits a garrison watchman. The brass trim on the brim and pauldrons is the right badge accent, and the brown crossbelt says "issued kit", not a knight's harness.

Other notes:
- The brim is near-square from above. A slightly wider, rounder brim with one stepped crown tier would read as a hat and not a slab.
- Pauldrons are thin grey plates, and at ARPG scale they merge with the brim. Make them blue with a steel edge so the hat stays the one grey shape.
- Fit holds on the slim avatar; no floating parts seen.
- The leg and boot detail (two-tone cuffs) is invisible from above. Move those parts to the shoulders and crown.
