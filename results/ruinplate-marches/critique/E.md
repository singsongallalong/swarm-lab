# E — Ruinplate helm / dress / sleeves

**Most important fix:** the colour and outline drift into the iron family. The set is mid-grey rather than pale ruin stone, and the skirt is a straight, slightly split column, so from pitch 50 it reads as a grey knight in a surcoat.

What works:
- The split-front skirt with dark side panels and bronze edging has a heraldic, surcoat feel. It is continuous and holds on the slim avatar.
- The small bronze brooch and cross at the chest are a nice touch close up.

What to change:
- Lift the main colour to pale bone stone and push the dark grey into small accents only. Right now this is the darkest Ruinplate candidate.
- Flare the skirt. Close the dark slits or make them stepped plates that widen toward the shins.
- The shoulders and sleeves are large square blocks, wider than the torso. They read as heavy pauldrons, which goes against the narrow-shoulder brief.
- The gold visor is the brightest thing on the model. Mute it.
- Moss is almost absent.
- Parts go into chest detail that is invisible from above. Move them to the skirt outline.
