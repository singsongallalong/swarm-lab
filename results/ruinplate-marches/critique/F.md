# F — Ruinplate helm / dress / sleeves

**Most important fix:** rework the helm crest. The upright cluster of prongs reads as a clenched stone fist or a broken hand from the ARPG camera. Make it a clear crown or fan crest, lower and wider.

What works:
- A strong tiered bell skirt: pale stone tiers stepping outward with bronze lips and faint moss bands, wide at the hem. It reads as a gown immediately from pitch 50 and is distinct from every reference family.
- A more fitted torso than C. The waist band separates bodice from skirt, giving a cuirass-over-skirt read.
- The tiers overlap without gaps and hold together on the slim avatar.
- The palette is right: bone stone, old bronze and a hint of moss, with a darker green-grey back that still sits in the night world.

What to change:
- The sleeves and shoulders are still square, nearly as wide as the upper skirt. Slim them.
- The pale hem tier is the brightest value on the model and edges toward white. Age it toward bone.
- Push the moss slightly higher on one tier so the green registers from above.
