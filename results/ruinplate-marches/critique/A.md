# A — Ruinplate crown / bodice / bracers

**Most important fix:** make the skirt a real skirt. The pale front panels read as a short apron over the legs, not as a full skirt to the shins. From the back and side the dark legs show through, and in the ARPG view the outline is a plain humanoid column with no flare.

What works:
- The pale stone bodice with moss-green bands at the bust and forearm cuffs is the clearest use of the moss accent in the batch. It reads as "ruin stone, overgrown".
- The crown is small and stays low, which suits narrow shoulders.

What to change:
- Wrap the skirt all the way around. Use panels that step outward from the hip so the back and side views get the same flare as the front. Right now the back view reads as dark trousers.
- The front skirt plates are segmented with visible gaps between the thigh and shin rows in three-quarter view. Overlap them.
- The upper arms and shoulder blocks are broad and boxy, wider than the bodice. Narrow them so the waist reads as fitted.
- The crown is too small to register from pitch 50. Give it a little height or a crest.
- From above it is close to the iron and leather reference sets. Only the pale colour separates it.
