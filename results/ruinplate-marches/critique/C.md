# C — Ruinplate crown / dress / sleeves

**Most important fix:** feminise the top half. The stepped pale-stone skirt is excellent, but the torso and shoulders are a broad slab and the crown is a small chimney. Above the waist it reads as a stone golem, not a lady knight.

What works:
- The strongest gown silhouette from pitch 50: a ziggurat of stacked pale ruin blocks, flaring evenly to the floor on every side. No other family looks like this. It reads as "Grashan ruin" in a way the others do not.
- The tiers overlap cleanly. There are no gaps between the thigh and shin bands, and the skirt holds on the slim fit avatar.
- The parts are spent on the outline. Every tier changes the silhouette.

What to change:
- Pull in the waist hard just above the first tier, and narrow the shoulder and sleeve blocks. At the moment the shoulders are as wide as the second skirt tier.
- Replace the box crown with a wider, lower circlet or crest that reads from above.
- The moss is almost invisible. Add a thin green line at one or two tier lips.
- The skirt blocks are all one flat stone value. Alternate the tiers slightly in tone, or add a bronze lip, so it reads as dressed plate rather than masonry.
