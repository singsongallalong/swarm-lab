# D — Ruinplate helm / dress / sleeves

**Most important fix:** widen the lower skirt so it reaches and flares at the shins. The bell starts well at the hip, then the outline tucks back in near the hem. From the ARPG camera the skirt reads as knee-length tassets over a column.

What works:
- The most feminine and elegant of the six. It has a V-neck chevron on the cuirass, layered hooped tassets with thin bronze cords, and a moss-touched hem. It reads as courtly armour, aged and quiet.
- The palette is right: bone-pale stone, dark bronze cords, a little moss at the hem.
- The tall crested helm gives a clear vertical accent at the head.

What to change:
- Extend the lowest skirt hoop outward and down so the hem is the widest point.
- In the slim fit, the tasset hoops look thin and slightly detached from the hips in three-quarter view. Thicken them or overlap the bands.
- The sleeves are stacked and bulky, and from the shoulder view they stand out from the torso. Slim them to keep the shoulders narrow.
- The helm crest tilts and reads as a plain block from above. Make it a recognisable crest shape.
