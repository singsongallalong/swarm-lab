# B — Ruinplate helm / dress / sleeves

**Most important fix:** give the skirt flare. The dress falls as a straight pale column from chest to floor, so from the ARPG camera it reads as a robe or a stone pillar, not a fitted cuirass over a full skirt.

What works:
- A coherent pale-stone palette with thin old-bronze trims at the waist and hem, and a moss band at the floor. It is muted and ancient and reads as ivory, not white.
- The crested helm with short upright prongs gives a clear crown read from above. It is one of the better headpieces.
- The skirt is continuous to the floor with no gaps. It holds on the slim avatar.

What to change:
- Narrow the waist and step the skirt outward in two or three tiers below the hip, so the outline goes from narrow to wide.
- The sleeves and shoulder blocks are square and as wide as the torso. That makes the figure broad-shouldered and masculine. Slim them.
- The gold visor band glows bright yellow at the face. Tone it toward old bronze so it does not become the focal point.
- The front details (the small chest plaque and the cord at the waist) disappear from above. Put those parts into the skirt shape instead.
