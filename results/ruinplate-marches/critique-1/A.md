# A - Ruinplate (crown/bodice/bracers/hem)

**Most important fix: build a skirt.** There is no gown here. In front and three-quarter views the legs stay separate: pale thigh plates, a bare dark gap at the knee, then a loose pale hem band at the ankles. From the ARPG camera it reads as a pale plated suit, a sibling of iron armour, not an armoured dress. Join the thigh plates and the hem into one flared tasset bell from waist to shin, closed at the back. The back view is also all dark grey with no skirt mass.

What works:
- Palette is right: pale stone, bronze edge lines, moss bands on the sleeves. The trims read as aged ivory, not white.
- The small crowned circlet with bronze prongs is feminine and fits the period. Keep it.

Change:
- The stacked moss/stone bands at the shoulder and upper arm step outward and widen the shoulders, against the brief's narrow-shoulder rule. Taper them and pull them in.
- Chest has small loose details (green tab, seam line) that vanish from above. Spend those parts on skirt panels.
- On the slim fit avatar the hem band hangs at the ankles apart from everything above it. That gap is the main fault.
