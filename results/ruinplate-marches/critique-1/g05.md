# F - Ruinplate (helm/dress/sleeves/hem)

**Most important fix: the moss shoulder blocks.** As in B, the green sleeve cubes sit square and full width at the shoulder, and from the ARPG camera they are the widest dark mass on the upper body. Slim them, drop the green lower on the arm, and keep a pale, narrow shoulder line.

What works:
- Best read from above: the stepped ziggurat skirt makes concentric pale rings in the ARPG view, which is unmistakably ruin stone and unlike any reference family. It has a fitted waist with a bronze belt and a strong flare to the shin.
- The spired stone crown is a striking Grashan headpiece and reads at small scale.
- The palette is right: pale stone, bronze bands, moss creeping on the skirt tiers. It holds together on the slim avatar.

Change:
- The tiers are rigid and evenly spaced, like a wedding cake. Vary the step heights, make the upper tiers shallower and the lowest tier deeper, or break the hem into tasset blocks for more grace.
- In the ARPG view the lowest ring is a hollow frame, so the floor shows through inside it. Cap it or bring the hem in so it does not read as an empty box.
- The back torso is dark green. Lighten it toward stone.
- In the side view the skirt is thin. Give it more depth so it flares in profile too.
