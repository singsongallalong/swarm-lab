# B - Ruinplate (helm/dress/sleeves/hem)

**Most important fix: the moss-green sleeve blocks.** They are the biggest, darkest masses on the figure, square and full width at the shoulder. In the close front and ARPG views they read as heavy pauldrons, so the shoulder line is broad and masculine. Make the upper sleeve slimmer than the shoulder edge, move the green lower (cuff or skirt hem), and leave the shoulder line pale stone.

What works:
- Real gown read: fitted pale bodice, a bronze-studded belt at the waist, then a stepped skirt that widens to the shin with a moss hem. Clearly its own family next to iron, leather and studded.
- The crenellated crown helm is a good Grashan ruin note and shows well from above.
- The skirt is closed all round, holds on the slim avatar, and has no gaps in the back or side views.

Change:
- The back is flat dark grey from shoulders to hem. Carry the pale stone onto the back skirt panels so the dress reads from every angle in the ARPG view.
- The yellow visor slot gives the face a goggle look. Narrow it or frame it in bronze.
- The skirt steps are very even. A slightly wider flare at the last tier would add grace.
