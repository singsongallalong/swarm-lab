# D - Ruinplate (helm/dress/sleeves/hem)

**Most important fix: slim the shoulders and sleeves.** The pale sleeve slabs are wider than the torso and stack in bands at the shoulder. In the close and ARPG views the top of the figure is a broad flat plank, the widest part of the body. Narrow the upper sleeves and taper them in at the shoulder so the waist-to-hem flare is the main shape.

What works:
- This is the most elegant palette: almost all aged pale stone, thin bronze bands, faint moss stains. It reads as ancient carved ruin and stays muted, not white.
- The tasseted skirt with scalloped bronze-trimmed hems flares nicely in front and three-quarter and gives a real knightly-lady line.
- The bronze V-necklace over the cuirass and the crested helm are feminine and of the period.

Change:
- The back is a single dark grey mass, and in the back and shoulder views the skirt reads as dark iron. Use pale stone on the back skirt tassets.
- In the side view the lower skirt narrows to a box at the shins. Let the tassets keep flaring in profile.
- Add a little more moss low on the hem for zone belonging.
