# C - Ruinplate (crown/dress/sleeves/hem)

**Most important fix: flare the skirt.** The skirt is a straight column of stacked pale plates with green side panels. The side view shows a tall rectangle, and from the ARPG camera it reads as a robed pillar or monk, not a flared plate dress. Widen each tier toward the hem so the outline makes an A or bell, with the waist clearly narrower than the hem.

What works:
- The tiered stone plates down the front read as carved ruin blocks and suit the zone well.
- Construction is solid: front, back and side close up, nothing floats, and it holds on the slim avatar.
- The crested crown with a tilted fin is an interesting headpiece.

Change:
- There is too much moss green (sleeves, skirt sides, whole back). Green is meant to be the accent. With this much, the set drifts toward fenscale and robe families. Make pale stone the main colour and keep moss for hems and seams.
- The green sleeve blocks are wide and square at the shoulder. Slim them.
- The back has a dark panel with a scratched rune that is lost from above. Spend those parts on skirt flare.
- The crest fin sits off-axis and looks broken from the front. Centre it or make it read deliberate.
