# E - Ruinplate (helm/dress/sleeves/hem)

**Most important fix: make the shape and colour bolder.** This is a pale-grey A-line robe with no strong waist, a flat small helm and almost no moss or bronze. From the ARPG camera it reads as a monochrome grey block. From behind, where it is all dark grey, it can pass for iron. Pull the waist in hard with a bronze girdle, widen the hem flare, and add moss to the hem and seams.

What works:
- The skirt is a clean continuous A-line to the floor that holds on the slim avatar with no gaps.
- The four-point bronze star on the chest is a nice ancient emblem, though it disappears from above.

Change:
- The sleeves are stacked stepped plates that flare out at the shoulder and elbow like pauldrons. This breaks the narrow-shoulder rule. Slim them to fitted vambraces.
- The helm is a flat cap with a tiny nub. Give it a crown or crest so it reads from the top-down camera. Right now the bald yellow face dominates.
- The back skirt is dark grey and plain. Carry the pale stone round.
- Spend the parts from the chest star detail on the silhouette (crest, hem flare) instead.
