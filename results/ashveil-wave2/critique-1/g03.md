# A: notes to the builder

1. **Lose the flat mortarboard headpiece.** From the ARPG camera the violet-rimmed square plate reads as a graduation cap, not a crown. Give the head real height: a thin charcoal circlet with two or three narrow upright tines, with violet only on the tips.
2. **The skirt reads as a pagoda.** It is stacked horizontal slabs of about equal width, with no bell. Make each tier clearly wider than the one above, flaring from the hip, so the bottom tier is the widest thing on the figure.
3. **Slim the sleeves.** The grey upper arms are still full block width and sit flush with the shoulder line. Narrow the upper sleeve and let any volume start at the forearm.
4. **Too much grey at mid-value.** Bodice, sleeves and skirt are all the same ash grey, so nothing leads the eye. Put charcoal at the hem as burnt edges and keep bone white for one accent.
5. The violet edge-lines are the right idea for places light can live. Gather them into a glowing hem seam and one gem rather than outlining every slab.
6. The fit avatar holds together, but the boxy tiers make the slim body look armoured.
