# D: notes to the builder

1. **Slim the sleeves.** The grey sleeves are full-width blocks that square off the shoulders from above, the main thing keeping this from reading as a lovely dress. Narrow the upper arm and flare a small cuff at the forearm.
2. **Push more violet in.** The violet hints at the hem and collar barely register. Make the hem edge a thin glowing violet seam and add one waist gem so the epic aura has somewhere to live.
3. **The skirt bell is the best of the run in side view.** Carry it to the front and back as well, so the hem visibly widens past the arms in the ARPG shot.
4. **The black spiked crown is good for height**, but it merges with the black hood and back panel. Tip the tines in bone white or violet so the crown reads against the dark.
5. The long coat-front with its dark centre panel edges toward robe or courtly coat. Pinch the waist tighter with a sash or seam so it reads bodice-and-skirt.
6. The fit avatar holds up well, and the parts look well spent on the silhouette.
