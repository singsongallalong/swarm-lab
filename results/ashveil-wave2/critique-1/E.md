# E: notes to the builder

1. **The skirt doesn't flare.** The bone-white vertical ribs hang straight down like a cage or a portcullis, and from above it reads as a column. Splay the ribs outward from the hip so they fan into a bell. That keeps the giants'-bones idea and makes it a gown.
2. **Slim the sleeves.** The dark sleeves are full shoulder-width blocks that read bulky and armoured.
3. **The bone-white spiked crown is great.** It's the strongest headpiece read from the ARPG camera, distinct and zone-true. Keep it and add a single violet gem at its base.
4. **The violet centre stripe on the bodice is a good glowing seam.** Echo it at the hem so the light frames the figure top and bottom.
5. The bodice and sleeves are near-black purple and sink in a night scene. Lift the bodice to ash grey, or char the edges, so the outline reads.
6. Too many thin ribs: fewer, bolder skirt panels will read better from above and cost fewer parts.
