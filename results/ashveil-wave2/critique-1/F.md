# F: notes to the builder

1. **Slim the sleeves.** The black sleeves are big full-width blocks, and with the black bodice they make a broad square top from above. This is the main fix. Narrow the upper arm and put a small cuff at the forearm.
2. **Widen the hem.** The pale lilac skirt is the right idea, a light skirt under a charred bodice, but it flares only modestly. Push it out so the hem is the widest thing on the figure.
3. **The dark-top, pale-skirt contrast is the clearest "dress" read in the run**, and the violet waist gem gives a good waist and a place for light. Keep both.
4. **The headpiece's yellow band is off-palette** and reads like a hat brim. Make it bone white or charcoal and let the spire end in a violet tip, or open it into a small crown.
5. Add a charred, darker edge at the hem with a thin violet glow, to tell the "half burnt, still glowing" story.
6. The X lacing reads as courtly. Consider a cracked violet seam down the bodice to set it apart from the courtly gowns.
