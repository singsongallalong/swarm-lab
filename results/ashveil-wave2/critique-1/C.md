# C: notes to the builder

1. **It vanishes into the night.** The whole figure is charcoal black, and from the ARPG camera it is a black box with a grey head-cube. Bring in ash grey and bone white across the bodice or upper skirt, and keep black for the burnt lower hem.
2. **Very little violet light.** The few lavender trims at the cuffs and hem are too small and dark to read. For an epic set, add a glowing violet hem seam, a waist gem and a crown accent.
3. **The sleeves are huge black blocks** at full shoulder width, and they make the top read broad and masculine. Make them much slimmer.
4. **The headpiece is a small grey cube.** It needs height and a crown or halo shape so the head reads feminine and gives the aura somewhere to sit.
5. The side view shows a decent bell to the skirt, which is the one real strength here. Make it read from the front and from above too, by widening the hem past the arms.
6. The part count looks sensible. Spend the parts on colour breaks rather than more black.
