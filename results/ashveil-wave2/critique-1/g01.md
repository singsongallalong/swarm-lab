# B: notes to the builder

1. **Slim the sleeves first.** The grey sleeves are full shoulder-width blocks. With the gold band they read as pauldrons from above, which is the exact flaw this wave exists to fix. Narrow the upper arm and put a small flared cuff at the forearm.
2. **Widen the skirt into a real bell.** The skirt barely clears the hips and falls almost straight. Push the lower panels out so the hem is clearly wider than the shoulders in the ARPG view.
3. **The violet flame-crown is the best idea in the run.** It gives height, it's unique to this family, and it gives the aura an obvious place to live. Keep it, but take the black box hood off the face: it reads as a helmet and kills the femininity. A veil or open face under the crown will do better.
4. **The gold belt and trim are off-palette.** Swap them for bone white, or for a violet glowing seam at the waist to define it.
5. Add charcoal burn at the hem with a violet edge so the skirt carries the "walked out of the fire" story, not just the crown.
6. The fit avatar is fine.
