# A - critique

1. **Sleeves are the worst in the run.** The black block sleeves are as wide as the shoulders and push out past them. From the ARPG camera they make a square black yoke that reads as plate armour, not a woman. Cut them to a thin upper arm and give the volume to a small flared cuff at the forearm.
2. **Remove the hip shelf.** The flat black bars that stick out sideways at the hip make the figure look like a robot pedestal. Let the bodice run into the skirt with no ledge.
3. **No real bell.** The skirt goes down in stacked grey slabs that barely widen, so the side view is nearly a column. Build three or four panels that start at the hip and lean out, and make the hem clearly wider than the hip.
4. **No EPIC moment.** The crown is three dark sticks, and the only violet is a thin line at the hem. Use a bone-white crown or halo with one violet gem, plus glowing violet seams up the skirt where it is burnt.
5. The burnt look is random black squares on grey. Char the hem upward in a ragged band instead.
6. The colour split (black top, grey skirt) works. Keep it, but lighten the bodice to ash so the figure doesn't disappear at night.
