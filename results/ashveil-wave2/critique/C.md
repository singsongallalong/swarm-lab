# C - critique

1. **The sleeves ruin the outline.** The huge black block sleeves stand wider than the shoulders and are the darkest mass on the figure, so from above the set reads as a broad black-shouldered knight. Make them slim, keep them close to the body, and give them ash-grey cuffs.
2. **Your skirt is the best bell in the run. Keep it.** The side view shows a true flare from the hip, the jagged charred petals fit the brief exactly, and the violet hem glow is right.
3. **Too dark for night.** Black sleeves, black skirt and a charcoal back will vanish in the Ashfields. Add an ash-grey or bone layer (an underskirt showing between the petals, or a pale bodice panel) so the gown has a light value from above.
4. The veil and helm reads as a closed helmet from the side and back. Open it into a crown band (the bone prongs are good) with a soft veil falling behind, and drop the boxy cap.
5. Add one or two Neon violet points high on the figure (the crown gem, the waist clasp) so the EPIC glow isn't only at the hem.
6. The waist is defined. Keep the belt, but make it thinner.
