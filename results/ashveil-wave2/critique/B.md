# B - critique

1. **Slim the sleeves.** The grey block sleeves are as wide as the shoulders and are the same tone as the bodice, so from above the torso reads as one broad box. Narrow the upper arm well inside the shoulder line, keep any volume at the forearm, and darken the sleeves to charcoal so the waist shows against them.
2. **Push the bell.** The stepped trapezoid skirt widens, but it is short and stiff. Make it longer and wider at the hem, with the top tier starting right at the hip and not under a waist ledge.
3. **The violet halo is the best light-carrier in the run. Keep it,** but make it a cleaner open ring or crown arc. Right now it reads as a cage or antenna. Make sure it reads from the top-down ARPG view, where it is currently just a squiggle.
4. The yellow-gold waist trim is off-palette. Make it bone white or violet.
5. The burnt patches are random dark squares. Group them into a charred hem band that fades upward, with a violet glow along the burn line.
6. The all-grey palette is safe at night but flat. Add one bone-white element (a collar or a hem edge) to add a value step.
