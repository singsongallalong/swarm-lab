# E - critique

1. **Slim and simplify the sleeves.** The grey block sleeves with banded cuffs are as wide as the shoulders and make the top read boxy. Narrow the upper arm. A slim forearm cuff is enough.
2. **The skirt splits into separate leg panels.** The bone-and-charcoal pleats fan out at the front, but they separate like ribs and show gaps, and from ARPG and the back the skirt is a dark tent. Close the pleats into one continuous bell and carry the stripes all the way around the back.
3. **The most distinctive family in the run.** The bone-white crown spikes, the violet chest seam and the rib-like pleats feel like the Ashfields' giants' bones. Keep this identity.
4. The crown reads beautifully from above. Add a single violet Neon gem at its front so the halo has a source of light.
5. The hem glow is only small violet flecks. Put a violet line along the pleat ends so the skirt edge reads as still-burning.
6. Define the waist. The black sash is good, but the bodice is the same width as the shoulder blocks. Taper it.
