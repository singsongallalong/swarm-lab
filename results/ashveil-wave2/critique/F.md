# F - critique

1. **Sleeves dominate.** The dark block sleeves are wider than the shoulders and are the biggest dark mass on the figure. From above, the set reads as a broad-shouldered monk. Slim them hard and keep them close to the body.
2. **The hood and helm hide the woman.** The dark cowl and tall spike make a priestly or nun head. Open the face, swap the hood for a crown or a halo arc, and keep the violet tip as its gem.
3. **The skirt tiers work.** The pale ash stepped tiers flare well and give the best light value in the run from the ARPG view. But they read as stairs, so add angled panels and char the lowest tier to charcoal with a violet edge (burnt from the hem up).
4. The bodice is plain dark grey with a thin violet thread. Give it a defined waist and a bone or violet seam.
5. The violet is barely there (one gem and a crown tip). An EPIC set needs two or three clear glowing parts.
6. The odd sticks at the hem corners look like broken parts. Remove them or turn them into deliberate charred fringe.
