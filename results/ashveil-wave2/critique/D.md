# D - critique

1. **Sleeves are still too broad.** The grey block sleeves sit at full shoulder width. Slimming them is the single change that would make this read as a woman from the ARPG camera. Pull them in and add a small flare at the wrist.
2. **Widen the hem more.** The A-line skirt with its pale front panel is the most gown-like shape here, but the flare is gentle and the side view is nearly straight. Lean the side and back panels out so the hem is clearly wider than the elbows.
3. **Lighting is well placed. Lean into it.** The violet hem line, waist gem, crown gem and V-neck chain are the right places for glow. Make the hem glow a ragged burnt edge, not a straight rule, and let one or two violet seams climb the skirt.
4. The black fan crown is good height, but it is dark on a dark head. Tip the fingers in bone white so it reads from above.
5. The back is a flat black slab panel. Echo the front's pale panel or carry the charred edge around.
6. Palette and family read well: ash, charcoal, bone and violet, and nothing like the reference sets.
