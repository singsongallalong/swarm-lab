# D: gold tassets with halo ring

**Most important fix: the skirt reads as armour.** The lower half is stacked rectangular gold plates splayed out at angles, like tassets or samurai kusazuri, over a black base. From the high camera it reads as a plated war skirt, not a feast gown. Rebuild it as one continuous bell with a hem trim.

- Shoulders: the sleeves are wine blocks against a gold torso. That contrast puts the eye on the widest point at ear level, the opposite of what the brief asks. Make the sleeves slim and the same colour as the bodice, or a darker gold, and put the wine lower down.
- Halo: the ring above the head is a good place for light and the most inventive idea in the set. Seen from above it reads as a halo or crown. Make it the main Neon part.
- The V on the chest reads as a letter, not a neckline. Replace it with a real neckline shape or a gem.
- Waist: there is a wine belt with a gem, but the bodice is a box and nothing narrows at the belt.
- Souring: the black base under the hem is the right idea, but it reads as a plinth. Let it creep up the skirt unevenly.
