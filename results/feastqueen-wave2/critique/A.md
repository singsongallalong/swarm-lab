# A: ivory-and-gold veiled feast queen

**Most important fix: slim the sleeves.** The ivory sleeves are full-width blocks stacked with two or three bands each, so from the ARPG camera the widest thing on the figure is the pale arm mass at ear level. Shrink them to near-limb width and drop the extra cuff layers. Then the gold bodice and the waist can carry the shape.

- Skirt: a real A-line flare with side panels, and it holds on the slim avatar. Push the hem out a little more at the back. From above, the skirt is hidden behind the shoulder blocks.
- Palette: ivory, beige and grey-taupe make up most of the figure. They read as pale and bridal, and they come close to linen in the field. It is not the night-muted wine and gold the brief asks for. Make the skirt wine or deep gold over an ivory underskirt and keep ivory as the accent.
- Souring: only a tiny dark patch at the front hem. Give the hem a shade-black creep (a ragged dark lower band rising at one side).
- Light: the candle-spike crown/veil is a good idea and the best light place here. Make two spikes Neon amber and add one gem at the bodice point.
- The gold bodice with its chain necklace and wine panel is the most elegant detail in the wave. Keep it.
- Parts: the back is a flat taupe slab, and the stacked sleeve bands use parts that should go into the hem.
