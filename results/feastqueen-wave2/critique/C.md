# C: wine court robe with gold hem basin

**Most important fix: the hem.** The gold ring at the hem is a rigid, open-topped basin standing apart from the body, so from the ARPG camera the figure looks like it is standing in a gold tub. Replace it with a skirt that widens from the hip, with the gold as a trim band on its edge.

- Silhouette: above the hem it is a straight column with the same width from shoulder to knee. There is no defined waist, so it reads as a cardinal's or king's robe, masculine. Narrow the waist with the gold belt and widen below it.
- Sleeves: full-width wine blocks with flared cuffs that stick out at the elbows. Slim them and lose the cuff flare.
- Crown: the open crown with tall tines is the best thing here. Seen from above, the wine cushion inside it makes a clear "royal" mark. Put one Neon gem at the front and keep the tines.
- Colour: the gold front stripe and the wine are good. The black dots at the cuffs and collar read as noise, not as souring. Use one deliberate shade-black creep instead.
- Fit: the slim avatar keeps the same column. The basin only works on the default body.
