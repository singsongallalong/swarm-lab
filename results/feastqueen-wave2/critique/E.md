# E: tiered gold robe

**Most important fix: there is no flare.** The skirt is three stacked rectangular tiers, each a step wider. From the ARPG camera it reads as a cake, a pedestal or a temple base, not a bell. Turn it into one widening skirt from the hip and keep a single wine hem band.

- Silhouette: the torso, sleeves and tiers are all the same gold with near-equal widths. The figure is a gold box from shoulder to floor, so there is no waist and nothing feminine in the outline.
- Sleeves: full-width gold blocks with wine cuffs that stick out sideways at hip level. Slim them and drop the cuff flare.
- The wrap neckline with its diagonal sash and ivory collar is a nice idea, but it reads as a kimono or monk robe. Fine on another set, but here it pulls away from "court gown".
- Crown: a small box crown on top. From above it is just a yellow square on a yellow square. Add height (tines, a halo or a candle) and one Neon gem.
- Colour: the gold is too saturated and too much for a night-muted world. Make the skirt wine and keep the gold for trim.
- Parts: many go into the tier stripes, which add nothing to the outline.
