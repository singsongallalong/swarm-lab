# F: sun-crowned gold gown with striped skirt panel

**Most important fix: break up the gold.** Head, torso, sleeves and back are all one saturated yellow-gold. From the ARPG camera, where the striped front panel is hidden, the set reads as a gold box with a gold head. That is too bright for the night world and has no wine weight. Make the bodice and sleeves deep wine or a darker gold and keep the bright gold for trim, crown and ruff.

- Sun-disc crown: the radiating crown with its central disc is the best light place in the wave. Make the disc and two rays Neon.
- The ruff collar is feminine and pretty and frames the head well. Keep it.
- Skirt: a real flare at the front with a lace hem, and the wine/ivory striped panel is festive. But the back and sides are plain gold and look straight from above. Carry the flare and the stripes all the way round.
- Sleeves: full-width gold blocks with panel inserts. Slim them so the waist and skirt carry the shape.
- Souring: none to be seen. Add shade-black creeping into one or two skirt stripes or up the hem.
- Fit: holds on the slim avatar. The sleeves still dominate.
