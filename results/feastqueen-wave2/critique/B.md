# B: wine bell gown with butterfly bodice

The strongest gown read of the wave. From every camera it reads as a red-and-gold dress. It needs width moved down and the sleeves slimmed.

**Most important fix: sleeves and shoulders.** The wine sleeves are full shoulder-width blocks the same colour as the bodice, so from the ARPG camera the top half is a broad red slab as wide as the skirt. Make them slim, perhaps darker wine with a gold cuff, so the gold bodice and waist stand out.

- Skirt: a good bell from the hip with a pale hem edge, and it holds on the slim avatar. From the ARPG camera the hem spreads into flat splayed bars that read as a platform or sledge. Close those gaps and round the hem so it reads as cloth.
- Bodice: the gold butterfly/heart panel with the waist gem is feminine and shows up from afar. Keep it.
- Crown: the candle-flame crown is right for the feast. Make the flame and the waist gem the Neon (two or three parts in all).
- Souring: the back and the underskirt go nearly black, which is the right idea, but the front hides it. Let shade-black creep up one side of the front hem.
- Palette: wine plus gold is rich and muted enough. It risks reading as studded at a distance, so the gold bodice must stay bright and the hem edge pale.
