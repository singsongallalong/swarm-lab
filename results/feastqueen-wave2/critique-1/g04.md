# A: ivory gown with wine shoulder blocks

**Fix first: the sleeves.** The two wine-red cubes at the shoulders are the widest, darkest and most solid shapes on the figure. From the ARPG camera they read as pauldrons, and the set looks like a broad ivory box with two red blocks on it. Cut them to slim ivory sleeves no wider than the arm. Put the wine on a narrow cuff or a sash instead.

**Second: it is not golden or epic.** Ivory and putty beige dominate. From above it sits too close to linen, and to the earlier pale gowns. Gold appears only as thin trim, a small necklace and a little crown. The brief asks for rich gold and candle amber. Make the bodice or an overskirt panel gold, and make the crown taller with one Neon gem so the head has height and a place for light.

**What works:** the skirt is the best part. It is a true A-line from the waist that widens to the hem, with gold hem edging and small splayed corner flares visible in the side view. The pendant necklace is a lovely, feminine touch.

**Souring:** there is none yet. Let shade-black creep up from the hem, or crack the ivory with a dark seam.

**Fit avatar:** it holds together, but the shoulder blocks still dominate.

**Parts:** spend fewer on the shoulder cubes and the stacked sleeve stripes, and more on the crown and the skirt.
