# B: crimson gown, gold bodice panel, tiara

**Fix first: the shoulders and sleeves.** The red sleeves are full-width slabs, ridged with gold lines. From the ARPG camera the upper body is a wide red rectangle, broadest at shoulder height. Slim the sleeves to the arm, and drop the trim lines that run across the shoulder tops. The waist should then become the narrowest point.

**Second: the bodice.** The gold plaque with a red window in it reads as a breastplate or a sign, not as a bodice. Shape it into a V or a corset point that runs down to the waist. That gives the feminine taper and a natural seam for glow.

**What works:** the colour. Rich wine and gold read epic, and clearly apart from the brown studded and iron sets. The tiara gives some head height. The skirt steps out from the hip to a flared, lighter hem, and that reads as a gown at all angles.

**Push:** the flare is modest and stepped. Widen the lowest tier further and let the hem go darker or ragged for the souring. The crown needs one or two Neon gems. At the moment no part glows.

**Fit avatar:** it holds well. The skirt keeps its shape and the column reads slimmer, which shows what the default body should aim for.

**Parts:** many are spent on parallel trim lines on the sleeves. Move them to the crown and the hem.
