# F: all-gold gown, sun crown, pleated skirt

**Fix first: the gold is too ochre and too uniform.** The whole set is a single mustard-brown tone. From the ARPG camera it reads as a muddy tan block, close to leather, rather than rich candlelit gold. Brighten the gold on the bodice and crown. Bring wine red in properly, for example on the skirt underlayer, the sleeves or the pleat gaps. Then add a little shade-black creeping up from the hem for the souring.

**Second: the sleeves.** They are block width at the shoulder, with big frilled cuffs that stick out at the elbow. From above, the shoulder line is the widest point. Slim the upper sleeve and keep the cuff small.

**What works:** this is the most gown-like skirt of the wave. Ivory pleat stripes and a ruffled hem flare from the hip into a real bell, and it holds on the slim avatar. The sun-ray crown with its central medallion gives height at the head and is a perfect place for light. The lace ruffles at the collar and cuffs and the ivory front panel read as feminine and courtly. It is the only unmistakably golden candidate.

**Push:** the black pendant at the hip reads as a stray block. Move it, or make it a gem. Two or three small Neon parts would sell it as epic: the medallion, a waist gem and a hem seam.

**Parts:** they are well spent on the skirt. Trim the cuff frills.
