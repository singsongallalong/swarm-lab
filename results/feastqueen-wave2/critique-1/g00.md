# C: wine coat, gold placket, spiked crown

**Fix first: there is no skirt.** From the waist down this is a straight column that ends in a gold box. The side view is a flat rectangle, so it reads as a long coat or priest's robe, not a feast gown. Give it a real bell that starts at the hip and reaches at least 1.5 times the hip width at the hem. Keep the gold hem band as the edge of that bell.

**Second: the sleeves.** They are block width at the shoulder and stacked with horizontal gold bars. From above, the torso is a broad red slab. Slim them.

**What works:** the crown is the best head in the wave. It is a tall, open gold crown with spires, height at the head rather than width at ear level, and it reads instantly from the ARPG camera. The gold centre placket gives a vertical line. The wine-and-gold palette is right, and there are small amber dots that hint at glow.

**Problems:** the black square on the chest reads as a missing texture or a hole, not as creeping shade. If it is meant as the souring, make it a crack or a stain that spreads from the hem or the cuffs.

**Fit avatar:** the gown stays a straight tube, with no waist and no flare.

**Parts:** they go on horizontal trim bars. Spend them on skirt panels that fan out.
