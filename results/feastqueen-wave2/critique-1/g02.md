# D: tiered wine-and-gold, halo ring

**Fix first: it reads as a stacked pagoda or an armoured box, not a gown.** Every level is a full-width horizontal block: shoulders, chest, waist and three hem tiers. From the ARPG camera it is a ziggurat of rectangles, wide at the top as well as the bottom, with no waist. Cut the upper half right back. Use slim sleeves, a narrow fitted bodice and one waist seam. Let only the skirt tiers step out, so the width sits low.

**Second: the dark base plinth.** The black-and-wine slab at the hem reads as a pedestal under the figure. Turn it into the outermost skirt flare, or make it the shade-black creeping in at the hem, but shaped like fabric.

**What works:** the octagonal halo ring above the head is the best invention in the wave. It is a crown and halo shape that the aura will love, and it gives height at the head. The wine-and-gold banding is rich and clearly epic, and it is distinct from every reference family.

**Problems:** the gold ripple sigil on the chest looks like a badge. Gold and wine alternate in equal stripes everywhere, so nothing leads the eye.

**Fit avatar:** it is slimmer and better, but it is still a stack of boxes.

**Parts:** too many go on horizontal bands. Halve them and move the budget to the skirt silhouette.
