# E: ivory bodice, wine sash, gold hoop-cage skirt

**Fix first: the skirt reads as scaffolding.** The gold hoop-and-strut cage is a clever idea: a pannier frame, a feast gown with its fabric gone. But from the ARPG camera it reads as a crate or a wire frame around the legs, not as a bell. Fill it. Hang ivory or wine panels between the hoops so the bell is solid, and keep a few gaps or bare struts at the back as the souring.

**Second: the torso and sleeves.** The ivory upper body is one wide block from shoulder to shoulder, with gold bars across it. It is the broadest shape on the figure and reads bulky from above. Slim the sleeves and narrow the bodice.

**Third: width at the hip.** The wine belt with its flared hip rim makes the widest point the hip. Lift the waist and narrow it, and let the width come at the hem instead.

**What works:** the diagonal wine sash is an elegant, courtly accent. The palette of ivory, wine and gold is on brief. The gold box crown with a candle-like spike reads as a feast lantern, which is a nice idea for a glow spot.

**Problems:** with ivory dominant it drifts toward the linen family from above, and it does not shout "epic". The crown is boxy.

**Fit avatar:** the cage holds its shape, but it is still see-through.

**Parts:** the cage struts cost a lot for a weak silhouette.
