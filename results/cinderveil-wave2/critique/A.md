# A - notes to the builder

1. **The gown sinks into the night.** Bodice, sleeves and skirt are all near-black, and from the ARPG camera it reads as one dark lump with a gold face. Put ash grey on the skirt's outer panels or the bodice front, and widen two or three of the red crack lines on the skirt so they show from above. At the moment they are hairlines.
2. **Sleeves and shoulders still read broad.** The sleeve blocks are full arm width, with horizontal red bands that make them look wider. Taper them to the wrist, drop the bands (or run one crack lengthwise), and keep the shoulder cap flush.
3. **Keep the skirt.** The bell from the hip is the best flare in the wave, and it holds on the slim avatar. Do not shrink it.
4. **Odd green waist line.** A thin green or olive line at the waist is off-palette. Make it sulphur yellow or remove it.
5. The helm's small crest is fine but reads dark too. A sulphur or magma tip would let it read from above.

Parts look well spent on the skirt. Move any sleeve-trim parts into skirt cracks instead.
