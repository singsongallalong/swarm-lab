# C - notes to the builder

1. **Bring the magma in.** This is the cleanest gown silhouette (charcoal bell skirt, red hem edge, sulphur sash), but the red cracks are thin slits that disappear from the ARPG camera. Run two or three bold jagged red fissures from the waist down the skirt, widening toward the hem as if it were splitting.
2. **Sleeves are still full arm-width blocks.** They are dark and plain, and from above they square off the shoulders. Taper them, or make them a slimmer inner sleeve with a narrow cuff.
3. **The bodice is a big flat dark block** with a grey plate at the chest. Add a narrow waist (an inset band, or a sash set lower and brighter) so the bell has a waist to flare from.
4. **Keep the ash-charcoal skirt tone.** It is the one gown that does not vanish into the night, and the flare holds on the slim avatar.
5. The helm's spiky dark crest is a good idea (it reads as smouldering) but is too thin. Give it one sulphur or magma tip.

Good parts economy. Spend the next few parts on cracks, not trim.
