# D - notes to the builder

1. **The skirt is a stepped stack of boxes**, a ziggurat or pedestal, not a flare. From every angle the figure looks like a statue standing on a plinth, and the pale grey ledges read as stone steps. Replace the tiers with angled, overlapping skirt panels that flare from the hip in one continuous bell.
2. **Not pretty yet.** Between the block bodice, the box tiers and the broad sleeves, it reads as heavy armour, closer to the iron family than to a gown. Narrow the waist and slim the sleeves.
3. **The ornate red chest clasp and red belt knot** are a nice idea but too small to read from above. Scale one of them up, or drop them.
4. **The helm with a red lid** reads as a box hat. Lower and soften it into a headpiece or circlet.
5. The red trim lines run along every tier edge, so the lava reads as outlining, not cracking. Make fewer, bolder, diagonal cracks.

The "decaying statue" idea has zone spirit. Keep it in a detail, not in the whole silhouette.
