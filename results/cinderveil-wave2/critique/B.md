# B - notes to the builder

1. **The skirt needs a real bell.** It is close to a straight A-line column, and the pale grey hem band reads as a plinth or pedestal under the figure, not a hem. Flare it outward from the hip (two or three widening tiers or angled panels) and make the hem band dark ash or magma rather than near-white.
2. **Sleeves are plain full-width black blocks.** From above they make the shoulders as wide as the skirt. Slim and taper them, and carry one orange crack down each forearm so they belong to the dress.
3. **The cracks are the best in the wave.** The bright jagged lava lines on black are exactly the zone, so keep them. The palette can drop the saturation slightly so it stays night-muted.
4. **The grey bodice panel and gold crown read like a knight's tabard and helmet.** Make the front panel narrower and V-shaped, and make the crown lower or more veil-like, more devotee than king.
5. At ARPG distance the figure reads as a black box with a yellow top. A grey or ash veil or collar would break up the mass.
