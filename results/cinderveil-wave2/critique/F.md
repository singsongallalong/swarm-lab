# F - notes to the builder

1. **A bright green block shows on the back of the head and the shoulders.** It is visible in the back, shoulder and ARPG views. It looks like a stray or mis-coloured part. Remove it or recolour it now; it ruins the read from the camera.
2. **The pleated skirt with red pleat lines is a good idea** (cooling-lava ribs), but it hangs mostly straight. Widen the pleat fan toward the hem so it reads as a bell, not a kilt.
3. **Sleeves are full-width dark blocks**, and the shoulders square off from above. Taper and slim them.
4. **All dark again.** Make the bodice, or every other pleat, ash grey so the figure does not vanish.
5. The helm's dark spike is fine. Consider a sulphur tip.

Many thin pleat parts. Merge them into fewer, wider panels and spend the saved parts on flare.
