# E - notes to the builder

1. **The shoulders are the broad, bulky flaw the brief warned about.** Big grey shoulder and upper-arm blocks stand out past the torso, and from the ARPG camera they dominate. Remove the shoulder masses and slim the sleeves to close-fitting tapered tubes.
2. **There is no flare.** The skirt drops straight, as a stack of grey and black bands. Give it a bell from the hip.
3. **The palette is the best idea here.** Ash grey over black, with a magma-red front panel and a sulphur hem edge, is visible at night and is clearly this zone. Keep the red panel and the gold hem line.
4. **The hood or helm is a tall grey block** that reads as a helmet, and from above it merges with the shoulders. Make it a lighter veil or a small crown.
5. Overall it currently reads as armour (close to the iron or studded families), not a dress. Fix 1 and 2 before anything else.
