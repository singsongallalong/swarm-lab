# F: critique

**Most important fix: close the skirt and flare it.** The front reads as open panels with the legs showing between them, which makes it a long coat or tabard over trousers, not a gown. Join the panels into a continuous bell that flares from the hip.

- **Sleeves:** full shoulder-width blocks with a ridged collar band, so the top-down outline is a broad dark rectangle. Slim them clearly.
- **Visibility:** it is nearly all black with fine red outlines. From the ARPG view only the small grey helm top reads. Add an ash-grey or sulphur sash at the waist, and put magma red on mass areas, such as a glowing underskirt seen between the panels. That would turn the gaps into a feature.
- **Helm:** a small spike on a box. Refine it into a slender crest or circlet.
- **Parts:** spending is reasonable, but none of it buys silhouette. Move parts from trim outlines into the skirt flare.
