# A: critique

**Most important fix: replace the stair-step skirt with a bell.** Seen from the ARPG camera, the lower half is a stack of flat slabs stepping outward. The grey bottom tiers make it read as a temple plinth or a wedding cake, not a gown. Use 2-3 tall, angled panels that flare from the hip, and keep the stepping, if any, to a single hem band.

- **Sleeves:** the arm blocks are as wide as the shoulders and sit flush with the bodice, so the top-down outline is a broad rectangle. Narrow them clearly, and put a small cuff flare at the wrist instead.
- **Palette:** the basalt bodice is right. The ash-grey tiers are the only light value, and they sit at the floor where the camera cares least. Move some ash or sulphur up to the waist or neckline so the figure keeps a readable top in the dark.
- **Magma red:** at present it is hairline trims that disappear at distance. One bold red seam running down the front of the skirt would carry the "cracks running through it" idea.
- **Helm:** the crown spikes are fine and small. Keep them.
- **Parts:** many thin tier slabs add little to the silhouette. Merge them.
