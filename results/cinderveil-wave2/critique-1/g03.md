# D: critique

**Most important fix: soften the lampshade.** The skirt is a wide pyramid of many even tiers, each edged in red. It reads strongly from above, the most distinctive candidate at distance, but it reads as a pagoda or lampshade, not a pretty gown. Cut the tiers to 3-4, vary their depth (the deepest at the hem), and let the red show at uneven gaps like cracks rather than as regular stripes.

- **Sleeves:** full-width blocks again, so the top-down outline is broad and square at the shoulders. Slim them.
- **Palette:** it is the most on-zone of the wave, with basalt and magma clearly present. Add a touch of ash grey for value contrast, and a sulphur detail on the red crown so it reads as the devotees' work.
- **Bodice:** the grey T-shaped placket is good. Make it shapelier, perhaps a V or sweetheart, for femininity.
- **Parts:** the many tier slabs plus their trim strips are probably near the budget. Fewer, bolder tiers would read better and free parts for the sleeves and neck.
- **Helm:** the red crown circlet works. Keep it.
