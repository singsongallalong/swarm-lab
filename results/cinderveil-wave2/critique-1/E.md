# E: critique

**Most important fix: there is no flare.** The skirt is a straight black column down to the floor, and from the side it is a slim plank. Give it a real bell or A-line from the hip. This is the brief's core ask.

- **Visibility:** it is the darkest candidate. In the top-down view it is a black blob with a gold face, and it will vanish at night. Add ash-grey and magma masses, not just hairline trims.
- **Sleeves:** wide black blocks with lapel-like shoulder pieces, so it reads as a coat or a priest's cassock rather than a gown. Slim the sleeves and drop the shoulder bulk.
- **What works:** the thin red vertical lines down the front and the sulphur-gold hem trim are the right colour idea. Scale them up into a few bold seams and a hem band that can be seen from above.
- **Helm:** the gold face-frame is a striking idea, but it reads as a mask. Either make it a light circlet or veil, or commit to it as a devotee's mask with a crest.
