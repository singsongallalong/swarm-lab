# B: critique

**Most important fix: this is not basalt.** The whole set is pale ash grey. From above it reads as a stone or linen robe, close to the classic and linen families, rather than a dark mountain gown. Make basalt black the main mass and keep ash grey as an accent: a stole, the bodice front, or the hem steps.

- **Skirt:** a stepped ziggurat with even, regular steps. The flare is there, but it looks like stairs, not cloth. Use fewer, taller panels that angle out from the hip.
- **Sleeves:** broad blocks flush with the shoulder, the bulky top-down read the brief warns against. Slim them clearly.
- **Magma red:** only scratched hairline cracks that vanish at camera distance. Make 1-2 cracks thick and glowing. Paired with dark basalt, that is the zone's signature.
- **What works:** it is the only candidate that will never vanish into the night. If you darken it, keep some lighter value near the top (collar or shoulders) for that reason.
- **Helm:** the spire and gold band read well. Keep them.
